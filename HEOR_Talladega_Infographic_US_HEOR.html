THE RACE BRIEF

Market access is a four-lap circuit. Clinical data gets you to FDA review; Health Economics & Outcomes Research helps payers, P&T committees, IDNs, and other decision-makers understand clinical value, budget impact, and real-world fit.

THE RACING LINE / FOUR LAPS TO US ACCESS VALUE
LAP
01
POLE POSITION
LEAD WITH DECISION-GRADE EVIDENCE.
Map the value story before Phase III locks, ideally before Phase 2 if possible. Align endpoints, comparators, subgroups, and PROs with the questions US payers will actually ask.
LAP TIMET-MINUS 36 MO.
LAP
02
THE PIT STOP
MAP US STAKEHOLDERS EARLY.
Understand key stakeholders (e.g., Commercial plans, Medicare plans, PBMs). Begin building the evidence package and pre-approval exchange plan.
LAP TIMET-MINUS 18 MO.
LAP
03
PRE-LAUNCH BUILD
LOCK THE ACCESS TOOLKIT.
Burden-of-illness pubs, AMCP dossier, budget impact model, CEA if applicable, value-story deck, and field tools ready before launch.
LAP TIMET-MINUS 12 MO.
LAP
04
CHECKERED FLAG
SECURE ACCESS FOR PATIENTS.
Deliver the payer package: clinical evidence, target patient population, budget impact, and differentiated value story.
LAP TIMELAUNCH + 90 D.

THE LEADERBOARD WHO FINISHES WHERE

P1
The team that built HEOR into the evidence plan early. Payers can evaluate. Access teams can negotiate. Patients have a clearer path.
P2
The team that waited until pivotal trials were locked. Strong finish, but spent the race retrofitting the payer story.
DNF
The team that assumed approval alone would secure access. Did not finish. Last place. Answered the wrong questions.
