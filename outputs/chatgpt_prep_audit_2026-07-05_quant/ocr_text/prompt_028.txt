1) Core trial evidence and populations studied
Cabotegravir(CAB-LA)
Evidence comes from two large randomized controlled trials:
·HPTN 083: cisgender men who have sex with men (MSM) and transgender women ) Ncel
·HPTN 084: cisgender women in sub-Saharan Africa > NcBI
Design note: Both compared CAB-LA to daily oral TDF/FTC, not placebo.
Main results (by trial population)
●HPTN 083 (MSM/trans women): ~66% reduction vs TDF/FTC (HR ~0.34) O> NcBI
●HPTN 084 (cis women): ~88% reduction vs TDF/FTC (HR ~0.12) > NcBli
Keyinterpretation constraint:
Differences between trials areheavily influenced by background incidence and oral PrEP adherence in the
comparator arm, not necessarily biological differences in CAB-LA efficacy.
