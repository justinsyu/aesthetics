The key point is that “less frequent dosing" is only one dimension of PrEP choice. In practice, patients and
clinicians weigh drug class differences,side-effect profiles,administration logistics,and clinical

1) Different drug class(important if someone hasintolerance or concern)
·Apretude is a cabotegravir (integrase inhibitor) long-acting formulation.
·Yeztugo is lenacapavir (capsid inhibitor).
Because they are different mechanisms, someone who:
·had intolerance, allergic reaction,orpoor experience with one class

2) Injection site differences and tolerability
·Yeztugo is subcutaneous (under the skin) and forms a drug “depot” with visible nodules. Freddie
Why this matters:
·Some patients prefer IM injections (no persistent lump under skin)
·Others prefer subcutaneous injections (often smaller needle,different pain profile)

·Yeztugo commonly causes palpable nodules at injection sites
