What Yeztugo actually is
Yeztugo (lenacapavir) is a long-acting injectable PrEP medication approved in the U.S. in 2025 for HIV
prevention,given about every 6 months after an initial loading regimen. Drug.com
It's part of a newer class of HIV capsid inhibitors and is designed to reduce HIV risk in people who are HIV-
negative but at risk. Drug.com
Clinical trials showed extremely high efficacy (about 99.9% protection / very few infections in treated
groups), which is why it's considered a major breakthrough. clad scienes .
Whyit doesNOTreplace otherPrEPoptions
Even thoughYeztugo is highly effective,it doesn't replace existingPrEPmethods for several reasons:
1. It is not the only approved PrEP
·Daily oral PrEP (e.g., tenofovir/emtricitabine tablets)

Yeztugo is simply one more option,not a replacement for the rest.
2.Different peoplebenefitfrom differentregimens
Medical guidelines and public health recommendations emphasize choice, because:
·Some people prefer daily pills (control,flexibility, low clinic dependence)
·Some prefer injections every 2months
·Some may prefer every 6 months
·Some may not be eligible for certain drugs due to kidney function, drug interactions, or other medical
factors
So clinicians don't switch everyone to Yeztugo.
