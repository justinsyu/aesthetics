2.Todetectacute(early)HiVinfectionthatstandard antibodytestsmaymiss
A key reason baseline testing is more intensive for injectable PrEP is the risk of very early HIV infection,
when:
·Antibody tests may still be negative
·Viral RNA is already detectable
·Symptoms may be mild or absent
Because of this, guidelines for injectable PrEP recommend HIV-1 RNA testing in addition to
antigen/antibody testing at baseline, specifically to rule out acute infection. Nationa..
This is especially emphasized for cabotegravir and lenacapavir because long-acting agents can suppress viral
replication without curing infection, making early HIV harder to detect later.
