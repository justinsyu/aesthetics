Yeztugo (lenacapavir) — prescribing information considerations
Indication
·Indicated for pre-exposure prophylaxis(PrEP) to reduce risk of sexually acquired HIV-1 in adults and
adolescents ≥35 kg who are HIV-negative and at risk. vezTugo HCP
·Must confirm HIV-1negative status before initiation. Drug.com
Boxed warning (key risk)
·Risk of HIV-1 drug resistance if used in undiagnosed HIV infection.
·Requires HIV testing before initiation and before each scheduled injection,with sensitivity to detect
acute infection. Drugs.com
Dosing and administration
·Missed or delayed dosing has specific management steps, including oral bridging dosing in certain
circumstances. Drugs.com
·Administration is subcutaneous (two injections per dose). Drugs.com
