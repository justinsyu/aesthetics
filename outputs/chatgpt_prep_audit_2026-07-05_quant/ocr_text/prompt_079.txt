1. Drug class and mechanism (per labeling + pharmacology literature)
Yeztugo (lenacapavir)
·Class: HIV-1 capsid inhibitor
·Mechanism: Binds HIV capsid protein and disrupts multiple replication stages (nuclear import, assembly,
and capsid formation) Drugs.com
·Key feature:First PrEP agent targeting the viral capsid (not integrase or reverse transcriptase)
Apretude(cabotegravir)
·Class: HIV-1 integrase strand transfer inhibitor (INSTI)
·Mechanism: Blocks integration of viral DNA into host genome
·Supported by FDA labeling and PrEP trial literature (HPTN 083/084)> NcBiI
Key distinction:
·Lenacapavir = multi-stage capsid disruption
·Cabotegravir = single enzymatic step (integration)
