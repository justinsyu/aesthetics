How effective are they?
·Both are highly effective when taken on schedule.
·Yeztugo's clinical trials showed very high protection rates(near-complete prevention in study
populations), leading to FDA approval in 2025.  Drug.com
·Apretude has also shown strong effectiveness compared with daily oral PrEP, and in trials was more
effective than daily pills in some populations due to better adherence. gskus
Key takeaway: Real-world effectiveness depends heavily on staying on schedule—missed injections reduce
protection.
