1)Theyarebothhighlyeffectivebutnotinterchangeable"upgrades"
·Yeztugo (lenacapavir) is a twice-yearly injectable PrEP option that has shown very high efficacy in
clinical trials and was recently approved for HiV prevention e cicanco. +1.
·Apretude (cabotegravir) is a long-acting injectable every 2 months and is already established in
guidelines and clinical use  Drugs.com .
·Both are part of the expanding set of PrEP options alongside daily oral FTC/TDF or FTC/TAF.
Importantly, there are nohead-to-head clinical trials showing superiority of Yeztugo over Apretude in real-
world populations. Differences in dosing interval do not automatically translate into better outcomes.
2)Evidencestrengthdiffers(maturityofdatamatters)
·Apretude has longer post-approval clinical experience and implementation data (since 2021),
·Yeztugo is newly approved, with strong trial efficacy but limited real-world effectiveness, resistance,
and implementation data so farReuters.
So while Yeztugo is promising, its evidence base is earlier-stage in population-level deployment.
