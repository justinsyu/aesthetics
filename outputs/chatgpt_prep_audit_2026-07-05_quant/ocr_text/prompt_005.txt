Core difference:dosing frequency and adherence
Daily oral PrEP
·Taken as a pill every day (most commonly tenofovir/emtricitabine-based regimens).
·Protection dependsheavily on consistent daily adherence.
·When taken correctly, reduces HIV risk by ~99% in sexual transmission settings. Fredie
Apretude(cabotegravirinjection)
·Injection every 2 months after initial loading doses.
·Given as an intramuscular injection in the buttock.Fredie
·Designed to reduce reliance on daily pill-taking while still requiring regular clinic visits.
Yeztugo (lenacapavir injection)
·Injection every 6 months after an initial “loading” phase (includes short-term oral dosing + injections).

·Given as a subcutaneous injection (under the skin), not into muscle.Freddie
