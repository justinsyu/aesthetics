1.What"breakthroughinfection"meansinthesestudies
In both programs, a breakthrough infection is an HIV infection occurring while the participant is assigned to
PrEP (i.e., during drug exposure in trials). These are rare events in both drugs' phase 3 programs, but they are
measured differently:
·Yeztugo: very low event counts in placebo-like comparator arms (FTC/TDF)
·Apretude:event counts compared against daily oralPrEP(FTC/TDF)
This difference in trial design is central to interpreting“breakthroughs."
2. Yeztugo (lenacapavir): what is known
What weknow
In the two pivotal Phase 3 trials (PURPOSE 1 and 2), lenacapavir showed zero breakthrough infections in the
active arm at theprimary interim analysis:
·0 HIV infections / 100 person-years in the Yeztugo arm in PURPOSE 1 interim analysis  vezTuGo HCp
·Compared with 1.69 infections / 100 person-years in the FTC/TDF control arm Yezruco Hcp
Across the program:
·No confirmed HIV infections in the lenacapavir arms at the time of primary reporting (as reflected in
FDA briefing and trial summaries)
·Efficacy interpreted as near-complete prevention during observed follow-up
