Big picture
Both are highly effective HIV prevention shots for people who are HIV-negative.The main differences are:
·how often you get them
·how they work in the body
·how new they are
Both are FDA-approved injectable PrEP options. HIV.g.
1. How often you get injections
·Apretude(cabotegravir)
·Every 2 months (6 times per year) after starting
·Given by a healthcare provider as a muscle injection
Yeztugo (lenacapavir)
·Every 6 months (2 times per year) after starting
·Given as a subcutaneous (under-the-skin) injection
So in simple terms:
Apretude = more frequent shots
Yeztugo = twice-a-year shots (less often)
