Core workflow

The clearest startup wedge is claim-level evidence automation before formal review

1. Intake

Classify the asset

vMLR frames smart intake around material type, brand detection, routing and readiness scoring before AI review starts.9

2. Extract

Find every claim

Revisto, ERMA, Copli, VeroMLR and Veritas.ai all foreground automated claim detection or extraction.17

3. Match

Attach evidence

Vendors compete on source matching, citation quality, tables/charts extraction, approved labels and reference anchors.10

4. Check

Apply code and SOPs

Siguru, VeroMLR, AITHENA and SecureCHEK emphasize code, guideline, policy or brand-standard checks before final committee review.46

5. Decide

Route to humans

Better-positioned tools preserve reviewer judgment, audit trails and export into systems such as Veeva or Aprimo.318

Practical implication

The strongest startup narratives are not "AI approves content." They are "AI gets the asset review-ready, with claim-level provenance and fewer avoidable reviewer cycles."

Direct MLR leaders

Revisto and Copli are the most visible US-style direct MLR startup competitors

Revisto

Purpose-built MLR and claims platform

Revisto positions around life-sciences marketing compliance, AI MLR Engine, claims generation, claims extraction, claims library creation, creative/content development and embedded workflow options.1

Funding disclosure gives it unusual startup proof: a November 2024 $4M seed round led by LiveOak with Eli Lilly participation, bringing total funding to $6M.2

Copli

Veeva-centered AI MLR reviewer

Copli says AI agents provide feedback in minutes, every claim links to its source, and the platform pushes into Veeva Vault PromoMats. Its public positioning spans marketing teams and MLR/compliance reviewers.3

Reported public proof points include 80% faster time to market, 98% accuracy on critical issues and official Veeva AI Partner and Publicis Partner positioning.3

Shared buyer promise

Fewer cycles, fewer unsupported claims, faster launch assets

Shared risk

Public claims need validation in a live brand, SOP and content-stack environment.

Primary diligence

Veeva depth, citation accuracy, rejected-claim handling and audit trail evidence

Diligence sequence

Screen vendors by evidence traceability first, then integration and commercial maturity

1. Traceability

Can every flag be defended?

Require claim text, source document, page/section/paragraph, evidence passage, confidence, rationale and reviewer override history.

2. Code and SOP fit

Does it know the market?

Test ABPI, FDA/OPDP, local rules, brand standards and internal SOP exceptions with materials that intentionally test edge cases.

3. Content boundary

What happens when evidence is absent?

Look for conservative no-answer behavior, unsupported-claim escalation and strict approved-content constraints.

4. Workflow ownership

Where does it sit?

Clarify whether the vendor replaces precheck, claims management, MLR annotations, content generation, agency handoffs or only a narrow assistant.

5. System integration

Does it reduce work in Veeva?

Validate import/export, document relationships, metadata mapping, lifecycle triggers, reviewer comments and audit trail preservation.

6. Enterprise readiness

Can pharma deploy it?

Check validation package, security posture, tenant isolation, customer references, implementation load and proof under real brand data.
