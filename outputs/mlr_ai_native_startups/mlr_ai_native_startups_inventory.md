## Market patterns

1. The core wedge is shifting upstream from workflow routing to claim-level evidence automation: claim extraction, source matching, table verification and approved-claim libraries are the most common differentiators.
2. MLR-focused startups increasingly frame AI as a pre-review or reviewer-assist layer, not autonomous final approval. Human sign-off remains explicit in the stronger compliance narratives.
3. UK/ABPI-specific tools are emerging as a distinct submarket, led by Siguru AI, VeroMLR and AITHENA, while US-oriented vendors emphasize FDA/OPDP, Form 2253 and Veeva PromoMats.
4. Veeva integration is now a major proof point. Copli, Revisto, vMLR, Shaman, Hyntelo, ACTO and incumbent benchmarks all position around Veeva data or workflow compatibility.
5. Broader AI-native pharma content platforms such as Solstice and Yama are turning MLR from a review bottleneck into an embedded constraint during content generation.
6. Public evidence is uneven: several vendors provide detailed feature claims but limited named customer, funding, validation or production-deployment data.
7. The buyer comparison set will not stay startup-only. Veeva, EVERSANA, Indegene, ZS and agency networks are likely to set procurement expectations for security, integration, validation and managed services.
