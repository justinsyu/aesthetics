03 / 09
Day plan

Sunday foundation

Use Sunday to choose a hands-on skill base. The morning has two overlapping options; the afternoon extends the applied GenAI course into architectures.

8:00 AM - 12:00 PMShort Course

Applied GenAI for HEOR: intro

Separate registration required. The rapid advancement in generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) presents an opportunity for transformative potential in the field of health economics and outcomes research (HEOR).

8:00 AM - 12:00 PMShort Course

Prompt engineering for HEOR

Separate registration required. Prompt engineering—the art and science of designing effective inputs for generative AI—has become a critical skill for health economists and outcomes researchers.

1:00 PM - 5:00 PMShort Course

Applied GenAI: advanced architectures

Separate registration required. Generative AI (GenAI) is rapidly transforming how health economics and outcomes research (HEOR) is conducted from literature reviews and evidence synthesis to economic modeling and HTA submissions.

If you are new to applied GenAI

Take the introductory HEOR course, then continue into advanced architectures after lunch.

If you already use chat tools

Prompt engineering is the practical morning alternative, especially for HEOR communication and workflow design.

Pre-conference setup

Bring examples from SLR, model, or evidence-generation work so the short courses map to your own use cases.
