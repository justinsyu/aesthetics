Reference Managers for Systematic Review Preparation

Covers citation collection, PDF management, duplicate removal, and library export. Excludes screening, extraction, appraisal, and review-automation software.

Included Scope

Citation managers used before screening across free, paid, and institutional access models.

Excluded Scope

Review conduct software, even when it imports, deduplicates, or manages citation records.

Selection Variables

  • Access model, including free, paid, subscription, or institution-provided team access.
  • Storage model, including local files, cloud synchronization, and multi-user team access.
  • Export format compatibility for screening tools, bibliography files, and later analysis.

Boundaries

  • This is not a comparison of screening, extraction, risk-of-bias, or AI review automation.
  • The examples show documented use, not endorsement, ranking, or comparative effectiveness.
  • The bars summarize citation-library preparation functions before formal review conduct.
Linked citations: 1-10 official tool pages; 11-20 PubMed/PMC examples. Bars summarize citation import, duplicate handling, export portability, access model, and review preparation.
