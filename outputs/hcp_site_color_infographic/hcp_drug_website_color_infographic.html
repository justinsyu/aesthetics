Drug websites speak in blue, gray, and one sharp accent

A color audit of HCP and branded drug websites shows a conservative design grammar: clinical blues, neutral scaffolding, and warm colors used mainly to create calls to action or brand distinction.

What the color market is doing

1

Blue is the category anchor, appearing on 494 of 681 URLs and far ahead of any single expressive hue family.

2

The second color is where brands differentiate. Teal/aqua is the most common companion, followed by purple/pink and warm accent colors.

3

Neutral systems matter because HCP websites carry dense prescribing, safety, dosing, and access content that needs structure before personality.
