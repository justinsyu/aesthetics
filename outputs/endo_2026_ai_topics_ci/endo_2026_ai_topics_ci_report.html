Executive summary

  • The largest cluster was imaging AI and computer vision, including retinal fundus deep-learning models and thyroid ultrasound/molecular/histology AI applications.345
  • Diabetes technology content retained CGM-linked machine-learning markers and artificial-pancreas control systems, while AID-only records without AI/ML/control-system language were excluded.1A2

Inclusive terms

AI, ML, algorithms, automation

Screening terms included artificial intelligence, AI, machine learning, ML, deep learning, neural network, algorithms, automated, prediction models, radiomics, LLMs, chatbots, and related phrases.B

False positives

Clinical abbreviations removed

Records were excluded when AI meant adrenal insufficiency, aromatase inhibitor, or assay index, when ML appeared only in names/credentials, or when AI appeared only in an umbrella session title.B

Interpretation boundary

Included records are AI-related conference topics identified from the record text. The report does not infer clinical validity beyond the abstract, poster, session, and presentation metadata.AB

03 / 09
Diabetes technology

Glucose-management AI content focused on CGM ML markers and control logic

CGM + ML

Progression tracking

The SY08 symposium description links CGM, self-adapting AI algorithms in insulin delivery, and CGM-based dynamic markers with machine learning for diabetes progression tracking.1A

AID boundary

Automation alone excluded

Automated insulin-delivery records were excluded when they described devices or real-world outcomes without explicit AI, ML, neural-network, or AI-control-system language.B

Artificial pancreas

Control systems

One diabetes poster states that insulin-delivery results can be optimized by integrating artificial-intelligence-based control systems with artificial-pancreas technology.2

Boundary note

Automated insulin delivery alone is not counted as an AI topic. AID and glucose-control records are retained only when the record also states AI, ML, or AI-based control-system content.B

04 / 09
Imaging AI

Computer-vision topics appeared across retina, thyroid, histology, and quantitative imaging

Retina

EfficientNet and ResNet

Diabetic-retinopathy records described EfficientNetB0/B1/B3 and ResNet152 neural-network approaches using fundus imaging for detection, severity staging, and differential diagnosis.3AA

Thyroid

Ultrasound and histology

Thyroid records included AIBx ultrasound software, an AI-driven multimodal thyroid tumor score, and a systematic review of predicting molecular alterations from routine histology.4A5

Quantitative imaging

Deep learning and DXA

Adjacent imaging records included automated deep-learning-derived prostate volume from MRI and kNN-assisted bone pore-space measurement from spinal DXA images.AA

Caveat

The retained data separates records that report AI/ML or computer-vision methods from records that only mention radiomics or advanced imaging as a potential future modality.A

05 / 09
Prediction and phenotyping

ML prediction and phenotyping topics were retained only when AI/ML methods were explicit

Metabolic disease

Diabetes and PCOS

Records described external validation of a diabetes prediction machine-learning model, unsupervised machine-learning PCOS subtypes, and a frugal AI PCOS risk score.A6A

Diagnostic modeling

SVM and routine biomarkers

One retained biochemical-marker record used support-vector-machine diagnostic models to distinguish primary hyperparathyroidism from IGF-I-induced metabolic changes in acromegaly.7

Biomarker panels

Random forest

One type 1 diabetes biomarker record used a machine-learning random forest model to identify a multivariate protein panel for autoantibody prediction.A

06 / 09
Generative AI and implementation

LLMs appeared in education, patient education tool-building, and evidence-based diabetes decision support

Teaching

LLM-assisted learning design

An education session states that large language models would be used to design learning pathways and case studies in a hands-on teaching workshop.8

Patient education

Clinician-built tools

A diabetes poster asks whether clinicians can build acceptable AI tools for diabetes education and matches the chatbot/LLM term set.A

Clinical decision support

RAG for type 2 diabetes

Evidence Rx is described as evidence-based clinical decision support in type 2 diabetes using a retrieval-augmented generation system.9

AI implementation and disclosure

Conference programming also included AI implementation sessions. Records that disclosed ChatGPT or AI-assisted tools only for language or grammar editing are excluded from the retained AI-topic count.10AB
