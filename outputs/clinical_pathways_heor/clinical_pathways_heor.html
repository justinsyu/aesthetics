HEOR - clinical pathway access - evidence key

Coverage opens one gate. HEOR helps unlock the pathway.

Payer coverage is necessary, but not sufficient. Pathway committees, IDNs, clinicians, and patients still need decision-grade evidence that explains value, budget impact, workflow fit, and real-world follow-through.

The access gap
Covered does not always mean used.

Clinical pathway adoption depends on whether the product can be positioned, afforded, implemented, and defended against the actual standard of care.

HEOR is the evidence key.

It connects clinical benefit to the access questions that decide routine use: which patients, compared with what, at what budget impact, with what burden, and with what real-world follow-up.
