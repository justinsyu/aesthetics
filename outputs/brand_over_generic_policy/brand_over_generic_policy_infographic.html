When a pharmacy policy favors
the brand over the generic

Examples of how formularies, prior authorization rules, and billing edits can steer dispensing toward a branded drug when rebates, access policy, or plan design make the brand the preferred product.

5

Policy mechanisms that can reverse the usual generic-first pathway

Rebate list

MassHealth explicitly says its Brand Name Preferred Over Generic list is based on lower net cost after rebates and generally requires a trial or rationale before using the non-preferred generic equivalent1

Generic PA

Montana Medicaid states that when preferred brands appear on the PDL, the generic equivalent requires prior authorization; the current May 2026 PDL shows Butrans Patch preferred while buprenorphine (Butrans) is non-preferred26

Adherence edit

Ohio Medicaid's April 1, 2026 UPDL defines BvG as brand preferred over the generic and marks current BvG products across multiple therapeutic categories3

Commercial PA

Mass General Brigham Health Plan has a commercial policy for brand preferred over generic drugs, requiring documentation such as trial, adverse reaction, or shortage rationale for the non-preferred generic4

MassHealth lists many named brands as preferred

Current BOGL examples include respiratory, CNS, diabetes, anticoagulant, and ophthalmic products.

North Carolina places some
brands above direct generics

The April 2026 PDL shows Butrans Patch as preferred while buprenorphine patch, its generic, is non-preferred; it also lists OxyContin Tablet as preferred while generic oxycodone ER is non-preferred5

Butrans OxyContin

Montana ties brand preference to state net cost

The manual says brands may be preferred over generics when there is a net cost benefit to the department, and the May 2026 PDL shows Butrans Patch preferred over generic buprenorphine (Butrans)26

Butrans

Ohio embeds the concept in UPDL terminology

Ohio's April 2026 UPDL defines BvG as brand preferred over generic and labels examples such as Mitigare, Vyvanse, Lantus, Farxiga, Alrex, and Lotemax as BvG3
