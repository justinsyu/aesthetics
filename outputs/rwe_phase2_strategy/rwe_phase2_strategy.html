Early strategy

What changes when RWE starts in Phase 2
HEOR is no longer asked to patch a launch story after the critical evidence choices have already been made.

Trial design gets sharper

Access-relevant outcomes and subgroup definitions are discussed while there is still room to influence evidence collection.

RWE studies get more purposeful

Real-world analyses are tied to specific payer questions, not only to publication activity.

Launch teams get fewer surprises

The payer evidence story has already been stress-tested before the first access objection arrives.

Core idea: evidence strategy should begin when the questions are clear, not when the deadline is close.
