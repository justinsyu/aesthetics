HEOR operating map

HEOR project types across the product life cycle

Vendor service pages converge on a simple operating model: define value and pre-launch RWE needs early, build integrated evidence before launch, translate it for HTA/payers at launch, and refresh it with observed RWE after uptake.

Lifecycle logic
Define value early
Build payer-relevant evidence
Translate for HTA and access
Refresh with RWE after uptake

Assemble the evidence package and payer story
  • Core value story and product value proposition1,11,12
  • Cost-effectiveness and budget impact models2,4
  • SLR, NMA, MAIC, ITC, and meta-analysis workstream1,4,12

Convert evidence into access decisions
  • HTA and reimbursement submissions6,13,14
  • Global-to-local model adaptations1,9,14
  • Payer tools, field materials, and objection handlers1,9
  • Appraisal, hearing, and post-submission support1,12
  • Publication and affiliate / field training7,9
Refresh value with observed use and outcomes
  • Observed uptake: database, chart review, and survey studies3,14
  • Treatment patterns, HCRU, adherence, and costs8
  • Comparative effectiveness and safety analyses4
  • Registries, post-market follow-up, and commitments10
  • Label expansion, LOE, and mature evidence refresh7,13,14
