HEOR operating map

HEOR project types across the product life cycle

Vendor service pages converge on a simple operating model: define value and pre-launch RWE needs early, build integrated evidence before launch, translate it for HTA/payers at launch, and refresh it with observed RWE after uptake.

Lifecycle logic
Define value early
Build payer-relevant evidence
Translate for HTA and access
Refresh with RWE after uptake

Post-launch optimization
Set access hypotheses before evidence is locked
  • Landscape review and reimbursement roadmap
  • Unmet need, epidemiology, and burden-of-illness RWE
  • Early value assessment and portfolio prioritization
  • Evidence gap analysis and integrated evidence plan
  • Endpoint, COA, PRO, and utility strategy
Make trials usable for market access and HTA
  • Trial design for market access / HTA requirements
  • Evidence synthesis plus ITC / NMA feasibility
  • Early economic model to inform price and evidence needs
  • Utility, HRQoL, and patient-preference studies
  • RWE protocol or external-control feasibility
Assemble the evidence package and payer story
  • Core value story and product value proposition
  • Cost-effectiveness and budget impact models
  • SLR, NMA, MAIC, ITC, and meta-analysis workstream
  • AMCP, PIE, GVD, and HTA dossier planning
  • Pre-launch RWE evidence package and message testing
Convert evidence into access decisions
  • HTA and reimbursement submissions
  • Global-to-local model adaptations
  • Payer tools, field materials, and objection handlers
  • Appraisal, hearing, and post-submission support
  • Publication and affiliate / field training
Refresh value with observed use and outcomes
  • Observed uptake: database, chart review, and survey studies
  • Treatment patterns, HCRU, adherence, and costs
  • Comparative effectiveness and safety analyses
  • Registries, post-market follow-up, and commitments
  • Label expansion, LOE, and mature evidence refresh
