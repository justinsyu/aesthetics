HEOR operating map

HEOR project types across the product life cycle

Typical HEOR vendor work centers on evidence synthesis, economic modeling, patient-centered outcomes, and real-world evidence—not payer messaging or access execution.

Lifecycle logic

Compare treatments
Model value
Refresh with real-world outcomes

Post-launch evidence refresh
Size burden and plan outcome evidence early
  • Epidemiology and treated-population sizing
  • Unmet need and burden-of-illness studies
  • Cost-of-illness and HCRU characterization
  • Early evidence gap assessment
  • PRO, COA, endpoint, and utility planning
Build patient-centered and comparative evidence plans
  • Trial HEOR endpoint strategy
  • HRQoL, PRO, and utility data collection plans
  • RWE protocol and external-control feasibility
  • Early cost-effectiveness model
  • ITC / NMA feasibility assessment
Synthesize clinical, economic, and outcomes evidence
  • Systematic and targeted literature reviews
  • Network meta-analysis and indirect treatment comparison
  • MAIC, STC, or external comparator analyses
  • Cost-effectiveness and budget impact models
  • Pre-launch RWE studies and HEOR publications
Adapt models and analyses for launch evidence needs
  • Global-to-local economic model adaptation
  • Evidence synthesis updates
  • Utility mapping or vignette studies
  • Comparative effectiveness analyses
  • HEOR publication support
Update evidence with observed treatment and outcome data
  • Retrospective database and chart review studies
  • Treatment patterns, adherence, HCRU, and costs
  • Comparative effectiveness and safety studies
  • Registry and prospective observational studies
  • Economic model and burden update studies
