# HEOR lifecycle project map: source mapping notes

## Stage mapping

- Early asset / indication strategy: grounded mainly in Lumanity HEOR strategy offerings for landscape reviews, reimbursement road mapping, portfolio planning, early product value assessment, clinical trial design, and evidence generation plans; Lumanity Our Approach "engage early" content on market/patient needs, health economic assessments, value proposition, stage-gating, value drivers, and risks; Cytel market access planning around early alignment and a market access roadmap; OPEN Health RWE/data analytics for epidemiology and burden; IQVIA pre-approval RWE applications for characterizing unmet need and disease burden; HEOR Ltd for evidence gap analysis and generation plans; ICON for COA, endpoint, utility, and early evidence-generation roadmaps.
- Clinical development: grounded in Lumanity market access / HTA trial design and Our Approach evidence-generation planning; Cytel's product lifecycle alignment, value evidence analysis, integrated evidence plan + SLR, and Evidence Hub use cases for R&D / clinical development; HEOR Ltd ITC/NMA feasibility and evidence synthesis; ICON patient preference / utility and outcomes selection services; Xcenda early HTA consultation / scientific advice and evidence-generation needs for reimbursement; Cytel RWE services for lifecycle RWE and external control capabilities; and IQVIA pre-approval RWE applications such as improving trial design, characterizing unmet needs, refining endpoints, and using historical/concurrent controls.
- Pre-launch access readiness: grounded in Lumanity value propositions, value dossiers, AMCP/PIE materials, SLR/TLR, ITC, models, advisory boards, and message testing; Lumanity Our Approach value proposition / stakeholder requirements / integrated evidence-generation plan content; Cytel market access roadmap, value evidence analysis, integrated evidence plan + SLR, product value proposition, value communication, and Evidence Hub; Cencora market access strategy from pre-launch to product maturity, HTA readiness, HEOR, reimbursement/launch, value/access strategy, market research, and payer insights; Xcenda global/local access consulting, payer insights, pricing research, economic models, epidemiology assessments, GVDs, advisory boards, HTA/reimbursement assessment, SLR/NMA, and database study capabilities; ICON GVDs, payer slide decks, objection handlers, early advice, and HTA dossiers. This is also where pre-launch RWE outputs are packaged into the payer / HTA narrative after earlier epidemiology, burden-of-illness, protocol, or external-control feasibility work.
- Launch / HTA submission: grounded in Lumanity HTA strategy, dossier writing, appraisal meeting preparation, local model adaptations, payer tools, and post-submission support; Cytel market access support for dossier preparation, local payer requirements, value communication, and health-authority evaluation support; Cencora reimbursement and policy consulting, geographic launch sequencing, HTA readiness, and evidence-based decision-maker engagement; Xcenda global/local launch support, GVDs, HTA submissions, global-to-local affiliate alignment, and market access support through HTA/reimbursement processes; ICON HTA submissions / early advice and value communications; HEOR Ltd dossier development, submission, post-submission support, national/local adaptations, objection handlers, payer negotiation, and training; Ipsos publication and field-tool framing.
- Post-launch optimization: grounded in Cytel RWE database studies, medical chart reviews, linked data, patient surveys, post-authorization safety studies, post-market analysis, Evidence Hub commercial/epidemiology use cases, and market utilization / post-marketing safety; OPEN Health RWE questions around treatment patterns, comparative effectiveness, and costs; Cencora comparative effectiveness research, outcomes analyzers, lifecycle management, retention, and LOE strategy; Xcenda RWE support along the entire product lifecycle, including database studies, adherence, resource use, care gaps, and prospective studies / registries; IQVIA post-approval RWE uses including post-market follow-up, active safety surveillance, effectiveness evaluation, registries, and market value messaging; Ipsos established-brand / LoE lifecycle framing. In the slide, post-launch RWE is reserved for observed uptake/outcomes, effectiveness/safety, registries, commitments, and evidence refresh rather than implying that all RWE starts after launch.

## Taxonomy note

The final five lifecycle stage labels are a transparent synthesis across vendor service descriptions. They are not presented as an exact taxonomy endorsed by Lumanity, Cytel, Cencora, Xcenda, or any other individual vendor.
