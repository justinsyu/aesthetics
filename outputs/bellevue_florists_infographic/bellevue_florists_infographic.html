Method notes

Google Maps was queried for "florist Bellevue, WA," "flower shop Bellevue, WA," and Bellevue ZIPs 98004-98008 on May 10, 2026. Sponsored out-of-area ads were excluded unless the same business appeared organically. Product prices are public advertised prices, not checkout totals.

Sources: Google Maps result panels for Bellevue florist searches; official/public storefront pages for Lawrence The Florist, French Florist, City Flowers, Bellevue Crossroads Florist, DeLaurenti Florist, Finishing Touch Florist & Gifts, Williams Flower & Gift, FLEUR, Le Fleurette, and The Van London Collection.
