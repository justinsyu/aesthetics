Limitations | Classification scope

The analysis is a local-corpus screen and should be treated as an auditable topic inventory

What is source-backed

  • Corpus size, retained record count, original titles, EHA IDs, abstract numbers, presentation metadata, and URLs come from local source records.1
  • Matched AI terms are deterministic lexical matches stored per record in the companion exports.1

What is analyst-derived

  • Topic cluster, disease area, use-case, and method-type labels are rule-based classifications for review.
  • Records without explicit AI/ML framing may be excluded even if they used advanced statistics.
  • Live-posting changes after the local scrape timestamp were not evaluated in this run.1
