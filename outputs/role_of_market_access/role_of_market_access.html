Launch readiness

Evidence for launch success

Decision-ready evidence has to answer what payers, HTA bodies, clinicians, and patients actually need to know.

The shared decision problem

Decision questions
Clinical value
Does it improve outcomes for the right population?

Effectiveness and clinical utility sit at the center of HTA and value assessment. 1,3

Economic value
Is the benefit worth the resources used?

Cost-effectiveness, budget impact, and economic models shape the decision frame. 2,3,4

Patient value
Does it reflect quality of life and real-world use?

The value case includes patient quality of life, real-world evidence, and uncertainty. 1

What needs to be ready before launch

Clinical, economic, patient, and implementation evidence should be connected before formal review, pricing, coverage, and adoption decisions begin.

What the evidence package has to do

From research to reimbursement
Input
Define population, comparator, endpoint, and unmet need.
Model
Project clinical, cost, and quality-of-life outcomes.
Dossier
Present objective clinical and economic evidence.
Review
Translate evidence into coverage, policy, and payment.
Monitor
Monitor affordability, uptake, and patient reach.

What changes across the product lifecycle

Launch work by phase
Pre-approval
Build

Map evidence gaps, target populations, endpoints, comparators, payer needs, likely restrictions, and launch price corridor.

Submission
Package

Finalize models, dossier evidence, sensitivity analyses, quality-of-life inputs, and the value narrative for HTA, formulary, coverage, and reimbursement criteria.

Launch
Execute

Support budget impact, subgroup value, uncertainty responses, terms, restrictions, stakeholder sequencing, and access barrier removal.

Post-launch
Refresh

Generate real-world evidence on utilization, outcomes, persistence, and total cost of care; refresh evidence, expand coverage, and maintain affordability arguments.
