Design system

Adapt the template by changing tokens, not rebuilding slides

Primary

Deep clinical blue

Used for headlines, main table headers, route anchors, and conclusion bars.

Scientific

RevMed-inspired green

Used for evidence maturity, clinical-data modules, study workstreams, and positive coverage.

Decision

Selective orange signal

Used only for priority gaps, decision gates, funding asks, and risk triggers.

Structure

White space and panel gray

Borrow the investor-deck restraint: one dominant evidence object, limited ornament, strong margins.

Typography

Bold sans-serif hierarchy

Large declarative headlines, compact clinical tables, directly labeled charts, readable appendix text.

Motifs

Blocks, rails, data routes

Use left navigation, clinical green bands, study IDs, table instruments, and decision pins.

Study options

Choose evidence tactics based on the decision standard and usable timing

Clinical / interventional

Phase IV, pragmatic trial, extension, comparative study, post-approval commitment.

RWE / data strategy

Retrospective database, prospective registry, external control, burden-of-illness study.

HEOR / market access

SLR, ITC/NMA, economic model, budget impact, payer research, HTA scenario work.

Patient-centered

COA, PRO, HRQoL, preference, adherence, caregiver burden, qualitative interviews.

Medical / publication

Post-hoc analysis, congress abstract, manuscript, evidence compendium, field medical use.

External research

IIR areas, collaborative registry, investigator network, guideline-oriented evidence questions.

Appendix worksheets

Keep dense traceability in appendix pages, not on briefing slides

Regional adaptation

Market, local evidence need, reusable global evidence, local gap, adaptation tactic, affiliate owner, timing.

Source log

Source, date, owner, URL or document locator, evidence type, confidence, limitation, linked claim or gap.

Methodology

Source review, interviews, workshop inputs, scoring rubric, assumptions, exclusions, and version history.

Glossary
