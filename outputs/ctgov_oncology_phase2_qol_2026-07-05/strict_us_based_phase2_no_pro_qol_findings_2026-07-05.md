## Method Notes

- Phase 3 initiation estimate column added from web searches run on 2026-07-06 UTC; entries distinguish official company forward-looking estimates from official company-confirmed initiated status, and CT.gov-only registry status was not treated as an official company estimate.
- CT.gov NCT links in the retained table point to the Phase 2 or mixed-phase trials reviewed.
- The endpoint confirmation is limited to structured CT.gov outcome fields: primary, secondary, and other outcomes.
- Two conservative endpoint exclusions were preserved from worker review: MeiraGTx AAV2-hAQP1 had a xerostomia-specific questionnaire endpoint, and Palvella/PTX-022 had a Patient Global Impression of Change endpoint.
- A product is not described as product-wide no-PRO/QoL unless the relevant Phase 2 trials checked are listed or otherwise auditable in the row.
