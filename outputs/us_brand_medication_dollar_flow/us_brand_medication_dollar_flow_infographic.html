Where a U.S. brand medication dollar is retained

A stylized retail-channel view of a $100 brand-drug expenditure shows manufacturers collecting about $76 and retaining about $58 after production cost, while insurers, PBMs, pharmacies, and wholesalers retain smaller gross amounts before their operating expenses.1

The same USC figure shows manufacturers collecting about $76 of the $100 brand-drug expenditure and retaining $58 after estimated production cost.1

Accounting-profit caution

USC reports brand-specific net margins only for manufacturers, with brand-drug manufacturer net margin estimated at 28.1%; non-manufacturer brand-specific net margins were not estimated.2

$606B

U.S. medicine spending at net prices in 2025, up from $548B in 2024; IQVIA attributes most of the $58B increase to protected brands outside GIP/GLP-1 agonists and COVID-19 products.3

6-9%

IQVIA projects U.S. medicine spending growth through 2030 at 6-9% on a list-price basis versus 4.5-7.5% after discounts, rebates, and other concessions.3

31%

NASEM estimated PBM intermediary margins rose from 23% in 2020 to 31% in 2022 in its retail-channel sample.4

~80%

FTC reported that the top three PBMs processed nearly 80% of about 6.6B prescriptions dispensed by U.S. pharmacies in 2023.5

Read the pool as retained revenue, not literal profit

Key caveats
  • Gross retained amounts include operating costs and other uses of funds; they are not equivalent to GAAP net income or economic profit.2
  • NASEM estimated net transaction values after rebates, discounts, and DIR, but cautioned that omitted manufacturer concessions such as copay assistance, 340B-related discounts, Medicaid best-price effects, and PBM service fees may bias PBM and pharmacy margin estimates.4
  • USC Schaeffer notes that PBM pass-through payments and vertical integration can make reported PBM profit margins highly sensitive to revenue-recognition choices and public segment reporting.6
Source note. Dollar-flow values are a normalized, retail-channel, privately insured brand-drug model from USC Schaeffer Figure 3 Panel A; components round to approximately $100 and should be read as gross retained amounts or production cost, not literal accounting profit.
