AUP = pharmacy selling price

TLV describes AUP as apotekens utförsäljningspris, the regulated pharmacy sales price for products in the medicines benefits system.

AIP = pharmacy wholesale price

TLV describes AIP as apotekens inköpspris. The pharmacy margin is the difference between AIP and AUP.
