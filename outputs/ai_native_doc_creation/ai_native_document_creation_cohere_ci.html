Executive summary

  • The market is splitting into submission-first platforms, protocol-first workspaces, CSR/narrative automation, study-startup document generators, and document graph/change-control systems.
  • Weave Bio and Yseop show the clearest maturity signals: Weave reported $20M Series A funding and a Parexel partnership, while Yseop publicly reports Word/Veeva-integrated, traceable regulatory authoring and major-pharma references.234
  • The visible feature is first-draft generation; the real battleground is source-to-claim traceability, redlines, audit trails, reviewer control, and cross-document consistency.

Diligence screen

RFP evaluation should test regulated workflow performance, not demo-page fluency

1. Source map

Can every claim be traced?

Require sentence- or word-level source links to TLFs, protocols, SAPs, IBs, prior submissions, guidance, and sponsor standards.

2. Review control

Can writers stay in charge?

Check tracked changes, reviewer comments, human approval, no-answer behavior, template governance, and controlled prompts.

3. System fit

Can it work where writers work?

Prioritize Word, Veeva Vault, SharePoint, structured-content repositories, clinical data packages, and exportable audit logs.

4. Change propagation

Does it update dependent documents?

Test whether a protocol amendment flags downstream effects in SAP, ICF, CSR shells, Module 2 summaries and operational materials.

5. Deployment model

Can sponsor data remain controlled?

Verify tenant isolation, local or private deployment options, retention policy, access controls, validation documents and data-residency options.

6. Evidence of maturity

Is the vendor deployable?

Ask for named references, partner deployments, funding runway, implementation team, validation artifacts and security certifications.

Shortlist logic

A practical screen starts with document type, then narrows by traceability and deployment proof

CSR and clinical reporting

Start with Yseop, TriloDocs, Narrativa, AlphaLife and Peer AI

Use TrialAssure and Certara as benchmarks where disclosure, anonymization, services or established regulatory-writing workflows are part of the buyer requirement.

IND / CTA / CTD / dossier

Start with Weave, Ritivel, Angelonia, Tehistark and Raycaster

Compare against Yseop, Certara and Docuvera when the workflow requires enterprise-grade traceability, structured content reuse or regulated document-management integration.

Protocol design and authoring

Start with Faro, Risklick, Kitsa, Cori, Merquell and Avenio

Include KNavis, Luminari, Confyde, Clinion and TrialStack as broader or earlier-stage comparisons depending on trial design, amendment risk and startup-package needs.

Protocol-derived operations

Use HumanTrue, Clinials, Tryal, Trialynx and Nimble as adjacent workflow screens

These are relevant when the buyer wants site, patient, feasibility or startup content generated from the protocol rather than full regulatory submission authoring.

Competitive hypothesis

  • The winning vendors will not be those with the most document types listed. They will be the vendors that can keep source evidence, sponsor standards, reviewer comments, and downstream documents synchronized through regulated change.
