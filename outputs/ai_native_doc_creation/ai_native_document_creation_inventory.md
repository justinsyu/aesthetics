# AI-Native Startups Focused on Life-Sciences Document Creation

Scope: AI-native, generative-AI-first, or heavily AI-enabled companies focused on creating, drafting, reviewing, harmonizing, or updating regulated life-sciences documents, including clinical protocols, CSRs, patient narratives, investigator brochures, informed-consent forms, CTD/eCTD modules, IND/NDA/BLA/CTA content, CMC sections, safety documents, and study-startup packages.

Retrieval date: June 3, 2026.

Confidence definitions:

- **Core**: Document creation is a primary product workflow and the public source explicitly describes AI-native, agentic, GenAI, LLM, NLG, or AI-powered drafting/review.
- **Protocol-first**: Strong fit for protocol design/authoring and protocol-derived documents; may not yet cover CSR or full dossier workflows.
- **Submission-first**: Strong fit for CTD/eCTD, IND/NDA/BLA/CTA, CMC, or regulatory submission-document workflows.
- **Adjacent**: Useful comparison point, but the company is broader, more incumbent-like, more clinical-operations-focused, or less clearly AI-native.
- **Watchlist**: Public evidence is thin, very new, or unusually marketing-heavy; merits demo and reference checks before serious diligence.

## Diligence Implications

- The market is no longer just "AI writing." The strongest vendors frame document creation as governed source-to-output automation, not generic text generation.
- Protocol tooling is accelerating because ICH M11/CeSHarP and USDM give vendors a structured target for protocol content, data exchange, and downstream reuse.
- CSR/narrative products are more mature where vendors combine deterministic extraction from TLFs/source documents with controlled generation and human medical-writer review.
- Submission-first vendors differentiate on traceability, reproducibility, local deployment, audit trails, health-authority response workflows, and cross-document consistency rather than first-draft speed alone.
- The broadest claims appear in very new vendors. For serious diligence, request a live generation from sponsor-provided source files, a full source map, validation package, security architecture, and redline/change-control workflow.
