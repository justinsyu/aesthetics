4. **Sequence work by launch horizon**
   - Screenshot: `screenshots/03-launch-horizon-filter.png`
   - Caption: Launch-horizon filtering isolates assets expected in a specific timing bucket while preserving heatmap and detail context.

   - Screenshot: `screenshots/04-asset-detail-traceability.png`

6. **Interpretation / definitions**

   - P&T use: Ensures committee users understand that the tool supports formulary planning, not clinical safety adjudication.
