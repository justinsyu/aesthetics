  5. Cimerli Supply to Be Paused, Drop in ASP May Be to Blame. Biosimilars Review & Report, February 24, 2025. Ranibizumab biosimilar Cimerli (FYB201) reached 49% volume share in Q3 2024 (Lucentis 43%, Byooviz 8%); increasing price discounts among ranibizumab providers were cited as Cimerli ASP dropped >35% over the prior 12 months.
  6. Study Finds Pavblu Biosimilar Safe and Effective in Real-World Use. Managed Healthcare Executive, ASRS 2025 conference coverage. Real-world analysis of 7,773 eyes treated by 302 retina specialists across 255 locations found stable vision and no unexpected safety signals; the article frames studies like this as important for confirming biosimilar safety and effectiveness outside controlled trials.
  7. Aflibercept Biosimilars Market Leads USD 1.68 Bn in 2025. Towards Healthcare market analysis, 2025. Global aflibercept biosimilar market valued at USD $1.68B in 2025; sales-at-risk projected to rise from 9% in 2025 to 25% by 2027 as additional biosimilars enter.
  8. Retina Specialists' Evolving View on Biosimilars in AMD Treatment. The Center for Biosimilars. Discussion of physician autonomy concerns and payer-driven step therapy as gating factors for anti-VEGF biosimilar uptake in wet AMD.
  9. Randomized Trial of Biosimilar ABP 938 Compared with Reference Aflibercept in Adults with Neovascular Age-Related Macular Degeneration. Ophthalmology Retina, 2025.
