Executive summary

  • Kura and Kyowa Kirin reported 99 frontline ziftomenib plus 7+3 patients, CRc rates of 96% in NPM1-mutant AML and 90% in KMT2A-rearranged AML, and MRD-negativity among CRc responders of 83% and 82%, respectively.2
  • BlossomHill reported BH-30236 activity signals: 29% of evaluable monotherapy patients and 55% of evaluable venetoclax-combination patients had at least 50% bone marrow blast reductions, including one MRD-negative complete remission.3
  • Moleculin reported no clinically significant left ventricular dysfunction among 78 verified ejection-fraction assessments; CERo reported second-cohort CER-1236 completion with no DLTs through the assessment period; Evaxion reported EVX-04 immune-response and tumor-growth-prevention data.465

Program map

Menin and CLK inhibitor programs were reported across three included sources123

Revumenib

Acute leukemia continuum

Syndax listed key revumenib data accepted for EHA 2026, including real-world evidence, frontline data, post-HSCT maintenance, relapsed/refractory combination data, and NUP98-rearranged acute leukemia results.1

Ziftomenib

Frontline intensive chemotherapy

Kura and Kyowa Kirin reported updated KOMET-007 results for ziftomenib 600 mg once daily with cytarabine plus daunorubicin in newly diagnosed NPM1-mutant or KMT2A-rearranged AML.2

BH-30236

Dose escalation and venetoclax combination

BlossomHill reported BH-30236 monotherapy and venetoclax-combination evaluation, with 28 monotherapy patients and 11 combination patients as of the January 23, 2026 cutoff.3

Program map

Anthracycline, vaccine, and cellular-therapy programs also reported AML updates456

Annamycin

Cardiac-safety analysis

Moleculin reported that an independent cardiac review analyzed monitoring data from 90 Annamycin-treated patients and found no clinically significant left ventricular dysfunction among 78 verified ejection-fraction assessments.4

EVX-04

Preclinical vaccine package

Evaxion reported that all 16 ERV fragments in EVX-04 elicit a specific immune response, that EVX-04 prevents tumor growth in preclinical models, and that a clinical trial application was planned for the second half of 2026.5

CER-1236

Cellular immunotherapy dose escalation

CERo reported escalation to a planned 1 x 10^7 cells/kg split-dose cohort and expected the next cohort to include patients with MDS and myelofibrosis.6
