Interpretation guardrails

Predictive-only records often describe statistical learning, prognostic models, risk scores, or simulation; they are included for landscape completeness, not as proof of AI deployment.
Direct AI/ML predictive records combine predictive framing with machine-learning methods or explicit AI language, such as XGBoost, random forests, neural networks, or ML model statements.
For follow-up CI, prioritize evidence quality, validation cohorts, model calibration, fairness, workflow integration, and whether records report prospective clinical impact.
