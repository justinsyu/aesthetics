# Wet AMD Retina Specialist Personas

## Purpose

This folder contains a pragmatic, non-exhaustive retina KOL shortlist, individual specialist profiles, and a reusable persona-creation strategy for simulated advisory board work focused on pipeline treatments for wet age-related macular degeneration (wet AMD, neovascular AMD, nAMD).

This is not an objective ranking of "best" clinicians. It is a working advisory-board shortlist designed to include retina specialists whose public records suggest they would bring useful perspectives on clinical trial evidence, anti-VEGF durability, treatment burden, safety, procedural delivery, gene therapy, imaging, and real-world implementation.

## Selection Methodology

Selection favored retina specialists with multiple signals relevant to a simulated wet AMD pipeline advisory board:

- Demonstrated wet AMD or neovascular AMD clinical trial leadership, authorship, or investigator roles.
- Influence on pivotal anti-VEGF evidence, including ranibizumab, aflibercept, faricimab, aflibercept 8 mg, PDS/Susvimo, and gene therapy programs.
- Academic, society, editorial, or research leadership in retina.
- Relevance to US practice patterns where possible, including high-volume private retina practices and academic retina services.
- Ability to represent distinct advisory-board tensions: visual acuity versus anatomy, durability versus safety, trial efficacy versus real-world burden, injectable versus implant/gene therapy delivery, and adoption in different practice settings.

Public sources included institutional profiles, PubMed-indexed publications, clinical-trial publications, society materials, and public industry/financial disclosure pages where available. Profiles do not claim private opinions. Each individual profile separates evidence-backed facts from analyst-derived persona hypotheses that should be treated as plausible simulation inputs, not statements of the physician's actual views.

## Advisory-Board Use Notes

Use these profiles as persona scaffolding, not as factual scripts of what named physicians would say.

- Label persona statements as inferred: for example, "likely to ask for inflammation mitigation details" rather than "believes X."
- Keep personas anchored to public evidence: trial roles, institutional setting, publications, and stated research interests.
- For pipeline wet AMD assets, prompt each persona on:
  - durability and injection reduction versus benchmark anti-VEGF agents;
  - inflammation, vasculitis, endophthalmitis, surgery/device, and corticosteroid-management risk;
  - BCVA, CST/fluid, disease-control consistency, and rescue-treatment rules;
  - treat-and-extend fit, visit cadence, home monitoring, and patient adherence;
  - trial population generalizability to elderly, bilateral, high-burden, previously treated, and underrepresented patients;
  - operational burden for academic centers, high-volume private practices, and surgical retina settings;
  - payer/value implications, especially where off-label bevacizumab or biosimilars are relevant comparators.

## Reusable Persona Strategy

Use [persona_creation_strategy.md](persona_creation_strategy.md) when adding new specialists, refreshing these profiles, or adapting this method to another disease area. The strategy defines evidence classes, update triggers, profile structure, simulation-output rules, and the boundary between source-backed facts and analyst-derived advisory-board behavior.

## Interview Question References

Use [interview_question_references/](interview_question_references/) as an evaluation set for persona calibration. It contains public interview prompts/questions and URLs for each specialist, plus a consolidated [interview_question_index.csv](interview_question_references/interview_question_index.csv). The files distinguish visible interview questions from weaker topic prompts and note whether the actual answer is available as transcript text, article quotes, video, or audio.

For model evaluation, start with the opinion-filtered benchmark at [interview_question_references/opinion_filtered/](interview_question_references/opinion_filtered/). That subset removes fact-recall and generic explanatory prompts so the remaining questions are more likely to reveal the doctor's perspective, judgment, risk tolerance, evidence interpretation, and adoption logic.

## Important Limitations

- The shortlist is US-heavy by design and excludes many credible global nAMD KOLs.
- Public industry disclosures are incomplete and time-sensitive; they were not exhaustively audited across every payment database, society disclosure, or recent meeting.
- Publication authorship does not prove treatment preference.
- Institutional pages can be promotional and may lag current appointments.
- Persona hypotheses should be reviewed by a medical affairs or clinical expert before use in any high-stakes simulation.
