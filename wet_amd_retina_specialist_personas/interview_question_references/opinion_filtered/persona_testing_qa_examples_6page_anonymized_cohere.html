Question

How does a provider choose among wet AMD treatments, including patient access and step-therapy considerations?

Actual answer summary

The specialist emphasized patient and physician choice, timely access, and matching the right medicine to the patient. The specialist said Avastin can work but is off-label and requires repackaging, opposed mandatory step therapy because it can delay care and risk vision, and advised patients to understand insurance constraints.

Why this tests a persona

Strong discriminator for payer pressure, access friction, and willingness to push back on policy constraints when vision is at risk.

Monitoring and Retreatment Logic

Page 2 / 5
Persona exampleMonitoring / treatment selection

Anonymous specialist

Question

How do you decide on retreatment after anti-VEGF therapy: visual acuity, OCT, or fluorescein angiography?

Actual answer summary

The specialist relies heavily on qualitative OCT for retreatment and treats for any macular fluid until the macula is dry. The specialist then follows monthly to learn the patient's fluid-free interval and returns just before expected recurrence, or uses treat-and-extend injections while dry to define and maintain that interval.

Why this tests a persona

Clear decision rule for OCT-driven retreatment; useful for testing precision, fluid tolerance, and protocol logic.

For patients who need frequent injections and are not fully dry after multiple monthly anti-VEGF injections, what is your next approach and patient discussion?

The specialist said the diagnosis is rechecked and masqueraders are considered before labeling a patient as a high-VEGF-need incomplete responder. Durable Delivery and Gene Therapy Thresholds

Persona exampleAdoption / treatment selection

Anonymous specialist

Question

Discuss whether retinal gene therapy is working, including anti-VEGF biofactory approaches for wet AMD, and where it may fit relative to injections.

Actual answer summary

The specialist said retinal gene therapy is moving from rare inherited disease into common retina diseases such as wet AMD, with evidence that anti-VEGF-like proteins can be produced durably. The specialist does not expect injections to disappear; the specialist sees gene therapy as a possible foundation therapy for selected high-burden patients, with injections still used as needed.

Why this tests a persona

Tests adoption threshold for disruptive therapy and whether the persona avoids overclaiming replacement of injections.

Question

Explain the significance of suprachoroidal RGX-314 AAVIATE data for high-burden previously treated nAMD patients.

Actual answer summary

The specialist called AAVIATE important because it was the first suprachoroidal gene-therapy trial and showed early tolerability plus meaningful treatment-burden reduction while maintaining anatomy and vision. The specialist stressed that RGX-314 is not curative, but a one-time in-clinic approach could reduce repeated anti-VEGF injections.

Why this tests a persona

Tests enthusiasm control: recognizes burden reduction and novelty while avoiding cure claims.

Risk, Futility, and Patient Selection

Persona exampleRisk / patient selection

Anonymous specialist

Question

When would an eye with wet AMD not receive treatment?

Actual answer summary

The specialist said active wet AMD may sometimes be observed or dosed infrequently when there is a disciform scar, limited visual potential, a well-sighted fellow eye, or near end of life. The specialist emphasized individualized decisions rather than a single universal stopping rule.

Why this tests a persona

Tests treatment futility thresholds, patient-context reasoning, and willingness to depart from reflexive treatment.

In a patient with prior cerebrovascular accident or myocardial infarction and occult CNV, what treatment would you choose?

Actual answer summary

The specialist said prior CVA or MI does not change his treatment choice. The specialist presents options and finds patients generally accept even a small possible vascular risk to avoid likely blindness or vision loss, framing the decision as shared risk-benefit judgment.

Practice Burden and Future Workflow

Page 5 / 5
Persona exampleEvidence / operations

Anonymous specialist

Question

What are your impressions of the durability of currently available anti-VEGF agents?

Actual answer summary

The specialist said durability varies by patient but is broadly similar across agents, with many patients extending beyond 8 weeks but fewer reaching 12 to 16 weeks. The specialist emphasized that durability definitions depend on tolerated exudation thresholds, and described PDS as technically more demanding than injection but well tolerated and supported by LADDER/ARCHWAY maintenance data.

Why this tests a persona

Tests realistic durability expectations, exudation tolerance, and procedural adoption realism.

Persona exampleOperations / risk tolerance

Anonymous specialist

Question

How do emerging options such as PDS, bispecific therapy, home OCT, and durability-focused therapies address wet AMD burden?

Actual answer summary

The specialist said durability is the biggest unmet need because anti-VEGF works when patients adhere, but maintaining frequent care is difficult. The specialist viewed PDS as a potential burden reducer while recognizing a small absolute, higher relative infection risk, and saw future durability plus home OCT as ways to reduce burden.

Why this tests a persona

Tests durability-versus-safety tradeoff, procedural realism, and openness to monitoring-enabled care models.
