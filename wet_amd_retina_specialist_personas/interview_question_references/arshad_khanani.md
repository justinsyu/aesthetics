## Search limitations

- Many ophthalmology video pages publish only a title and short description, not a transcript. Those entries are labeled as interview prompts/topics rather than exact questions.
- HCPLive often summarizes the interview and provides selected quotes. I did not infer exact question wording from those articles.
- I prioritized wet AMD/nAMD, anti-VEGF durability, faricimab, PDS/Susvimo, gene therapy, biosimilars/value, retina pipeline, and clinical trial design. GA/DME items were included only when they strengthen the durability, pipeline, access, or study-design evaluation set.
