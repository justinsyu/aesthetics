- Allen Ho has many public AMD sources as moderator, editor, or presenter; fewer accessible wet AMD sources show him as the person answering a direct interviewer question.
- The strongest Ho-as-respondent items found were the Eyetube optogenetics transcript and the Fellow Eye transcript sections on wet AMD gene therapy; both are broader than classic nAMD treatment selection.
- Retina Today and New Retina Radio sources are still valuable evaluation prompts, but several are Ho-moderated discussions where actual answers are mainly from other panelists.
