## Search limitations
The public Brown set was much sparser than the Wykoff set. Searches found many Brown clinical-trial publications, press-release quotes, conference-program appearances, and articles where he presented data, but relatively few public interviews with accessible question text. Entries DB-005 through DB-008 are weaker fallback prompts and are labeled as topic prompts rather than exact questions. For evaluation, DB-001 through DB-004 should be weighted higher.
