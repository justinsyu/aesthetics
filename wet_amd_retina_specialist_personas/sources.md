## Notes on Source Quality

- PubMed and PMC sources were treated as the strongest evidence for clinical trial authorship and trial design.
- Institutional profiles were used for appointments, research interests, and practice-setting context, but may be promotional or lag current employment.
- Company press releases and trade publications were used only for public role/disclosure signals and pipeline exposure, not as independent evidence of product efficacy.
- Public financial disclosures were not comprehensively audited across CMS Open Payments, every journal article, and every meeting presentation.
