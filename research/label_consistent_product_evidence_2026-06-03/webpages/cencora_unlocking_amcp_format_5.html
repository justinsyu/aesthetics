Unlocking AMCP Format Version 5.0: Navigating updates and best practices for formulary dossiers

Watch this webinar on-demand today to learn about the latest AMCP Format Version 5.0 updates and best practices for formulary dossiers, featuring Adam Hoye-Simek, chair of the AMCP Format for Formulary Submissions Committee, and experts from the Cencora Evidence Generation and Value Communications, and Evidence Synthesis and Modeling teams.

This engaging webinar will kick off with a presentation covering key revisions in the AMCP Format 5.0, followed by an insightful panel discussion. Watch now to learn about dossier updates timing, proactive sharing strategies, economic modeling best practices, and more. Don’t miss this opportunity to refine your dossier submissions with expert guidance.

Moderator: Jasmine Knight, PharmD, MS, Senior Director, Digital Solutions, Cencora

Panelists:

  • Adam Hoye-Simek, PharmD, RPH, Chair, Format for Formulary Submissions Committee, AMCP
  • Ken O'Day, PhD, MPA, Senior Director, Evidence Synthesis & Modeling, Cencora
  • Ben Penley, PharmD, MS, Manager, Evidence Generation & Value Communications, Cencora
  • Kimberly Ruiz, EdM, Senior Director, Evidence Generation & Value Communications, Cencora

 
What you’ll come away with:

  • Understanding of the updates and revisions in the latest AMCP Format Version 5.0
  • Best practices for creating dossiers that align with the new AMCP Format guidance
  • Effective approaches to economic modeling with dossier submissions
  • Strategies for digital therapeutics, leveraging real-world evidence, addressing health disparities, and optimizing clinical evidence in dossier content
