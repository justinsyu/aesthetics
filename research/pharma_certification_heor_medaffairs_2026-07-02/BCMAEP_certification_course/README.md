# BCMAEP Certification Course

**Board Certified Medical Affairs and Evidence Professional (BCMAEP)**
Program brand: PRIME (Pharmaceutical Readiness in Integrated evidence and Medical affairs Excellence)
Version 1.0, prepared 2026-07-02

This package is a complete, source-backed certification course that prepares clinicians and other professionals to transition into pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and medical-technology industry roles across medical affairs, medical science liaison (MSL), medical information, HEOR, market access, and evidence generation. It is benchmarked against the Accreditation Council for Medical Affairs BCMAS program and designed to match its role coverage and add depth in integrated evidence generation planning, HEOR and market access, and responsible AI use.

## What this program is, in one paragraph

BCMAEP is a board-style certification: a structured 15-module curriculum leads to a criterion-referenced board examination and a rubric-scored work-product portfolio. The credential recognizes demonstrated competency, not course attendance. It is designed to be accreditation-ready against ISO/IEC 17024 and the NCCA Standards for the credential and the ANSI/IACET Standard for the curriculum; it is not accredited at version 1.0 (see `governance/accreditation_and_quality_alignment.md`).

## How it aims to exceed existing offerings

- **Integrated coverage.** Medical affairs operations and HEOR/market-access methods are taught in one curriculum, rather than as separate programs.
- **Integrated evidence generation planning (IEGP) as a core competency.** A dedicated module and the signature capstone deliverable, reflecting a named competency in the MAPS Evidence Generation domain and the peer-reviewed integrated-evidence literature.
- **Governed AI throughout.** A program-wide AI-use policy with validation, source verification, privacy, disclosure, and human accountability, grounded in current FDA, EMA, ISPOR, and publication-ethics guidance, and audited in the capstone.
- **A required work-product portfolio.** Eight deliverables that resemble first-year industry outputs, scored with analytic rubrics, so the credential reflects the ability to produce usable work, not only to answer questions.
- **Transparent, source-backed content.** Every sourced teaching claim carries a hyperlinked citation to a verified authoritative source.

## How to use this package

- **A prospective career changer** reads the handbook to understand the credential, then works the modules in order, completing each applied activity, and assembles the capstone portfolio for review.
- **An instructor or program manager** uses the syllabus and modules as courseware, the case library for exercises, and the rubrics for grading; the AI-use policy is enforced in every applied activity.
- **A program designer or sponsoring body** uses the handbook, the exam blueprint, and the accreditation-alignment document as the specification to build an item bank, run a job-task analysis and standard-setting study, and pursue accreditation.

## The credential at a glance

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Modules | 15, organized into five domains, plus a capstone |
| Approximate effort | 90 to 110 hours over a 6-to-12-month window |
| Examination | 150 scored items (plus 15 pilot), 3 hours, proctored, criterion-referenced pass |
| Portfolio | 8 work products plus an executive summary, rubric-scored |
| Recertification | Every 3 years; 30 hours of continuing development, including evidence and AI-governance minimums |
| Accreditation target | ISO/IEC 17024 and NCCA (credential); ANSI/IACET (curriculum) |
