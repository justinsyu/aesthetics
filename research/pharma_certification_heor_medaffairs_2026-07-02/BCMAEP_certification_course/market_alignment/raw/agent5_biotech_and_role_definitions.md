## Part B: role-definition and competency references

### MSL Society (The Medical Science Liaison Society) - What is a Medical Science Liaison (MSL)?
- URL: https://themsls.org/what-is-an-msl/
- Access: fetched
- Role elements / responsibilities:
  - Role within pharmaceutical, biotech, medical device, CRO, and healthcare industries; concentrates on specific therapeutic areas/disease states; typically holds PhD, PharmD, or MD
  - Primary purpose: establish and maintain peer-to-peer relationships with KOLs at major academic institutions and clinics
  - Engaging external stakeholders: develop KOL engagement plans and collect insights; identify clinical investigators; communicate medical/scientific data; connect internal and external stakeholders; provide conference support; support external research activities; respond to medical information requests
  - Collaborating with internal stakeholders: provide training to colleagues; support clinical research teams; serve as subject matter expert on cross-functional teams
  - Maintaining expertise: remain current on scientific knowledge; maintain knowledge of pharmacoeconomics; coordinate activities with other field personnel
  - Core activity focus: engaging with KOLs and other HCPs, directly or indirectly

### MAPS (Medical Affairs Professional Society) - MAPS Medical Affairs Competency Framework
- URL: https://medicalaffairs.org/maps-medical-affairs-competency-framework/
- Access: snippet only (page returned 404 on direct fetch; details from search result and MAPS announcements)
- Role elements / responsibilities:
  - Framework details 42 competencies across 7 domains identified as essential to Medical Affairs professionals' success
  - Purpose: organize existing resources and create new materials to ensure a standard knowledge base across early-career professionals and train the next generation of Medical Affairs leaders
  - Represents a multi-year, skills-based collaboration of industry leaders defining domains and competencies that deliver value to the organization and support a Medical Affairs career
  - 7 Domain Teams (within the MAPS Program Development Committee and Focus Area Working Groups) are building a certification program to train these skills
  - Resources accessible via the MAPS Content HUB, filterable by domain and competency

### Rutgers School of Graduate Studies - Career Spotlight: Medical Science Liaison
- URL: https://grad.rutgers.edu/news/career-spotlight-medical-science-liaison
- Access: fetched
- Role elements / responsibilities:
  - Definition: blends business with technical/scientific acumen to promote disease-state awareness and foster communication between clinicians and in-house researchers
  - Establish and maintain peer-to-peer relationships with KOLs at academic institutions
  - Educate physicians to ensure products are used effectively; function as scientific peers and expert knowledge source within therapeutic area
  - Collaborate with clinicians to guide investigator-initiated clinical trials; assist with publishing clinical-trial papers
  - Cover a specific US region and maintain KOL relationships within that territory
  - Requirements: doctorate (PhD, MD, or PharmD); therapeutic-area expertise
  - Distinction: MSLs are NOT sales representatives; housed under the Medical Affairs group

### APPA, IFAPP, MAPS, MSLS - Promoting Best Practices for Medical Science Liaisons (Position Statement)
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8492581/
- Access: fetched
- Role elements / responsibilities:
  - Primary role: communication of accurate, unbiased scientific and medical information to HCPs; MSLs are the scientific face connecting companies with the medical community
  - Build peer-to-peer credibility through exchange of highly credible, unbiased scientific/clinical information
  - Scientific exchange: deliver presentations/education on disease, unmet needs, treatment landscape, clinical evidence; engage experts on scientific data generation; conduct introductory meetings to assess unmet medical needs
  - Evidence generation: support company-sponsored studies, investigator-initiated trials, real-world evidence studies, clinical audits; facilitate HEOR studies
  - Insight gathering: gather field insights to inform internal clinical development, marketing, and market access; participate in advisory boards, working groups, roundtables; report adverse events within 24 hours
  - Core activities: translate research to clinical practice (bench to bedside); provide non-promotional scientific education; maintain independence from sales/commercial activities; support appropriate and safe medicine use
