# BCMAEP Source Register

This register consolidates the verified sources cited across the BCMAEP package. It is the canonical citation pool for the curriculum: content should cite from these sources (or from the archived source set in the research folder) and should not introduce unverified links. Journal citations are verified via PubMed and carry a DOI; guidance and program citations carry the official page URL.

Attribution note: journal references in sections C and D were verified using PubMed metadata; DOIs link to the article of record.

---

## A. Benchmark programs and certifications

- [ACMA, BCMAS Program Information](https://medicalaffairsspecialist.org/certifications/bcmas/program-info): 13 core competencies, self-paced online, approximately 40 hours, IACET/ANSI provider accreditation, proctored board exam with four attempts.
- [ACMA, BCMAS Recertification](https://medicalaffairsspecialist.org/certifications/bcmas/bcmas-recertification): annual maintenance, two approved learning activities plus attestation.
- [MAPS, Foundations in Medical Affairs](https://medicalaffairs.org/medical-affairs-training/): 42 competencies across seven domains of the MAPS Medical Affairs Competency Framework.
- [MSL Society, MSL-BC Board Certification](https://themsls.org/msl-board-certification/): psychometrically developed exam, bachelor's degree plus one year of MSL experience, 140 items (125 scored), three domains, three-year recertification.
- [ISMPP, CMPP certification](https://www.ismpp.org/cmpp): 150-item exam, three duties (develop plan, implement plan, foster ethical and compliant behavior), five-year credential.
- [DIA, Medical Affairs eLearning Program](https://www.diaglobal.org/en/course-listing/elearning/modules/medical-affairs): scenario-based medical affairs modules.
- [University of Washington CHOICE Institute, Certificate in Health Economics, HTA, and Market Access](https://sop.washington.edu/choice/graduate-education-training-programs/certificate-in-health-economics-health-technology-assessment-and-market-access/): HEOR/HTA/market access certificate.
- [ISPOR, Short Courses](https://www.ispor.org/education-training/short-courses): HEOR method short courses.

## B. Standards and accreditation

- [ISO/IEC 17024:2012](https://www.iso.org/standard/52993.html): general requirements for bodies operating certification of persons.
- [Institute for Credentialing Excellence, NCCA](https://www.credentialingexcellence.org/accreditation/earn-accreditation/ncca): NCCA Standards for the accreditation of certification programs (2021 revision, 23 standards).
- [IACET, ANSI/IACET 1-2018 Standard for Continuing Education and Training](https://www.iacet.org/standards/ansi-iacet-2018-1-standard-for-continuing-education-and-training/): continuing-education provider standard; one CEU equals ten contact hours.

### Compliance and legal frameworks (US and EU primary sources)

- [FDA, Office of Prescription Drug Promotion (OPDP)](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/cder-offices-and-divisions/office-prescription-drug-promotion-opdp): oversight of truthful, non-misleading, fair-balanced prescription drug promotion.
- [FDA, Responding to Unsolicited Requests for Off-Label Information About Prescription Drugs and Medical Devices](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/responding-unsolicited-requests-label-information-about-prescription-drugs-and-medical-devices): the nonpromotional scientific-response channel.
- [HHS Office of Inspector General, Fraud and Abuse Laws](https://oig.hhs.gov/compliance/physician-education/fraud-abuse-laws/): Anti-Kickback Statute, False Claims Act, Stark Law, Exclusion, and Civil Monetary Penalties.
- [CMS, Open Payments (Physician Payments Sunshine Act)](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/key-initiatives/open-payments): public reporting of manufacturer transfers of value.
- [PhRMA, Code on Interactions with Health Care Professionals](https://www.phrma.org/resources/code-on-interactions-with-health-care-professionals): industry self-regulatory standards.
- [HHS, Health Information Privacy (HIPAA)](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/index.html): US health-information privacy.
- [Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng): EU data protection, including special-category health data.

## F. Verification status and cautions

- All journal DOIs in section D were verified via PubMed metadata on 2026-07-02.
- Some official sources (for example fda.gov and iso.org) block automated fetching; where a page could not be fetched directly, the fact was corroborated through an authoritative alternative (the Federal Register notice for the FDA AI guidance; the ISO catalogue listing for ISO/IEC 17024) and is cited to a link that resolves.
- Public availability of a source does not grant republication rights. In any delivered course, treat archived third-party materials as assigned-reading links and cite them; do not reproduce substantial third-party text, slides, or figures without permission.
