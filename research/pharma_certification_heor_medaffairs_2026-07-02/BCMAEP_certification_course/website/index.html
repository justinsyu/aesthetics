Board Certified Medical Affairs and Evidence Professional

BCMAEP prepares clinicians and other professionals moving into pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and medical-technology industry roles: medical affairs, medical science liaison, medical information, HEOR, market access, and evidence generation. A structured 15-module curriculum leads to a criterion-referenced board examination and a rubric-scored work-product portfolio. About the credential

BCMAEP is a board-style certification: a structured 15-module curriculum leads to a criterion-referenced board examination and a rubric-scored work-product portfolio. The credential recognizes demonstrated competency, not course attendance. The curriculum is organized into seven competency domains derived from the responsibilities documented across medical affairs and evidence roles (see the market alignment analysis).

It covers the full role landscape a career changer enters, from field medical and medical information to HEOR, market access, and evidence generation, and adds depth in three areas: integrated evidence generation planning across the product lifecycle; HEOR, health technology assessment, and market access method fluency; and governed use of artificial intelligence with explicit human accountability. Learners produce eight realistic work products scored with published rubrics, so the credential reflects the ability to produce usable work, not only to answer questions. The design and competency framework are set out in the program handbook.

Five instructional domains

Domains A through E map to the 15 modules; medical governance and compliance (Domain F) and strategy and decision making (Domain G) are embedded across modules and assessed in the portfolio. Domain C carries the largest examination weight (30%).

How the credential is earned

1

Complete the curriculum

Work the 15 modules in sequence. Each module states measurable objectives, teaches with cited primary sources, and closes with a worked example, an applied activity that produces a draft work product, and a knowledge check. See the curriculum and syllabus.

2

Pass the board examination

A proctored examination of 150 scored items (plus 15 unscored pilot items) over 3 hours, weighted by a published domain blueprint, with a criterion-referenced passing standard set by a modified-Angoff study. See the examination blueprint and sample items.

3

Pass the capstone portfolio

Eight work products resembling first-year industry outputs, from a product situation brief to an integrated evidence generation plan, assessed against published analytic rubrics. See the capstone portfolio specification.

Eligibility

Two routes, consistent with common credentialing practice for certifications of persons:

  • Credentialed route: a doctoral or professional clinical or scientific degree (for example MD, DO, MBBS, PharmD, PhD, DNP, NP, PA, BDS, DDS, DVM).
  • Experience route: a bachelor's degree plus two years of relevant professional experience in healthcare, life sciences, or a related field.

The examination and the portfolio, not the entry route, determine the credential.

How to use this site

Read the program handbook to understand the credential, then work the modules in order from the curriculum page. Each module page ends with a knowledge check and a button to mark the module complete; completion state is stored only in your browser, and the curriculum page can export it as a file. The case library, AI-use policy, and source register support the applied activities throughout.

Program status and integrity

This is a version 1.0 curriculum and certification design, prepared July 2026. It has not undergone job-task-analysis validation, item-bank piloting, formal standard setting, or accreditation review. It is designed for alignment with ISO/IEC 17024, the NCCA Standards for the Accreditation of Certification Programs, and the ANSI/IACET Standard, and it is not accredited by any body at this version. The accreditation and quality alignment page states precisely what is and is not claimed, and the roadmap to accreditation.

Every sourced teaching claim carries a hyperlinked citation to a verified authoritative source, consolidated in the source register. A market alignment analysis of about 75 real job postings across 39 companies and organizations documents how the curriculum maps to the responsibilities of the roles it prepares for.
