This register consolidates the verified sources cited in the curriculum, assessment, governance, and market-alignment content of the BCMAEP package. It is the canonical citation pool for that content: pages should cite from these sources (or from the archived source set in the research folder) and should not introduce unverified links. The design-research reports in website/design_research/ carry their own inline citations and are not duplicated here. Journal citations are verified via PubMed and carry a DOI; guidance and primary-source citations carry the official page URL.

Attribution note: journal references in sections B and C were verified using PubMed metadata; DOIs link to the article of record.

A. Standards and accreditation

  • ISO/IEC 17024:2012: general requirements for bodies operating certification of persons.
  • Institute for Credentialing Excellence, NCCA: NCCA Standards for the accreditation of certification programs (2021 revision, 23 standards).
  • IACET, ANSI/IACET 1-2018 Standard for Continuing Education and Training: continuing-education provider standard; one CEU equals ten contact hours.

B. Regulatory, HTA, and society primary sources (guidance, cases, manuals)

  • FDA, LEADER 3D rare-disease drug development program, with case PDFs: user guide, odevixibat, vutrisiran, tofersen, avalglucosidase alfa and seladelpar, fosdenopterin, olipudase alfa.
  • FDA, Complex Innovative Trial Design Meeting Program, with cases: alopecia areata external control, master protocol chronic pain.
  • FDA, Application of Model-Informed Drug Development in Study Design and Analysis.
  • FDA, Considerations for the Use of AI to Support Regulatory Decision-Making for Drug and Biological Products (draft, January 2025; comment period closed April 2025; remains a draft), Federal Register notice.
  • FDA, Guiding Principles of Good AI Practice in Drug Development (January 2026, ten principles issued jointly by FDA and EMA).
  • EMA, Reflection paper on the use of AI in the medicinal product lifecycle (EMA/CHMP/CVMP/83833/2023, first published September 2024; adopted as final).
  • NIH, Clinical Trial Definition Case Studies.
  • National Academies, NIH's role in drug development, workshop proceedings.
  • ICER, Guide to Understanding Health Technology Assessment.
  • NICE, health technology evaluations manual (PMG36).
  • iDSI, Health Technology Assessment Toolkit.
  • AMCP, Format for Formulary Submissions guidance; AMCP, pre-approval and post-approval clinical and economic evidence guidance.
  • PBAC, Guidelines for preparing a submission, version 5; PBAC, Public Summary Document, pembrolizumab early triple-negative breast cancer, 2023.
  • IQVIA, Medical Science Liaisons white paper.
  • MAPS/H1, autoimmune KOL intelligence case study; MAPS, Challenges to Opportunities workshop; MAPS/Amedea, innovation and metrics webinar; MAPS, launch-excellence masterclass brochure.
  • MAPS, Digital, Advanced Analytics and AI in Medical Affairs benchmark report, 2024.
  • ISPOR, HEOR impact-case abstract example; ISPOR, workshop proposal example.
  • EUPATI, discovery and development of medicines; EUPATI, guidance for patient involvement in industry-led medicines R&D.
  • ICMJE, Defining the Role of Authors and Contributors (recommendations, including AI provisions).

Compliance and legal frameworks (US and EU primary sources)

  • FDA, Office of Prescription Drug Promotion (OPDP): oversight of truthful, non-misleading, fair-balanced prescription drug promotion.
  • FDA, Responding to Unsolicited Requests for Off-Label Information About Prescription Drugs and Medical Devices: the nonpromotional scientific-response channel.
  • HHS Office of Inspector General, Fraud and Abuse Laws: Anti-Kickback Statute, False Claims Act, Stark Law, Exclusion, and Civil Monetary Penalties.
  • CMS, Open Payments (Physician Payments Sunshine Act): public reporting of manufacturer transfers of value.
  • PhRMA, Code on Interactions with Health Care Professionals: industry self-regulatory standards.
  • HHS, Health Information Privacy (HIPAA): US health-information privacy.
  • Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), EUR-Lex: EU data protection, including special-category health data.

Regulatory pathways, pharmacovigilance, and additional HTA primary sources

  • EMA, Marketing authorisation (overview): EU authorisation procedures, including accelerated assessment and conditional marketing authorisation.
  • EMA, Orphan designation: overview: EU designation for medicines for rare diseases.
  • ICH, Efficacy Guidelines (E2 pharmacovigilance series): international pharmacovigilance terminology and standards.
  • FDA, E2D(R1) Postapproval Safety Data: Definitions and Standards for Management and Reporting of Individual Case Safety Reports: individual case safety reporting definitions and standards.
  • FDA, Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies (REMS): US risk-management program requirements.
  • EMA, Good pharmacovigilance practices (GVP): EU pharmacovigilance modules, periodic reporting, risk-management plans, and the pharmacovigilance system.
  • Canada's Drug Agency, Reimbursement Reviews: Canadian HTA reimbursement review process and recommendations.
  • CMS, Inflation Reduction Act and Medicare (Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program): US Medicare drug price negotiation program.

E. Verification status and cautions

  • All journal DOIs in section C were verified via PubMed metadata on 2026-07-02.
  • Some official sources (for example fda.gov and iso.org) block automated fetching; where a page could not be fetched directly, the fact was corroborated through an authoritative alternative (the Federal Register notice for the FDA AI guidance; the ISO catalogue listing for ISO/IEC 17024) and is cited to a link that resolves.
  • Public availability of a source does not grant republication rights. In any delivered course, treat archived third-party materials as assigned-reading links and cite them; do not reproduce substantial third-party text, slides, or figures without permission.

Program status. Version 1.0 curriculum design, prepared July 2026. This program is a complete curriculum and certification design; it has not undergone job-task-analysis validation, item-bank piloting, formal standard setting, or accreditation review. It is designed for alignment with ISO/IEC 17024, the NCCA Standards, and the ANSI/IACET Standard, and is not accredited by any body at this version (see accreditation and quality alignment).

Accessibility. This site targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance: minimum 4.5:1 text contrast, keyboard-operable interactive components, visible focus indicators, and single-column reflow at narrow widths.

Sources. Teaching claims carry hyperlinked citations to verified authoritative sources; the consolidated pool is the source register. Cited third-party materials are assigned readings; public availability does not grant republication rights.
