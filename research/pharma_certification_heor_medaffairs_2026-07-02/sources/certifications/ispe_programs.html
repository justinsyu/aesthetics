Training Programs in Pediatric Pharmacoepidemiology, Pharmacology, Pharmacy and Related Areas

ISPE Pediatric SIG – Report on programs offering training in pediatric pharmacoepidemiology or related content area.

In 2013 and 2014, the ISPE Pediatric SIG conducted a needs assessment to identify critical needs in pediatric pharmacoepidemiology and directions for future activities. The preliminary results of the needs assessment were presented at the ISPE mid-year meeting in Rotterdam in April 2014. The programs identified are listed below.
