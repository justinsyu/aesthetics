The RPIF Program

The RPIF Program is an industry-based training program that partners with leading pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical companies.

It is designed for individuals with a Doctorate of Pharmacy or Medicine to develop their foundations as future leaders in the industry through work experience and professional development.
