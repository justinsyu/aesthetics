What is IMAUD?

IMAUD is a comprehensive online market access course tailored for healthcare, pharmaceutical, and life sciences professionals. You will gain essential skills to design effective market access strategies, navigate pricing and reimbursement in healthcare, and position products for success in competitive markets.

If you’re asking how to become a market access specialist, IMAUD provides the tools, frameworks, and expert mentorship you need. Whether you need pharmaceutical market access training or seek to deepen your expertise, this course equips you to excel.

Programme content

Module 1: Market Access Fundamentals (4 weeks)

Explore the full market access journey from clinical trial design to early access strategies.
Master evidence generation, pricing research, negotiation, and lifecycle management.
Includes practical workshops like Alzheimer’s Disease case study and real-world applications.

Module 2: Global and Regional Market Access Strategies (2 weeks)

Analyze market access frameworks across key countries including US, EU, China, and more.
Understand regional variations, EU HTA processes, and global pricing strategies.
Features interactive workshops and panel discussions for international comparison.

Module 3: Special Topics and Future Directions (2 weeks)

Delve into AI, real-world evidence, and innovative pricing and funding models.
Focus on vaccine market access, MedTech pathways, and challenges in low-resource settings.
Engage in workshops covering Rotavirus and MedTech, and explore future market access trends.

FAQ after

What to do if you don't see any email from us in your inbox?

  • Check your SPAM, Promotions, Notifications, or other email folders – sometimes messages end up there automatically.

  • Search your mailbox for the word “IMAUD” or MAS.

  • Make sure you provided the correct email address during registration.

  • If you still haven’t received any message within 24 hours, please contact us at [support email address] – we’ll help you regain access.

What to do if, after logging in, you don't have access to the IMAUD course (lock icon)?

  • Make sure you are logging in with the same account used to purchase the course.

  • Refresh the page or log out and back in again.

  • If you still don’t see the course, contact us with the email address used for login and include a screenshot of your course dashboard – this will help us resolve the issue faster.

What to do if you don’t know how to navigate the course?

  • Check the platform tutorial – follow it step by step.

  • If you still have problems, write to our support team.

  • Remember: some course content unlocks only after completing previous modules.
