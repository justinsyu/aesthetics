    BCMAS® The Badge of Excellence in Medical Affairs.

    Ranked #1 by Pharma Industry Insiders

    The Board Certified Medical Affairs Specialist (BCMAS®) certification is the most comprehensive Medical Affairs & Medical Science Liaison professional development and training program in the world.

    Achieving the BCMAS demonstrates the highest standards of knowledge and excellence in the Medical Affairs profession. It also sets the stage for continual professional development through values centered on lifelong learning.

    As a BCMAS certified professional, you must meet specified requirements in order to maintain and renew your certification every (1) year. Maintaining your certification is important as a professional.

    Warning

    BCMAS certifications that are not maintained every year will be suspended

    Requirements for renewing your BCMAS certification:

    Upon successfully completing the BCMAS program, your board certification is valid for one year. To maintain your active status and credential, you must recertify annually.

    Purchase your recertification from this page using the same email address you originally used for the BCMAS program.

    After your purchase, complete any 2 approved learning activities in the LMS. You may choose 2 recertification courses, 2 webinar recordings, or 1 course and 1 webinar recording.

    Once you complete your 2 selected activities, the final attestation module will unlock.

    After you review and confirm the attestation, your recertification for the year will be complete.

    Your updated certification certificate will then be available for download, and your next recertification will be due one year after you complete the attestation.

    The cost for recertification is $99 USD. BCMAS Recertification is a separate product from the full BCMAS certification program ($2,399 USD for new candidates). The former $599 recertification price no longer applies.

    You may pay for BCMAS Recertification online by credit or debit card on this page after confirming eligibility with the same email address you used for your original BCMAS program.

    You must choose at least 2 activites from the list below to maintain your certification.

    Course: Patient Reported Outcomes

    Course: Effective Communication

    Course : Speaker Program

    Course : Drug Literature Evaluation

    Podcast : Accol! Open Sesame! Exceptions! The Sometimes Overlooked Path to Approval. (It's Not Magic)

    Podcast : Why Do Drug Launches Fail?

    Podcast : Patient Journey & the Importance of Health Literacy with Irma Lucero

    Podcast : Matrix Teams and How to Efficiently Measure Performance in Reimbursement Teams with Steve Fields

    Course : Buy & Bill

    Course : Copay Maximizers & Accumulators
