The AI for Medical Affairs Course

With James Turnbull & Jess Blackwell

Course Details

THE CONTENT

AI is transforming how Medical Affairs teams work by enhancing insight generation, content creation, and decision support. Yet with new tools and applications emerging rapidly, uncertainty remains about where to focus, how to ensure compliance, and how to create real value.

This course provides a clear and up-to-date overview of where and how AI can add value across Medical Affairs activities. You will learn how to identify, assess, and prioritise AI opportunities, design focused pilots, and apply practical tools to enhance productivity and efficiency. The programme combines strategic frameworks with hands-on exercises and real-world examples.

To harness AI’s full potential and strengthen the strategic and operational impact of Medical Affairs, this course is a must-attend!
 

This was a great course that helped me use AI much more in my daily Medical Affairs routine.

Menarini

Mike Zuurman, Global Hematology Medical Lead - The Netherlands - October 2025


THE EXPERTS

This course is given by James Turnbull or Jess Blackwell, highly experienced leaders in AI-enabled medical communications and omnichannel strategy:

  • James Turnbull is a recognised pioneer in applying artificial intelligence to Medical Affairs. As the Founder of Camino, an innovative medical communications agency, he has led the development of AI-enabled solutions for pharma and spoken at leading industry conferences including ISMPP, MAPS and PING. With over fifteen years in leadership roles, James has helped healthcare brands grow through innovative approaches and has earned multiple industry awards.

  • Jess Blackwell, Executive Director and Co-Founder of Camino Communications, brings more than 17 years of experience in healthcare communications. She has led integrated programmes for companies of all sizes, with deep expertise in omnichannel strategy, adult learning theory, and the design of digital learning platforms and impactful communication initiatives. Jess is a regular speaker on innovation and generative AI in pharma and is the creator of the sold-out conference Adventures in Pharma.

  • James and Jess regularly train Medical Affairs professionals and are praised for their clarity, real-world expertise, and highly engaging training style. 
     

To get a flavour of Jess and James’ expertise and teaching style, watch this recent educational webinar: 


THE AUDIENCE & INTERACTIVE LEARNING FORMAT

This course is ideal for Medical Affairs professionals at any level who want to better understand how to apply AI in their daily work.

This hands-on course combines expert-led sessions, interactive case discussions, group exercises, and peer learning. You will leave with practical skills and insights that will help you take the next steps toward using AI effectively in your Medical Affairs role.
 

THAT WAS EXCELLENT!

Ferring

Milos Kalina, Head of Medical Affairs - Central & Eastern Europe - March 2025

  1. Understanding AI in a broader pharma context: Terminology,  applications, and challenges.

  2. How to effectively use Generative AI for day-to-day tasks, medical slide deck creation, medico-marketing materials, and more.

  3. Learning from hands-on exercises and real-life use cases in Medical Affairs.

  4. Using a planning framework for assessing, prioritising, and prototyping AI projects.

  5. Addressing ethical, privacy, and regulatory considerations for AI use in pharma.

  6. Emerging AI technologies and future applications that will affect Medical Affairs.

Day 1: Foundations and Practical Applications of Generative AI in Medical Affairs
10:00Welcome & General Introduction
10:30Understanding Artificial Intelligence (AI) - Overview of Applications in Pharma, Biotech & MedTech
 
  • Defining the world of AI: Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Generative AI (GenAI) and Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • Current examples of AI applications across R&D, clinical, and commercial functions
  • Classification of GenAI applications: personal use – developmental projects – enterprise solutions
  • Introduction to reasoning models, agents and multimodal capabilities
11:30Using ChatGPT, Copilot, etc. for Day-to-day Tasks
 
  • Practical examples of using GenAI chatbots in day-to-day tasks (ChatGPT, Copilot and Claude)
  • Tips & tricks for optimal performance, such as:
    • Creating and clearing the context window
    • How to prompt for effective results
  • Exercise: Medical Slide Deck Creation
    Working in small groups, participants explore how language GenAI tools can assist in generating a medical slide deck from summarising clinical data to drafting key messages and creating slides. Findings will be discussed in plenary.
12:30Lunch Break
13:30Practical AI Use Cases for Medical Affairs
 
  • Guided examples of real Medical Affairs use cases: Ad board outputs, literature review, competitor intelligence, PAG collaboration, etc!
  • Case study: congress coverage and content generation with AI
14:00Exercise: AI-Powered Literature Review & Data Analysis
 
  • Participants practise using AI on their own computer or preferred platform to organise, filter, and analyse literature data in a guided exercise. 
15:00Case study: Using AI to Prioritise Effort Through Data Analysis
 
  • A guided case study on the use of AI to analyse omnichannel data and engagement metrics, helping identify what works, what doesn’t, and where to focus effort.
15:45Ethical Guidelines and Challenges in Pharma
 
  • Ethical guidelines for GenAI usage in pharma companies
  • Addressing challenges: Bias, data privacy and model updates
16:45Planning Guidelines for Using GenAI in Medical Affairs
 
  • Key success factors: Data quality and strategic alignment
  • Developing a strategic roadmap for AI implementation
  • Criteria for prioritising projects 
17:00Workshop: Assessing and Prioritising Medical Affairs AI Projects Based on Efficiency, Value, and Innovation
 

Participants will use a planning framework, which evaluates AI projects based on their ability to deliver efficiency, value, and innovation. Working in small groups, they will:

  • Identify a potential use case for GenAI in their organisation (e.g., automating content creation, improving HCP engagement).
  • Assess the project idea using the framework to determine whether it meets the criteria for a good AI project.
  • Use GenAI tools (e.g., GPT or Claude) to test and refine ideas, generating brainstorming outputs or exploring alternative approaches.

By the end of the session, participants will have a prioritised use case and initial ideas for how AI could be applied, setting the stage for more detailed planning in Part 2.

17:30Close of Day 1

 

Day 2: Strategy, Projects, and Future Trends
09:00Recap and Reflections from Day 1
09:15Image, Video, and Audio Generation for Medico-Marketing
 
  • Demonstrations of multimodal AI tools (Claude, Midjourney, Runway, Synthesia, etc.)
  • Practical use case: AI learning assistant
10:15Workshop: Refining and Prototyping of Medical Affairs AI Projects Using Multi-modal AI Tools
 

Participants will use a structured planning framework to refine and develop GenAI project ideas that deliver efficiency, value, and innovation. In small groups, they will:

  • Refine project concepts: Build on ideas from Day 1, considering key factors such as stakeholder needs, business impact, and alignment with strategic goals.
  • Prototype outputs: Use multi-modal AI tools (e.g., MidJourney for images, Runway for video, ElevenLabs for voice synthesis) to create prototypes that illustrate their project concepts.
  • Prepare and deliver presentations: Each group will present their refined project plans and prototypes to the wider group, showcasing: 
    • The problem their project addresses.
    • How GenAI will deliver a solution.
    • A demonstration of their prototypes, created with AI. 
12:30Lunch Break
13:15Future Trends for AI in Medical Affairs
 
  • What’s coming up in AI: reasoning, agents, multimodal and enterprise adoption
13:45Workshop: Automating Tasks with AI
 

Participants will work in small groups and:

  • Learn how AI can analyse and structure data to extract hidden insights
  • Use online AI tools to create automations for routine tasks and workflows
  • Explore how AI can generate practical outputs such as spreadsheets, analyses, and simple scripts, and discuss how these can be applied in day-to-day roles
15:30Final Q&A and Closing Remarks
16:00End of Course
