The Medical Affairs Excellence Course

With Chris Toller

Course Details

THE CONTENT

Medical Affairs has become a strategically essential function for the success of pharmaceutical products. Professionals across all levels in Medical Affairs are increasingly expected to contribute to strategic decision-making and to collaborate effectively in cross-functional teams.

This course provides a practical and comprehensive foundation for building business acumen and understanding the key functions that shape product strategy and planning such as Market Access, Marketing, HEOR, and RWE. 

Built around the six Domains of Medical Affairs Excellence, this course offers concrete concepts, tools, and techniques to strengthen your strategic thinking, improve collaboration, and increase your organisational impact. Whether you are early in your Medical Affairs career or preparing for a more senior role, you will leave with insights you can apply immediately in your daily work.

To strengthen your strategic mindset and enhance your influence across the organisation as a medical affairs professionial, this course is a must-attend!

THE EXPERT

Chris Toller is a globally recognised consultant in pharmaceutical Medical Affairs and the Managing Partner at Havas Life Medicom UK.

  • A trained physician, Chris has spent his career supporting Medical Affairs teams across nearly every therapeutic area and global region. He has developed strategies and delivered programmes for more than 50 medical brands, working with many of the world’s leading pharmaceutical companies.

THE AUDIENCE & INTERACTIVE LEARNING FORMAT

As the impact of Medical Affairs on the commercial success of new pharmaceuticals continues to grow, all Medical Affairs professionals can benefit from attending this course.

It is particularly suitable for:

  • Medical Affairs managers involved in preparing the market for a new pharmaceutical during the pre-launch phase

  • Individuals who are new to Medical Affairs and looking to build a strong understanding of their function's strategic role

Through a highly interactive format that blends expert instruction, case-based discussions, group exercises, and peer-to-peer learning, you’ll leave this course inspired and equipped to boost your effectiveness in any Medical Affairs setting.

Day 1
Welcome & Introduction (~15 min)
The Evolving Role of Medical Affairs (~1 h 15 min)
  • Strategic medical affairs in a changing pharma landscape
  • Implications: more science – more stakeholders – more data sources – more scrutiny – more demands
  • Defining our scope: the 6 domains of medical affairs excellence
Drive Medical Insight (~1 h 30 min)
  • Components and sources of medical insight
  • Group exercise: gaining & using insight
  • Insight mapping from the patient journey
  • Medical insight supporting brand positioning
Lunch Break
Own the Data (~45 min)
  • Building a scientific platform
  • Identifying data gaps
  • Scientific publications and congress planning
  • Characteristics of Big Data and RWE
Communicate and Educate (~2 h 15 min)
  • Early brand lexicon
  • Scientific messaging and narrative
  • Digital and multichannel communications
  • Efficient content review
  • Building a medical communications strategy

 

Day 2
Engage with Stakeholders (~45 min)
  • Patients and patient centricity
    • What does patient centricity really mean and what are the benefits of a patient first approach
    • Examples of patient centric strategies
  • Cognitive bias: What is it and why is it important in the context of medical education and HCP communications
Group Exercise: Scientific Communications (~1 h 30 min)
  • Each team will be provided with clinical and market data related to a recently launched product. On that basis, the teams will need to prepare key points of a medical communications strategy, which they present and defend with the group
Support the Value Proposition (~1 h 30 min)
  • Basics of pharmacoeconomics
  • The concept of incremental value
  • Cost-benefit and cost-effectiveness
  • Cost-utility and the concept of QALYs
  • Basis of choice – understanding ICERs
Lunch Break
Lead and Manage the Medical Affairs Function (~2 h)
  • Demonstrating value of medical affairs
  • Group exercise: towards better KPIs
  • Skills, competences and sources of power
  • Structure and culture within medical affairs
  • Group exercise: defining a vision
Course review and Summary (~15 min)

Group Exercise: Scientific Communications (~1 h 30 min)
  • Each team will be provided with clinical and market data related to a recently launched product. On that basis, the teams will need to prepare key points of a medical communications strategy, which they present and defend with the group
Support the Value Proposition (~1 h 30 min)
  • Basics of pharmacoeconomics
  • The concept of incremental value
  • Cost-benefit and cost-effectiveness
  • Cost-utility and the concept of QALYs
  • Basis of choice – understanding ICERs
Lunch Break
Lead and Manage the Medical Affairs Function (~2 h)
  • Demonstrating value of medical affairs
  • Group exercise: towards better KPIs
  • Skills, competences and sources of power
  • Structure and culture within medical affairs
  • Group exercise: defining a vision
Course review and Summary (~15 min)
