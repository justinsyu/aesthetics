Certificate Programs

Unlock Your Regulatory Affairs Potential with RAPS Certificate Programs

Advance your career and enhance your professional skills with a diverse range of RAPS certificates. Whether you are looking to build foundational regulatory affairs knowledge, strengthen your communication abilities, or gain insights from world-renowned institutions, our curated certificate offerings are designed to meet your goals.

Online Certificate Programs

Develop a robust foundation in regulatory affairs through a fully online, self-paced learning experience. These well-recognized certificates are created by regulatory experts and are ideal for those who are new or looking to start a career in regulatory. Each certificate includes core courses and offers 40 electives to choose from, so you can customize your learning to fit your needs.

Elevate your certificate program by completing a Foundation in Regulatory Affairs (FRA) exam, which is included. Earning your FRA demonstrates your newly acquired knowledge to employers.

Regulatory Communication Certificate Programs

Certificate in Effective Communication

Enhance your communication fundamentals through this flexible e-learning certificate that focuses on essential skills such as active listening, clear messaging, and impactful presentation, building confidence for regulatory professionals at any career stage.

Advanced Strategic Communication Certificate Program

Master advanced skills in regulatory communications with a focus on strategy, storytelling, and executive presence. This program combines live virtual sessions and practical exercises, empowering regulatory professionals to drive influence, manage crises, and communicate with clarity at senior levels.

 

Leadership Programs

eCornell Regulatory Leadership Certificate

This program blends leadership and business courses designed by Cornell faculty with an interactive online experience you can take from anywhere. Learn valuable leadership lessons and network with other regulatory leaders, then attend a live capstone session that ties everything together.

Kellogg Executive Development Program

Learn business and leadership lessons from faculty at one of the world’s most prestigious business schools during this 3.5-day program. Held at Northwestern’s Kellogg School of Management, this program has prepared regulatory leaders to speak the language of business for more than 25 years.

 

Which Certificate Is Right for You?

  • New to regulatory affairs → Pharmaceuticals or Medical Devices Certificate
  • Working across product categories → Dual Certificate: Medical Devices & Pharmaceuticals
  • Preparing for leadership roles → RAPS eCornell Regulatory Leadership Certificate
  • Building business and leadership acumen → Executive Development Program at Kellogg
  • Improving communication skills → RAPS Certificate in Effective Communication
  • Enhancing strategic, executive-level communication → Advanced Strategic Communication Certificate Program
