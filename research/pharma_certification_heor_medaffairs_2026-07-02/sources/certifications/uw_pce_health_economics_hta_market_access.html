Certificate in Health Economics, Health Technology Assessment & Market Access

Transform Health Care Decision-Making

About This Program

This program was previously called the Certificate in Health Economics and Outcomes Research (HEOR).

With constant innovations in health technology, the ability to evaluate a new intervention’s value — its effectiveness, impact on patients and holistic cost — is essential.

Whether you work in industry, government, academia, clinical practice or consulting, this certificate prepares you to navigate the interplay between clinical evidence, cost-effectiveness, value assessment, and market access and pricing strategies. Gain the conceptual grounding and practical tools to support high-quality, organizational decision-making to contribute meaningfully to the evolving value-based health care landscape.

Designed For

Professionals in the health care, biomedical technology or life sciences industries who want to be better prepared to evaluate new health technologies and inform resource allocation, coverage and policy decisions.

Learn More

This program is offered by The CHOICE Institute at the UW School of Pharmacy. For more information and to apply, visit the Certificate in Health Economics, Health Technology Assessment & Market Access page.

Approved by the UW Comparative Health Outcomes, Policy, and Economics (CHOICE) Institute.
