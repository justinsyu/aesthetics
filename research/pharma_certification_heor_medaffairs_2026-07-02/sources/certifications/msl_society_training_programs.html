MSL SOCIETY TRAINING PROGRAMS

All our training programs are developed using a variety of current best practices employed by successful MSL teams. Our insights are gathered from original, current research and real-world experience from those that have hands-on experience in managing and training Medical Science Liaisons (MSLs) as well as unique insights from top performing MSL teams.

For all relevant training programs, each participant will receive comprehensive workbooks and/or templates that highlights the content covered throughout the training as well as additional activities that can be utilized for continued practice and learning beyond the course.

To ensure your teams needs are met, we offer 3 different levels of training customization detailed below:

Semi-Customized Trainings

With our semi-customized training programs, you have the flexibility to select which of our most popular training programs fits your team’s professional development needs. We then tailor the case studies and format to ensure the existing content targets your unique team and their current skill set and address their professional needs.

Fully-Customized Trainings

In our fully-customized training programs, we tailor the curriculum from scratch, to meet your specific requirements. This ensures that both format and content align precisely with your team’s current skill set and professional development needs.

MSL TRAINING PROGRAMS: KEY DIFFERENCES

Type of Content

Fully Customized Trainings

  • Entirely custom content based on company’s needs.
  • All content is specific to company’s TA, products, and culture.
  • Unique content developed for each training program.

Semi-Customized

  • Limited custom content with standardized content.
  • Selected activities are specific to company’s TA, products, and culture.
  • Mix of targeted unique content and general MSL skills.

Open-Enrollment

  • Standardized content applicable to a wide audience.
  • General MSL skills and knowledge.
  • General content applicable to a wide array of companies or products.

Flexibility

Fully Customized Trainings

  • High flexibility: Entire program is adaptable to company’s specific needs and preferences.
  • Ability to fully incorporate unique case studies, policies, and practices.

Semi-Customized

  • Moderate flexibility: Allows for customization of select activities.
  • Limited ability to incorporate unique case studies, policies, and practices.

Open-Enrollment

  • Low flexibility: Fixed curriculum designed for broad applicability.
  • No customization for individual or company-specific needs.

Budget

Fully Customized Trainings

  • Higher, due to bespoke development.

Semi-Customized

  • Moderate, depending on the level of customization.

Open-Enrollment

  • Lower, due to standardized content.

Duration/Format

Fully Customized Trainings

  • Flexible duration: Can be designed to fit specific schedules.
  • Flexible format: Options for in-person, virtual, or hybrid.

Semi-Customized

  • Flexible duration: Can be designed to fit specific schedules.
  • Flexible format: Options for in-person, virtual, or hybrid.

Open-Enrollment

  • Fixed duration: Determined by the specific program.
  • Various formats: Determined by the specific program.

Scalability

Fully Customized Trainings

  • Moderate scalability: Can accommodate large groups (limited by number/types of group activities).

Semi-Customized

  • Moderate scalability: Can accommodate large groups (limited by number/types of group activities).

Open-Enrollment

  • High scalability: Easy to accommodate large groups.

Post-Training Support

Fully Customized Trainings

  • Highly supportive: Multiple bespoke resources can include follow-ups, debriefs, mentoring, workbooks, etc.

Semi-Customized

  • Moderately supportive: Limited bespoke resources can include follow-ups, debriefs, mentoring, workbooks, etc.

Open-Enrollment

  • Fixed duration: Determined by the specific program.
  • Various formats: Determined by the specific program.

SEMI-CUSTOMIZED TRAINING

For semi-customized training programs, you get to select from our most successful and relevant training topics and curricula, while retaining some flexibility for customization. The content will incorporate case studies tailored to your company and team’s background, responsibilities, and existing skill set. Additionally, our semi-customized training programs offer the versatility of being Live In-Person or Virtual Instructor-Led.

CUSTOMIZATION OPTIONS

Audience

We can tailor trainings according to your team’s experience, responsibilities, and current skill set. All training programs can be adapted to benefit all types of MSL professionals, from newly hired MSLs to Executive Management.

Format & Duration

This training can be conducted either as Live In-Person or Virtual Instructor-Led. All training programs can be adapted to last between a few hours to 2 days.

Scenario-Based Activities

For all applicable scenario activities/case studies used during the training, we will use relevant examples from your company.

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

We recognize that every MSL team is different and has unique training needs, so we always collaborate closely with you to ensure we meet your team’s budget and professional needs. For all our fully-customized and semi-customized training programs, here’s what you can expect:

Initial Consultation

We begin with scheduling a call to understand your goals and learn more about your team’s size, current skill set, schedule, budget, and customization preferences.

Tailored Proposal

Following our initial consultation, we will create a customized proposal for your team, which we will then send over for your review. We will be happy to schedule another call to fine-tune the details of the contract to ensure agreement.

Contract and Preparation

Once the contract is signed by both parties, and the training is scheduled, we prepare for the upcoming training. Your team will receive relevant materials in advance to aid in preparation.

Training Session

During the training, we ensure an interactive learning environment for your team. The session, content, duration and materials will all be based on your needs agreed upon in the contract.

Post Training

After the training, we conduct an evaluation with you and provide any additional relevant materials to support ongoing development.
Our commitment extends beyond the training session, ensuring a tailored and effective learning experience for your team. We are ready to help you with your training needs!

Contact Heliana Sula, Chief Operating Officer, at heliana.sula@themsls.org to schedule a call to discuss details.
