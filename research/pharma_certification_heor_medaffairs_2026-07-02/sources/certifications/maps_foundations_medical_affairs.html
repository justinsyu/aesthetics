Introducing the Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program

Here at MAPS, we understand the necessity of ongoing professional development, and the Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program stands as a decisive step for individuals determined to enhance their skills and boost their impact within the field.

The Foundations program, is an essential component of the MAPS eCademy Learning Journey, specifically designed to elevate your understanding of critical concepts and practices in Medical Affairs.

Developed in collaboration with over 60 industry experts from leading pharmaceutical and MedTech organizations globally, this program is grounded in real-world experience and insights, encompassing 42 key Competencies across seven critical Domains that address the multifaceted challenges faced in today’s Medical Affairs landscape. 

Best of all, the program is fully customizable! You choose the Domains and Competencies that align with your needs. Take the full program, focus on a specific Domain, or select individual 15-minute microsessions on your schedule. Your learning, your way. 

MAPS eCademy Learning Journey – Where Foundations Aligns

Take a Glimpse at the Details

What is eCademy?

Foundations is a component of the MAPS eCademy Learning Journey, designed to provide you with a deeper understanding of essential concepts and practices within Medical Affairs. This program empowers you with the knowledge and skills necessary for success in this dynamic industry through a range of learning options, including introductory, foundational, and advanced courses. 

Medical Affairs Primer (Introductory)

Are you new to Medical Affairs and looking for a convenient overview of the field? 

As part of the MAPS eCademy Learning Journey, this comprehensive course is designed to help you unlock your potential in Medical Affairs. It covers essential concepts such as the roles and responsibilities within the field, key regulatory frameworks, and effective communication strategies with healthcare professionals. You will gain valuable insights from an industry expert, enhancing your understanding of Medical Affairs and providing you with the necessary knowledge and skills to start your career. 

Revised and updated for 2025, the course is free, self-paced, and can be completed in one hour. 

Why the Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program is essential

Foundations delivers comprehensive, essential knowledge across all MAPS Medical Affairs Competency Framework Domains. Participants will engage with a structured curriculum that covers the Competencies necessary for success in Medical Affairs. Each Domain is crafted to enable participants to understand the theories, best practices, and real-world applications that define effective medical communication and strategy. 

The MAPS Medical Affairs Competency Framework is crucial for professional growth and success in Medical Affairs. The Foundations program offers more than just essential learning, it provides a structured and impactful development experience designed to: 

  • Strengthen your ability to communicate scientific data effectively. 
  • Enhance decision making through evidence-based strategies. 
  • Deepen your understanding of regulatory, compliance, and business landscapes. 
  • Improve collaboration across functions and engagement with stakeholders. 

By the end of the program, participants will have a solid understanding of essential Competencies, along with the confidence and knowledge needed to contribute more effectively to their teams. 

What is the format of the Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program?

  • Seven Distinct Domains – Spans the full scope of Medical Affairs Competencies.    
  • 42 Courses – Each lasting approximately 15 minutes for convenient, flexible learning.   
  • Expert Moderators – Each Domain is guided by an industry leader, offering unique insights and perspectives.    
  • Convenient Learning – Hosted on a dedicated Learning Management System (MAPS eCademy) for seamless access, the program is entirely online and self-paced, allowing professionals to advance on their own schedule.
  • Interactive and Engaging – No skipping ahead; learners must actively participate to progress.
  • Fully Customizable – Take individual microsessions, select specific Domains, or complete the full program. You decide what’s right for your career development.  

Who Should Enroll?

The Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program is ideal for: 

  • Early-career professionals seeking a solid foundation in Medical Affairs 
  • Individuals transitioning into Medical Affairs from other functions (e.g., clinical, commercial, regulatory) 
  • Employers, executives, and MA leaders looking to onboard new Medical Affairs team members 
  • Medical Affairs professionals eager to enhance their expertise and stay at the forefront of the field by integrating the most current information and cutting-edge technologies 
  • Busy professionals who want to target specific Competency gaps with individual microsessions rather than committing to a full program upfront 

Enrollment Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites for enrolling in the Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program. This open accessibility ensures that anyone with some background knowledge in Medical Affairs can participate and benefit from the program. 

Is there an assessment at the end of each competency?

Participants will take multiple-choice questions after completing each Competency within a Domain to guarantee an understanding of the concepts covered. If a question is answered incorrectly, participants must retake it until they achieve the correct answer, ensuring a solid understanding of each Competency. 

Upon completion of all six Competency courses in a Domain, participants will receive a Certificate of Completion for that Domain (you will not receive a Certificate of Completion after each 15-minute microsession).

The Seven Domains of the MAPS Medical Affairs Competency Framework

Below, you will discover the seven essential Domains and the powerful skills you will accrue as you complete all included competencies. Choose the Domains most relevant to your role, or explore them all:

Take a Glimpse at the Details

Strategy Domain

The Strategy Domain is crucial for articulating the vision, value, and impact of Medical Affairs. This area of expertise reinforces essential capabilities, including the development of robust medical strategic and launch plans, the identification of actionable insights, and the execution of initiatives with operational excellence. By mastering these skills, you’ll be equipped to drive impactful and transformative results in the industry.

Scientific and Technical Knowledge Domain

The Scientific and Technical Knowledge Domain provides essential insights into drug development, clinical trial design, and the key statistical and epidemiological concepts relevant to both pre-approval and post-approval phases. It highlights the strategic role of the Target Product Profile (TPP) and underscores the importance of regulatory agencies in ensuring drug safety and quality. By developing these competencies, you will be better prepared to analyze data effectively, interpret scientific literature, and support the safe and informed use of pharmaceuticals.

Business Knowledge Domain

The Business Knowledge Domain focuses on understanding the financial and strategic frameworks that drive the pharmaceutical and healthcare industries. Professionals will learn to analyze key financial goals, evaluate healthcare systems, and understand reimbursement landscapes. By developing these skills, you will build a solid foundation in business principles, allowing you to navigate complex market dynamics with confidence and strategic insight. 

Evidence Generation Domain

The Evidence Generation Domain is vital for ensuring that actions, decisions, and policies are based on credible evidence, leading to effective, efficient, and equitable healthcare outcomes. Key competencies include integrated evidence planning, identifying data gaps, and utilizing real-world evidence alongside Health Economics and Outcomes Research. Prioritizing innovative evidence generation techniques and health equity is essential for advancing accessible healthcare for all.

Customer Engagement and Scientific Communication Domain

The Customer Engagement and Scientific Communication Domain is essential for developing effective communication strategies, including scientific publications, medical education, digital engagement, and interactions with external stakeholders. By promoting meaningful scientific exchanges with healthcare professionals, key opinion leaders, and patients, Medical Affairs professionals ensure that accurate, evidence-based information reaches the appropriate audiences. By mastering these skills, you will help improve patient outcomes through informed decision-making and enhanced scientific dialogue. 

Leadership and Management Domain

The Leadership and Management Domain plays a vital role in impacting healthcare. It focuses on mastering diverse leadership models and effective communication within matrix teams, collaborating with key stakeholders like healthcare professionals, caregivers, and advocacy groups. By nurturing talent, creating succession plans, and navigating challenges with agility, Medical Affairs professionals can significantly drive healthcare advancement and achieve meaningful results.

Medical Governance and Compliance Domain

The Medical Governance and Compliance Domain is pivotal for effectively interpreting and applying regulations and industry codes of practice. These competencies encompass ethical considerations, risk mitigation strategies, and robust control frameworks. By prioritizing these elements, we strengthen our partnerships with healthcare professionals, patients, and other stakeholders to actively pursue innovative, patient-centered business opportunities in a dynamic and rapidly evolving environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Program Overview

What is the Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program?

The Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program is an online, self-paced Medical Affairs training experience designed to provide early Medical Affairs professionals and those new to the field with the core knowledge and competencies essential for success in the field.

Does the Foundations program offer CEU credits?

The Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program is not accredited by a third party, and therefore, no Continuing Education Credits are offered.

Can I take individual courses instead of the full program?

Yes! Foundations is fully customizable. You can purchase and complete individual 15-minute Competency microsessions, focus on specific Domains, or enroll in the complete program. This flexibility allows you to target the competencies most relevant to your role and schedule.

What needs does the Foundations program address within the Medical Affairs community?

The Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program is designed to establish a global standard of foundational knowledge for professionals in Medical Affairs. As the field continues to evolve in scope, complexity, and strategic significance, there is a growing demand for comprehensive training. This Medical Affairs training provides professionals with the skills and understanding necessary for success in cross-functional, evidence-driven environments. 

How was the Foundations program created, and what learning methodologies does it utilize?

The Foundations in Medical Affairs Certificate Program is founded on the MAPS Competency Framework, which includes 42 key Competencies across seven critical Domains. These Competencies are designed to address the complex challenges present in today’s Medical Affairs landscape. The program was developed in collaboration with over 60 industry experts from leading pharmaceutical, MedTech, and partner organizations around the world and is based on real-world experience and insights.

Curriculum & Learning Experience

What topics are covered in the program?

The program covers seven Domain courses that span the full scope of Medical Affairs competencies: 

  • Strategy 
  • Scientific and Technical Knowledge 
  • Business Knowledge 
  • Evidence Generation 
  • Customer Engagement and Scientific Communication 
  • Leadership and Management 
  • Medical Governance and Compliance

How long does the program take to complete?

The Foundations program is designed to be self-paced, enabling learners to move through the material at their own speed and revisit any modules as needed. On average, the full program can be completed in about ten hours. It consists of seven Domain courses, each containing six Competency Modules. Each module takes approximately 15 minutes to complete—perfect for fitting learning into a busy schedule.

Is there an exam or assessment?

Yes, there is a final knowledge check after each module to assess your understanding of the course material. You must complete the knowledge checks before being able to move on to the next Competency module.

Yes. The program is available globally and accessible online 24/7. All content is in English.

Technical & Support Questions

What platform is the program hosted on?

The Medical Affairs training is hosted on a user-friendly, mobile-responsive Learning Management System that allows you to track progress, complete interactive modules, and access materials anytime. 

What are the technical requirements?

No special software is required, but you will need: 

  • A reliable internet connection 
  • A computer or mobile device 
  • A modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge) 

Is my personal data and completion metrics private?

We prioritize the privacy and security of our users’ data. We want to assure you that your personal information will remain confidential and will not be sold or shared with any third parties. Your trust is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your data while you focus on your learning experience. 

Who can I contact if I experience issues accessing the course?

Please contact Support on the Learning Management System for technical assistance or course access issues.
