Key outcomes

Health outcomes research is used to develop patient-centered best practices, practice guidelines and cost-effective therapeutic and behavioral interventions, improve healthcare system performance and inform policy. It is not restricted to just medicine or health services but encompasses a broad range of health measurements. It requires scientific knowledge and data-driven insights for informed decision-making.

The Certificate in Applied Health Outcomes Research may be completed 100% online in one year. The program provides an opportunity to learn:

  • Fundamental elements of outcomes research with real‐world data and evidence.
  • Basic knowledge and understanding of evidence-based decision-making, including:
    • Assessment of clinical and patient‐reported outcomes and patient satisfaction
    • The impact of non‐clinical patient‐level factors on patient health
    • The role of economic evaluation methods in assessing the cost effectiveness of pharmacological and non‐pharmacological treatments
    • The intended and unintended consequences of health policy decisions

Salary and Career Outlook

Earning a health economics and outcomes certificate online can expand your career opportunities in both the public and private sectors. With the growing emphasis on value-based care, professionals trained in HEOR are in high demand across employers such as pharmaceutical companies, health systems, consulting firms and government agencies. Job growth in HEOR-related roles is expected to remain strong as healthcare organizations increasingly rely on data to guide policy and spending decisions.

Graduates are qualified for roles such as:

  • HEOR project coordinator
  • Outcomes evaluation specialist
  • Real-world evidence manager

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics includes many of the jobs related to applied health outcomes research in its medical scientist category. Basically, this classification includes scientists who primarily conduct research in order to understand and improve health outcomes. U.S. BLS reports a national median annual wage of $100,590 for medical scientists. Industry salaries vary, and additional estimates include $67,280 at universities, $95,140 at medical and diagnostic laboratories and $121,240 in research and development.

In conclusion, this certificate positions you for career advancement in one of the fastest-growing areas of healthcare, helping you stand out in a competitive job market.

Applied health outcomes research is a growing field that evaluates the real-world impact of healthcare treatments, policies and services. It focuses on measuring how interventions affect clinical outcomes, economic costs and patient quality of life. This is often referred to as economic, clinical and humanistic outcomes.

By combining methods from epidemiology and economics, applied health outcomes research helps decision-makers determine the most effective and efficient use of healthcare resources.

Core focus areas of applied health outcomes research

  • Clinical outcomes: Measures the effectiveness of treatments in real-world settings
  • Economic outcomes: Assesses cost-effectiveness and value for money
  • Humanistic outcomes: Evaluates quality of life, patient satisfaction and adherence
  • Comparative effectiveness analysis: Compares different healthcare interventions to determine which performs best
  • Health policy and access: Informs policy decisions that impact areas such as access, equity and health system performance

Why the health economics and outcomes research field matters now more than ever

The demand for professionals trained in HEOR continues to grow across the healthcare, pharmaceutical and public sectors.

This is because applied health outcomes research:

  • Improves patient care by identifying treatments that offer better results or fewer side effects
  • Supports evidence-based decisions made by providers, payers and policy makers
  • Informs pricing and reimbursement strategies for new drugs or medical technologies
  • Promotes healthcare accessibility by uncovering barriers in treatment access or outcomes
  • Helps control rising healthcare costs through value-based analysis

As healthcare systems shift toward evidence-based approaches, professionals with HEOR training are well-positioned to recommend improvements in care and policy.

In summary, applied health outcomes research plays a vital role in shaping smarter, more effective healthcare decisions, making your expertise in this field both timely and impactful.

The Application Process and Financial Aid

UNT Health designed admission to the Online Graduate Certificate in Applied Health Outcomes Research to be accessible for qualified professionals who are ready to advance their careers in health economics and outcomes research. Therefore, the application process is streamlined and no GRE is required.

Basic admission criteria

In order to apply, you must meet requirements such as:

  • Bachelor’s degree in any discipline from an accredited institution or current enrollment in a PharmD program
    • While exposure to healthcare is beneficial, it is not required.
  • Prerequisite coursework: Completion of at least three semester credit hours in statistics is required.
  • Minimum GPA of 2.75: A cumulative GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale is typically expected.
  • No GRE required: You do not need to submit GRE scores as part of your application. This helps streamline the process and reduce barriers for working professionals.
  • English language proficiency (if applicable): International applicants must meet the university’s TOEFL requirements.

Required application materials

  • Official transcripts: Submit transcripts from all colleges or universities you’ve attended.
  • Statement of purpose: A brief essay explaining your interest in health outcomes research and how this certificate aligns with your professional goals.
  • Updated resume or CV: Include your academic background, work experience and any relevant certifications or training.
  • One letter of recommendation: A letter from a professional or academic contact who can speak to your readiness for graduate-level work.
  • Application deadline: Apply by July 15 in order to be considered for fall enrollment.
