# Pharma Certification, HEOR, and Medical Affairs Readiness Research

Research date: 2026-07-02

This folder archives public materials related to medical affairs, MSL, HEOR, HTA, market access, evidence generation, AI in medical affairs, and pharma-readiness training for clinicians or healthcare professionals moving into industry.

## Contents

- `source_inventory.csv` / `source_inventory.json`: canonical source index with title, source type, URL, local saved path, status, byte size, and download error if any.
- `sources/certifications/`: saved public HTML pages and PDFs for certification, certificate, fellowship, and training programs.
- `sources/case_studies/`: saved public HTML pages and PDFs for case studies, practice resources, white papers, syllabi, methods guides, and workshop examples.
- `subagent_reports/certification_sources.md`: detailed certification and training research report.
- `subagent_reports/case_study_sources.md`: detailed case-study and practice-resource research report.
- `subagent_reports/cohere_style_ci_feedback.md`: report on the reusable `cohere-style-ci` skill update.
- `subagent_reports/second_pass_certification_gaps.md`: second-pass training, credential, certificate, and fellowship gap report.
- `subagent_reports/second_pass_case_study_gaps.md`: second-pass case-study, template, simulation, and practice-material gap report.
- `subagent_reports/second_pass_cohere_style_ci_feedback.md`: report on the second reusable `cohere-style-ci` source-handling update.
- `curriculum_opportunity_map.md`: synthesis for a clinician-to-pharma course concept.

## High-Value Anchors

- ACMA BCMAS: role-oriented medical affairs and MSL certification benchmark.
- MAPS Foundations and MAPS evidence-generation guidance: competency framework, evidence generation, governance, and medical affairs practice.
- UW CHOICE / UW PCE: strongest public HEOR, HTA, and market access certificate structure; three syllabi are archived.
- ISPOR: short-course and HEOR impact-case standards.
- DIA: medical affairs eLearning and case-competition models.
- FDA: regulator-authored development, rare-disease, complex trial, and drug approval case studies.
- NICE, ICER, iDSI, CDA-AMC URL-only: HTA and value-assessment methods for payer/market-access simulation design.
- GMDP/KCL, IFAPP, FPM, PharmaTrain, ECPM: global pharmaceutical medicine and medicines-development credential standards.
- Jefferson, UNT, JHU, USC, Penn, LSHTM URL-only, Eu2P, ISPE: HEOR, RWE, pharmacoepidemiology, and drug-safety training landscape.
- RAPS, Arizona/C-Path, USC, Maryland, FDA NCTR/UAMS, UCSF ACDRS: regulatory-science credential and course landscape.
- NIH SEED, AMCP, PBAC, MSAC URL-only, FDA AI/RWE, EMA, WHO, EUPATI URL-only: expanded practice-case, dossier, HTA, AI, and regulatory-science training materials.
