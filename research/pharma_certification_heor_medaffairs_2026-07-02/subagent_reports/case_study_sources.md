# Public Case Study and Practice-Resource Sources for Pharma Readiness Curriculum

Research date: 2026-07-02. Scope: public case studies, simulations, workshop materials, sample assignments, and teaching/practice resources that can help learners understand pharma, medical affairs, HEOR, market access, evidence generation, RWE, HTA, drug development, and commercialization. Files were not downloaded; URLs below identify public pages or documents.

## High-fit public case and exercise sources

### 1. LEADER 3D rare disease drug development case studies
- **Organization:** FDA CDER Accelerating Rare Disease Cures program.
- **URL:** https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/accelerating-rare-disease-cures-arc-program/learning-and-education-advance-and-empower-rare-disease-drug-developers-leader-3d
- **Source type:** FDA educational case-study hub with videos, resource lists, a user guide, and drug-specific case PDFs.
- **Downloadable content:** Yes.
- **Direct PDF/document URLs:** User guide: https://www.fda.gov/media/185425/download?attachment= ; Odevixibat case: https://www.fda.gov/media/186133/download?attachment= ; Vutrisiran case: https://www.fda.gov/media/186134/download?attachment= ; Tofersen case: https://www.fda.gov/media/186135/download?attachment= ; Avalglucosidase alfa-ngpt / Seladelpar case: https://www.fda.gov/media/186136/download?attachment= ; Fosdenopterin case: https://www.fda.gov/media/185752/download?attachment= ; Olipudase alfa-rpcp case: https://www.fda.gov/media/185753/download?attachment= .
- **Topic area:** Rare disease regulatory strategy, clinical outcome assessment, externally controlled trials, surrogate endpoints, biomarkers, single adequate and well-controlled trial plus confirmatory evidence, and dose escalation.
- **Curriculum use:** Anchor a regulatory readiness module where learners map each case to evidence standard, endpoint choice, data limitations, FDA interaction points, and what a development team would need to prepare before a Type B or rare-disease-focused FDA meeting.
- **Caveats/licensing:** FDA states the case studies are not designed to provide product-approval strategies and that rare disease development must be assessed case by case: https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/accelerating-rare-disease-cures-arc-program/learning-and-education-advance-and-empower-rare-disease-drug-developers-leader-3d .

### 2. Complex Innovative Trial Design Paired Meeting Program case studies
- **Organization:** FDA.
- **URL:** https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/complex-innovative-trial-design-meeting-program
- **Source type:** FDA public program page with case-study PDFs for complex adaptive, Bayesian, master protocol, external-control, and simulation-heavy designs.
- **Downloadable content:** Yes.
- **Direct PDF/document URLs:** Alopecia areata external/synthetic control case: https://www.fda.gov/media/188560/download?attachment= ; master protocol chronic pain case: https://www.fda.gov/media/155403/download?attachment= ; other case PDFs are linked from the program page under "CID Paired Program Trial Design Case Studies": https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/complex-innovative-trial-design-meeting-program .
- **Topic area:** Trial simulation, Bayesian borrowing, external controls, master protocols, adaptive designs, and regulatory evaluation of design operating characteristics.
- **Curriculum use:** Use as a simulation-readiness module: learners critique whether the design assumptions are defensible, identify required simulation scenarios, and translate FDA's disclosed categories into a sponsor briefing-package outline.
- **Caveats/licensing:** FDA notes each case focuses on the specific trial design submitted and does not discuss all other development-program evidence needs: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/complex-innovative-trial-design-meeting-program .

## Cross-curriculum fit notes

- **Best regulatory case backbone:** FDA LEADER 3D and FDA CID provide the strongest public, regulator-authored case material for realistic development decisions, because they include specific product or design contexts plus FDA caveats and decision considerations: https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/accelerating-rare-disease-cures-arc-program/learning-and-education-advance-and-empower-rare-disease-drug-developers-leader-3d and https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/complex-innovative-trial-design-meeting-program .
- **Best HEOR/HTA backbone:** ICER, NICE, CDA-AMC, iDSI, ISPOR, and UF materials can be combined into a sequence from pharmacoeconomic basics to jurisdiction-specific HTA submission thinking: https://icer.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/ICER-Guide-to-Understanding-Health-Technology-Assessment-6.19.18.pdf , https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/pmg36/resources/nice-technology-appraisal-and-highly-specialised-technologies-guidance-the-manual-pdf-72286779244741 , https://www.cda-amc.ca/sites/default/files/MG%20Methods/MG0030-Quantitative-Methods-Manual_Mar%202025.pdf , https://f1000research.com/documents/8-703/pdf , https://www.ispor.org/conferences-education/conferences/submit-abstract/abstract-submission-instructions , and https://curriculum.pharmacy.ufl.edu/wordpress/files/2025/09/PHA5267-Syllabus-2025.pdf .
- **Best medical affairs practice backbone:** MAPS case/workshop materials plus IQVIA's MSL deployment example support role-specific practice in scientific communications, launch readiness, KOL engagement, medical metrics, and field medical operating models: https://medicalaffairs.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Challenges-to-Opportunities.pdf , https://medicalaffairs.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/MAPS-MasterClass-Singapore-English-Brochure-5.13-min.pdf , https://medicalaffairs.org/case-study-h1/ , https://medicalaffairs.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FINAL-SLIDES-MAPS-Webinar-Mar-10-Amedea-Pharma-Final-Updated-for-download14-Read-Only.pdf , and https://www.iqvia.com/-/media/library/white-papers/medical-science-liaisons.pdf .
- **Main licensing caution:** Many sources are public but not necessarily open-license. For a paid or distributed curriculum, use public URLs as assigned readings, cite clearly, avoid copying large excerpts or slide images, and seek permission before republishing third-party content.
