# Certification, Certificate, and Training Sources for Medical Affairs / HEOR / Market Access

Research date: 2026-07-02  
Scope: current public web research on certifications, certificate programs, and training materials relevant to clinicians moving into medical affairs, MSL, HEOR, market access, evidence generation, and pharma industry readiness. No files were downloaded.

## Executive takeaways

- The strongest comprehensive pharma-readiness anchors are ACMA BCMAS for medical affairs/MSL role coverage, MAPS Foundations for competency-framework-based onboarding, DIA Medical Affairs eLearning for practical job-aid/scenario-based modules, and UW CHOICE for HEOR/HTA/market access depth. Sources: https://medicalaffairsspecialist.org/certifications/bcmas/program-info; https://medicalaffairs.org/medical-affairs-training/; https://www.diaglobal.org/en/course-listing/elearning/2023/05/medical-affairs-elearning-program; https://sop.washington.edu/choice/graduate-education-training-programs/certificate-in-health-economics-health-technology-assessment-and-market-access/
- HEOR-specific depth is best covered by UW CHOICE, Rutgers HOPE, Tufts HEOR, and ISPOR short courses/education center; these cover economic evaluation, HTA, pharmacoeconomic modeling, outcomes research, RWE-adjacent methods, pricing, reimbursement, and market access. Sources: https://sop.washington.edu/choice/graduate-education-training-programs/certificate-in-health-economics-health-technology-assessment-and-market-access/; https://sph.rutgers.edu/academics/certificate-programs/health-outcomes-policy-and-economics-certificate; https://gsbs.tufts.edu/academics/clinical-translational-science-programs/certificate-health-economics-outcomes-research; https://www.ispor.org/education-training/short-courses
- Industry immersion is represented by Rutgers RPIF and St. John's/Pfizer fellowships, but those are selective fellowship programs rather than broadly accessible certificate courses. Sources: https://pharmafellows.rutgers.edu/; https://www.stjohns.edu/academics/schools/college-pharmacy-and-health-sciences/pharmaceutical-industry-fellowship-programs
