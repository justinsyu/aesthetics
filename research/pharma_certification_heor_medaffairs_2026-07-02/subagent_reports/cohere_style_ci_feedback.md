## Decision

A narrow reusable lesson applies to competitive-intelligence work on pharma certification, training, and course-market research.

The existing skill already covered adjacent issues: conference education inventories, technical training inventories, source-observed facts versus analyst implications, and prompt-template scans. The current request adds a more specific recurring risk: competitor course or certification claims can be unintentionally converted into curriculum design conclusions or course strategy recommendations without enough separation between public-source facts and analyst synthesis.

## Change made

Added one bullet to `_skills_to_install/cohere-style-ci/SKILL.md` in the evidence rules near the existing conference-education and technical-training inventory guidance.

The new guidance requires certification, training, or course-market CI work to:

- Archive competitor course claims as source-stated facts before deriving curriculum implications.
- Preserve exact visible claim text, course owner, credential or certificate language, audience, format, duration, price when visible, modules or learning objectives, URL, access date, and screenshot or archived-source path.
- Keep source-stated competitor claims separate from analyst-derived curriculum design implications, white-space hypotheses, positioning options, or course strategy recommendations.
- Label those implications as synthesis unless a source directly states them.
