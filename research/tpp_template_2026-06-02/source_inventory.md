## Practical synthesis

A pharma TPP should be a living, cross-functional decision document. The most practical structure is a matrix that compares minimum viable, base case, and aspirational/stretch attributes across clinical, regulatory, CMC, HEOR, market access, payer/HTA, patient, HCP, commercial, and lifecycle domains.

Key implications for the template:

- Start with metadata, version control, owners, next refresh date, and the decision the TPP supports.
- Define indication, population, unmet need, current/future standard of care, comparator landscape, label intent, and target product claims.
- Use minimum/base/aspirational columns, with explicit go/no-go criteria for essential attributes.
- Link each attribute to evidence needs: clinical, nonclinical, CMC/QTPP, RWE, PRO/COA, HEOR, economic modeling, payer research, and HTA/dossier requirements.
- Pressure-test the profile against physician prescribing, payer coverage, patient choice, pricing/reimbursement, utilization management, HTA outcomes, and launch sequencing.
- Treat the TPP as dynamic: update it after data readouts, payer feedback, competitor events, regulatory/HTA changes, scenario triggers, and lifecycle decisions.
