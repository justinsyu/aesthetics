# Practical pharma TPP template

Checked: 2026-06-02

Purpose: a concise working template for a pharma target product profile (TPP) or reimbursable TPP (rTPP). Use it to align clinical, regulatory, CMC, HEOR, market access, commercial, patient, HCP, and payer/HTA assumptions.

## 7. Scenario planning and lifecycle triggers

| Trigger | Scenario to evaluate | TPP section affected | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor data or approval | Future SoC changes | Comparator, efficacy, value, pricing | Refresh benchmarks and minimum profile |
| Regulatory feedback | Label or trial design changes | Intended use, endpoints, population | Update development plan |
| Payer/HTA feedback | Evidence or access assumptions change | HEOR, market access, pricing | Update evidence plan and value story |
| Clinical readout | Asset misses, meets, or exceeds threshold | Minimum/base/aspirational profile | Go/no-go or redesign |
| CMC/manufacturing issue | Formulation, stability, device, scalability changes | QTPP, route/admin, launch readiness | Update risk and timeline |
| BD&L / valuation milestone | Investment case changes | Market size, differentiation, lifecycle | Refresh scenarios and opportunity map |
