## Cross-Source Synthesis

The regulatory sources support using a TPP as a voluntary, label-oriented development planning tool, while also showing that the 2007 FDA TPP guidance is withdrawn and should not be treated as current guidance. FDA RWD/RWE and PFDD/COA materials broaden the TPP beyond pivotal efficacy and safety by adding real-world evidence, postapproval evidence, patient experience, and fit-for-purpose outcome assessment expectations.

The HTA and HEOR standards sources show that a useful pharma TPP must connect clinical claims to payer and HTA decision needs: comparator selection, uncertainty, economic value, access scenarios, evidence synthesis, and patient-relevant outcomes. NICE and ISPOR provide the standards frame; vendor sources show how pharma teams operationalize that frame across pricing, evidence generation, RWE, value dossiers, payer communications, and launch planning.

Across HEOR and market access vendors, the consistent pattern is lifecycle evidence integration. The practical template should therefore include owners, status, evidence needed, minimum/target/aspirational thresholds, and decision implications for every major attribute. It should also maintain a living competitive intelligence and evidence surveillance layer so assumptions about target label, value story, price, access, and differentiation are updated as the asset and market evolve.
