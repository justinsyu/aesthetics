Integrated Evidence Strategy for US Launch of a Rare Blood Cancer Treatment

Value

  • Established a high standard for evidence quality and transparency

  • Enabled cross-functional synergies to avoid a duplicative evidence generation efforts

  • Created an IEGP and SOP that became the blueprint for all future assets

  • Met stakeholder needs to support rapid clinical adoption, reimbursement, and market access across the US

Resource Type
Case Study

Opportunity

Develop and implement an IEG plan to support the US launch of an innovative treatment in clinical development for a rare blood cancer. 

Challenge

  • The client had recently entered a co-commercialization agreement with a company and needed external expertise to provide disease area insight. 
  • The client was relatively new to IEG planning and required an experienced partner to lead cross-functional strategy development and execution. 

What We Did

  1. Collaborated closely with the client and their partner company to identify key internal stakeholders involved in the IEG plan development process
  2. Leveraged our deep medical and scientific expertise to develop an aspirational value proposition and define value messages, and subsequently identified evidence gaps
  3. Pressure tested the value proposition and messaging with external stakeholders, including a broad US payer network, ensuring relevance across the majority of insured patients
  4. Built a collaborative cross-company team, breaking down siloes and aligning stakeholders around the asset value story
