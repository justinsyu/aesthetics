Integrated Evidence Generation Planning: A Strategic Imperative for Biopharma Innovation

Whitepaper Aug 5, 2025
GP Carlo

Explore best-practice considerations for integrated evidence generation planning (IEGP) enriched with real world case studies that illustrate the consequences of both success and failure in evidence planning. Learn how a strategic approach can transform evidence from a cost center to a strategic accelerator – cutting costs, shortening time-to-evidence, and boosting stakeholder trust with unified narratives validated by real world impact.

About the author

GP Carlo, Managing Consultant

GP has over 3 decades of industry experience across a number of pharma and biotech, and he has led or been a part of IEGP teams for much of it. His experience spans most therapeutic areas, including rare diseases and vaccines.

Contact GP

Ready to optimize your evidence strategy? Our IEGP experts have decades of experience working with companies of all sizes. Contact us to learn how Lumanity’s Evidence Navigator can transform your path to market.

Evidence Navigator™

Evidence Navigator is our comprehensive approach to Integrated Evidence Generation Planning (IEGP). It leverages decades of industry expertise to create a robust, data-driven strategy that aligns with your business goals.
