### Reference 1
- Source: Encoded Therapeutics press release
- URL: https://encoded.com/press-releases/encoded-therapeutics-presents-new-clinical-data-from-polaris-phase-1-2-trials-of-etx101-gene-therapy-in-dravet-syndrome-at-the-asgct-2026-presidential-symposium/
- Visible publication date: May 13, 2026
- Date screenshot: `screenshots/date-verification/source-01-date.png`
- Evidence screenshots: `screenshots/evidence/source-01-evidence-01.png`; `screenshots/evidence/source-01-evidence-02.png`
- Facts extracted: Encoded presented expanded POLARIS Phase 1/2 ETX101 data in SCN1A+ Dravet syndrome at ASGCT 2026; the update included children aged 6 months to 7 years and analyses through an April 10, 2026 data cutoff; DL3 showed approximately 76% median monthly countable seizure frequency reduction from Week 5 through Week 52; DL4 early data showed the strongest response in patients who did not receive sirolimus; patients with 52 weeks of observation showed VABS-3 adaptive-behavior improvements; patients treated before age 2 showed Bayley-4 cognitive gains; no treatment- or procedure-related serious adverse events were reported.

### Reference 2
- Source: The Jackson Laboratory
- URL: https://www.jax.org/news-and-insights/2026/may/precision-dna-editing-targets-root-cause-of-severe-childhood-epilepsy-in-preclinical-study
- Visible publication date: Article | May 13, 2026
- Date screenshot: `screenshots/date-verification/source-02-date.png`
- Evidence screenshots: `screenshots/evidence/source-02-evidence-01.png`; `screenshots/evidence/source-02-evidence-02.png`
- Facts extracted: JAX described a preclinical Dravet syndrome study focused on the SCN1A R613X variant; researchers used adenine base editing delivered by a single brain injection in very young mice on day 1 or day 12; treated mice had nearly 60% correction of mutated DNA and near-normal gene expression; the edit restored gene function, prevented seizures, improved survival when given at birth, and showed benefit when administered on day 12.
