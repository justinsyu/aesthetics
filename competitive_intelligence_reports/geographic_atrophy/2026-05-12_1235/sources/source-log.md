## Reference 1

- Source: Ocugen Provides Business Update with First Quarter 2026 Financial Results
- URL: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/05/3287702/0/en/Ocugen-Provides-Business-Update-with-First-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results.html
- Visible publication date: May 05, 2026 08:32 ET
- Date verification screenshot: screenshots/date-verification/source-01-ocugen-date.png
- Evidence screenshot: screenshots/evidence/source-01-ocugen-evidence.png
- Facts extracted:
  - Ocugen reported positive 12-month OCU410 Phase 2 ArMaDa clinical trial data for geographic atrophy.
  - Ocugen stated that the optimal dose for Phase 3 showed a statistically significant 31% reduction in lesion size and 27% EZ preservation.
  - Ocugen stated that about 20% of patients demonstrated no progression and 75% of subjects showed more than 30% reduction in lesion growth compared with control.
  - Ocugen stated that the data allow a potential combined U.S./EU registrational Phase 3 trial with 300 subjects and adaptive design powered at over 95%.
