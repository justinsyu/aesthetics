Company primary sources

Two company releases reported GA program updates inside the evidence window12

Ocugen displayed a May 5, 2026 release date, and Annexon displayed a May 7, 2026 release date.12

Ocugen | OCU410

12-month ArMaDa data statement

Ocugen stated that positive 12-month data from the OCU410 Phase 2 ArMaDa clinical trial for GA indicated a statistically significant 31% reduction in lesion size and 27% EZ preservation in the optimal dose for Phase 3.1

Ocugen stated that about 20% of patients demonstrated no disease progression and that 75% of subjects showed more than 30% reduction in lesion growth versus control.1

Ocugen stated that the data allow a potential combined U.S./EU registrational Phase 3 trial with 300 subjects and adaptive design powered at over 95%.1

Annexon | Vonaprument

Phase 3 timing statement

Annexon stated that topline pivotal Phase 3 data for vonaprument for the treatment of GA are expected in Q4 2026.2

Annexon stated that its cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were approximately $225 million as of March 31, 2026 and that anticipated runway extended into the second half of 2027.2

Company-source boundary

  • The OCU410 and vonaprument statements above are reported as company-disclosed facts from the cited releases.12
  • No efficacy comparison between OCU410, vonaprument, or marketed GA products is made from these releases.12

Development activity

The registry records list GA studies spanning systemic, intravitreal, and comparator-controlled designs3456

The statements on this slide describe only the intervention and study-purpose fields visible in the cited records.3456

FWY003

Novartis Phase 2

The FWY003 record describes a randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled dose-range study to assess efficacy and safety in GA secondary to AMD.3

Pozelimab / Cemdisiran

Regeneron Phase 3

The SIENNA record describes subcutaneously administered pozelimab with cemdisiran or cemdisiran alone compared with placebo in participants with GA secondary to AMD.4

JNJ-81201887

Janssen extension

The JNJ-81201887 record describes a long-term extension study assessing safety and tolerability after an intravitreal injection administered in parent clinical studies.5

RO7669330

Roche Phase 1

The RO7669330 record describes ocular and systemic safety and tolerability assessment after multiple unilateral intravitreal doses, and lists Syfovre and Izervay in the intervention field.6
