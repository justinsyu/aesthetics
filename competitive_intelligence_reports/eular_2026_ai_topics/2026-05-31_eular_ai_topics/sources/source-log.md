## Method

The screen included records with explicit Artificial Intelligence keywords or title/body references to AI, machine learning, deep learning, large language models, NLP, computer vision, radiomics, segmentation, robotic imaging, digital twins, and related AI-enabled workflow concepts. Records were clustered into six competitive-intelligence themes. The CSV also assigns each retained record an AI role: substantive AI tool/workflow/evaluation, AI as analytic method, AI infrastructure/simulation, or policy/adoption readiness. Broader ML-method records are labeled separately from explicit AI-tag/title records.

## Limitations

The review prioritized structured local extraction over manual PDF-by-PDF reading. Partial or missing abstract extraction could undercount a small number of AI mentions. Some broader ML-method records are methodological rather than commercial product signals; the deck separates these from explicit AI-tag/title records.
