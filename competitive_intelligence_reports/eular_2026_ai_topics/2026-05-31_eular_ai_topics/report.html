Executive summary

  • The EULAR 2026 data include artificial intelligence-related work in diagnostic triage, LLM/NLP workflows, evidence synthesis, patient education, real-world-evidence prediction, omics stratification, and governance.
  • Imaging and sensor-based applications account for the largest cluster, including ultrasound robotics, MRI/HRCT/radiographic scoring, capillaroscopy, salivary-gland pathology, facial-feature assessment, and hand-photograph assessment.
  • Rheumatoid arthritis has the highest number of artificial intelligence-related records; systemic lupus erythematosus and systemic sclerosis include prognostic modeling, endotyping, safety or real-world-evidence records, and interstitial lung disease imaging topics.
