BGB-B2033 registry update

BeOne BGB-B2033 includes HCC monotherapy expansion arms and estimated enrollment of 3928

HCC population

The Phase 1 study includes locally advanced and metastatic HCC conditions

The BGB-B2033 study includes metastatic hepatocellular carcinoma and locally advanced hepatocellular carcinoma conditions8

The study evaluates BGB-B2033 alone or in combination with tislelizumab with or without bevacizumab in selected advanced or metastatic solid tumors8

Expansion arms

HCC monotherapy dose expansion is separated by geography

Part C is Asia Monotherapy Dose Expansion in HCC8

Part D is US Monotherapy Dose Expansion in HCC8
