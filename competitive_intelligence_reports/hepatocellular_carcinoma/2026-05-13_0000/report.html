Regulatory and trial-status updates

Two source-dated FDA-related HCC items named RZ-001 and Namodenoson1,2

RZ-001

Rznomics reported FDA RMAT designation for RZ-001 in HCC

The source states FDA granted Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy designation to RZ-001, the company's lead investigational candidate for HCC1

The source states RZ-001 previously received Orphan Drug Designation in 2024 and Fast Track Designation in 2025 for HCC1

Namodenoson

Can-Fite documented Phase III HCC evaluation and FDA Fast Track status

The SEC exhibit states that Can-Fite's liver drug, Namodenoson, is being evaluated in a Phase III trial for HCC2
