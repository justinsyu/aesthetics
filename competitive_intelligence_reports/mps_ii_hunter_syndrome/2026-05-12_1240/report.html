Regulatory and commercial events

Windowed sources recorded one FDA voucher notice, one U.S. launch update, and one Peru authorization125

FDA / PRV

AVLAYAH voucher notice

The Federal Register API listed a May 11, 2026 publication date for FDA's notice announcing issuance of a priority review voucher for AVLAYAH.1

FDA determined that AVLAYAH, approved March 24, 2026 and manufactured by Denali Therapeutics Inc., met the criteria for a priority review voucher.1

U.S. launch

Commercial treatment began

Denali's May 7, 2026 SEC exhibit stated AVLAYAH had launched in the U.S. and that first patients were treated in the commercial setting in April.2

Denali stated that commercial product availability, distribution, and a patient support hub were in place.2

Peru authorization

Hunterase ICV

GC Biopharma stated on May 6, 2026 that Hunterase ICV received marketing authorization from Peru's DIGEMID.5

GC Biopharma stated the Peru authorization was Hunterase ICV's third overseas authorization after Japan and Russia.5

Additional recorded facts

  • GC Biopharma stated Hunterase ICV is administered directly into the cerebral ventricles once monthly.5
  • Denali stated the ongoing global Phase 2/3 COMPASS study is designed to generate confirmatory evidence and support global regulatory submissions for AVLAYAH.2

Tividenofusp alfa extension
