Screening and access

Two sources reported screening-market and eligibility facts inside the evidence window12

Guardant published a May 7, 2026 financial update, and Ontario published a May 7, 2026 screening-eligibility release.12

Commercial screening

Guardant Health reported Q1 screening revenue and Shield volume

  • Guardant reported screening revenue of $41.6 million for Q1 2026, compared with $5.7 million for the corresponding prior-year period.1
  • Guardant said Shield screening test volume grew to approximately 44,000 tests in Q1 2026, from approximately 9,000 tests in the prior-year period.1
Public screening

Ontario announced a July 1 eligibility expansion

  • Ontario said publicly funded colorectal cancer screening eligibility will be lowered from age 50 to age 45 for all individuals starting July 1, 2026.2
  • Ontario said eligibility for people at increased risk will be age 40, and the expansion gives more than one million additional people the option to connect to screening services.2

Recorded screening facts

The source set contains one U.S. company commercial update for a blood-based screening test and one Ontario government release changing access to publicly funded colorectal cancer screening.12

Pipeline and trial activity

One company pipeline update and four registry records describe active colorectal cancer development activity34567

Immunocore's May 6 update described IMC-R117C enrollment, while four ClinicalTrials.gov records showed visible May 6 or May 8 registry updates.34567

IMC-R117C

Immunocore

Immunocore stated that it is enrolling a Phase 1/2 dose-escalation trial of IMC-R117C in HLA-A*02:01 positive patients with advanced solid tumors, including colorectal cancer, as a single agent and with standards of care.3

Initial data

2027 timing

Immunocore stated that it expects to present initial data for IMC-R117C in 2027.3
