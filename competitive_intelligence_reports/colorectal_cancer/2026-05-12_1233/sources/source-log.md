- Facts extracted:
  - Ontario said publicly funded colorectal cancer screening eligibility will be lowered from age 50 to age 45 for all individuals starting July 1, 2026.

  - Ontario said the expansion gives more than one million additional people the option to connect to life-saving screening services.
