  • Coherus reported enrollment of a Phase 1b/2a tagmokitug/toripalimab study in 4L+ colorectal cancer with initial data expected in 2H 2026; BBOT reported BBO-10203 clinical benefit in colorectal cancer patients described as more than 80% third line or later.12

CCR8 and RAS-PI3K disclosures define late-line colorectal cancer readout cadence for 2026

Tagmokitug plus toripalimab

CCR8 4L+ CRC 2H 2026 data

Coherus described tagmokitug as a selective cytolytic CCR8 antibody and reported that the tagmokitug/toripalimab Phase 1b/2a combination study in 4L+ colorectal cancer is enrolling, with initial data expected in 2H 2026.1

BBO-10203

RAS-PI3Kα CRC & HR+ BC 2H 2026 update

BBOT described BBO-10203 as an orally bioavailable molecule designed to block the RAS-PI3Kα interaction and reported clinical benefit in colorectal cancer patients described as more than 80% third line or later, with tumor reductions observed in some patients.2

The cited disclosures identify one immunology combination and one RAS-pathway program with colorectal-relevant clinical data timing in 2H 2026. BBOT's mixed-tumor disclosure is cited only for the colorectal cancer population explicitly named in the source.12

First-posted second-line metastatic and adaptive TNT records describe regimen-level evaluation

Liposomal irinotecan in second-line mCRC

Phase 2/3 Second line 408 estimated enrollment

NCT07585279 was first posted on May 13, 2026 and lists a randomized Phase II/III study comparing liposomal irinotecan combination regimens versus irinotecan combination regimens in second-line metastatic colorectal cancer.5

Adaptive short-course TNT in LARC

Phase 2 pMMR/MSS LARC 60 estimated enrollment

NCT07581626 was first posted on May 12, 2026 and lists a Phase 2 locally advanced rectal cancer regimen comprising short-course radiotherapy, chemotherapy, cetuximab for wild-type RAS/BRAF or bevacizumab for mutant RAS/BRAF, and sintilimab.6

The metastatic record evaluates comparative chemotherapy backbones with liposomal irinotecan, while the rectal-cancer TNT record combines radiation, chemotherapy, biomarker-directed targeted therapy, and PD-1 blockade within one investigational regimen.56
