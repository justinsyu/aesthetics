Drug study

BL1107 versus timolol

ClinicalTrials.gov listed NCT07168902 as an active-not-recruiting Phase 2 study of BL1107 eye drops versus timolol in adults with primary open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension, with Bausch & Lomb Incorporated as sponsor and May 7, 2026 as Last Update Posted.1

Laser study

Direct versus standard SLT

ClinicalTrials.gov listed NCT07408154 as a recruiting record titled "Direct vs Standard SLT for Glaucoma: A Noninferiority Trial," with Medical University of South Carolina as sponsor and May 6, 2026 as Last Update Posted.3

Terminated

Krytantek, Eliptic, and Gaap study

ClinicalTrials.gov listed NCT04702789 as a terminated study comparing Krytantek Ofteno PF or Eliptic Ofteno PF plus Gaap Ofteno PF for primary open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension, with Laboratorios Sophia S.A de C.V. as sponsor and May 7, 2026 as Last Update Posted.4

Not yet recruiting

Prostaglandin-analogue neuroprotection

ClinicalTrials.gov listed NCT07074782 as not yet recruiting and titled "Retinal Ganglion Cell Neuroprotection Under Prostaglandin Analogues," with Association for Innovation and Biomedical Research on Light and Image as sponsor and May 6, 2026 as Last Update Posted.5
