FDA review

LEQEMBI IQLIK

Biogen and Eisai stated that FDA extended the sBLA review period for once-weekly subcutaneous lecanemab-irmb as a starting dose by three months and set an August 24, 2026 PDUFA action date.1

Phase 2

PROGRESS-AD

Alector stated that the PROGRESS-AD Phase 2 trial of nivisnebart in early Alzheimer’s disease was discontinued after a pre-specified interim futility analysis.2

Phase 3

Buntanetap

Annovis described buntanetap as an investigational oral therapy for neurodegenerative diseases and stated that it was in a pivotal Phase 3 clinical trial.3

Additional recorded facts

  • Biogen and Eisai stated that FDA requested additional information and determined that the information constituted a major amendment to the sBLA.1
  • Alector listed AL037/AL137 as an ABC-enabled antibody program for Alzheimer’s disease and AL064/AL164 as an ABC-enabled tau siRNA program for Alzheimer’s disease and other tauopathies.2
  • Annovis scheduled its Fierce Biotech Week presentation for Wednesday, May 13, 2026.3

Peer-reviewed article

Remote capillary biomarkers

A Nature Communications article published on May 6, 2026 reported that self-administered fingerprick tests measuring p-tau217 and GFAP correlated with venous biomarkers and with cognition and function.4

The article stated that the cohort included 174 people who were cognitively normal or had mild cognitive impairment or Alzheimer’s disease.4

Official organization release

(re)think your brain

The Alzheimer’s Association announced the launch of (re)think your brain on May 11, 2026 and described it as a science-based initiative intended to move people from brain health awareness to action.8

The same release stated that the initiative includes a 6-Step Challenge with practical actions to support brain health and cognitive function.8
