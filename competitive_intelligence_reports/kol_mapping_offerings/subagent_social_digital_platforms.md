## Cross-vendor CI takeaways

- The strongest **public-data-replicable** components are publication/trial/congress mapping, public social-post analysis, follower/mention/reply networks, Open Payments, guideline authorship, conference faculty, and news mentions.
- The least replicable components are **identity resolution**, verified-HCP membership, claims/Rx/lab/treatment-pattern data, peer nominations, closed-community engagement, first-party media behavior, and proprietary influence scores.
- CREATION.co and Aissel are the closest direct comparators for **open-social DOL intelligence**. IQVIA, Veeva, H1, and Within3 compete more as **multi-domain KOL/DOL intelligence platforms**. Sermo, Skipta, Medscape/WebMD, M3, Impetus, and ExtendMed are stronger as **activation, engagement, research, or closed-network access layers** once a target audience or expert cohort is defined.
- Expert marketplaces and market-research recruiters such as Kolabtree and KOLgroups can support expert access and primary insight generation, but public materials do not show the same KOL influence analytics or DOL targeting depth as dedicated KOL/DOL intelligence platforms.
