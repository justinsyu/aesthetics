## Analyst Readout

- **Most directly dedicated KOL platforms:** Veeva Link Key People, IQVIA Expert Ecosystem, Definitive/Monocl, H1 HCP Universe, and ZS ZAIDYN Opinion Leader Intelligence.
- **Clinical investigator / site intelligence that overlaps with KOL mapping:** Citeline Sitetrove / Investigator SmartSelect and Clarivate Cortellis Clinical Trials Intelligence.
- **Broad CI / R&D platforms rather than KOL platforms:** Clarivate Cortellis Competitive Intelligence / Drug Discovery Intelligence and Citeline Pharmaprojects / Trialtrove. These are valuable for KOL-adjacent context but do not replace a dedicated KOL/expert graph.
- **Public-data replication ceiling:** no reviewed classic platform is fully replicable from public sources. Public data can reproduce a lower-fidelity map of publication authorship, clinical-trial investigators, congress presenters, payments, grants, guidelines, affiliations, and some digital activity. The differentiators are identity resolution, licensed claims/patient data, peer nominations, proprietary conference/site performance feeds, analyst curation, AI scoring, and CRM/engagement integration.
