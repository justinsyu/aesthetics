EGFR-mutant NSCLC

Firmonertinib pivotal trials advanced. CFT8919 was not moved forward outside Greater China13

EGFR exon 20 insertion

FURVENT

FURVENT is a global pivotal three-arm Phase 3 trial of firmonertinib in first-line nonsquamous locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 20 insertion mutations, and the study enrolled 398 patients globally1

Top-line monotherapy data from FURVENT were projected for mid-20261

EGFR PACC

ALPACCA

ALPACCA is a global pivotal two-arm Phase 3 trial of firmonertinib in first-line nonsquamous locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC with EGFR PACC mutations1

The ALPACCA study continued to enroll patients globally and evaluates firmonertinib 240 mg once daily versus investigator’s choice of osimertinib or afatinib1

EGFR L858R degrader

CFT8919

C4 Therapeutics made the decision not to advance CFT8919, an EGFR L858R degrader, into the next phase of clinical development outside Greater China at this time3

The cited rationale included the evolving EGFR-mutated NSCLC treatment landscape, capital priorities, and available clinical data to date3

ASCO 2026

EIK1001 and sac-TMT plus KEYTRUDA were positioned around NSCLC data presentations26

TLR7/8 agonist

EIK1001 plus pembrolizumab and chemotherapy

EIK1001 is a systemically administered dual agonist of Toll-like receptors 7 and 8 designed to stimulate innate and adaptive immune responses, and the program continues across melanoma and NSCLC trials2

Three ASCO abstracts were accepted, including updated data from a fully enrolled Phase 2 trial evaluating EIK1001 with pembrolizumab and chemotherapy for NSCLC; Eikon also documented initiation of a Phase 2/3 trial in stage 4 NSCLC with pembrolizumab and chemotherapy2

TROP2-directed ADC

OptiTROP-Lung05

Merck listed Phase 3 OptiTROP-Lung05 data from China, led by Kelun-Biotech, evaluating sacituzumab tirumotecan plus KEYTRUDA in advanced non-small cell lung cancer for the ASCO 2026 lung cancer metastatic oral abstract session6

Sacituzumab tirumotecan was described as an investigational TROP2-directed antibody-drug conjugate with ongoing research in non-small cell lung cancer6

Additional mechanisms

KRAS, cell-cycle, and virotherapy programs documented NSCLC-relevant activity or development status457

KRAS G12C

BBO-8520

BBO-8520 is an orally bioavailable small-molecule direct inhibitor targeting both the ON and OFF states of KRAS4

As of November 15, 2025, BBO-8520 monotherapy in KRAS G12C NSCLC showed a 65% ORR and 68% six-month PFS, with 83% of patients eligible for six-month follow-up remaining on treatment for at least six months4

WEE1 / PKMYT1

ACR-2316

Initial Phase 1 monotherapy dose-escalation data for ACR-2316 demonstrated a favorable safety profile primarily limited to transient neutropenia, and clinical activity included tumor shrinkage, prolonged clinical benefit, partial responses, and strong disease control in SCLC and squamous NSCLC5

AP3-based AACR data documented a mechanistic rationale for potential combinations with immune checkpoint inhibitors5

Systemic virotherapy

CLD-401

CLD-401 is the first lead candidate from Calidi’s RedTail platform, a systemically delivered virotherapy platform engineered to selectively target tumors, remodel the tumor microenvironment, and enable high-level expression of therapeutic genetic payloads directly within tumors7

CLD-401 was in IND-enabling studies and targeted non-small cell lung cancer, head and neck cancer, and other tumor types with high unmet medical need7
