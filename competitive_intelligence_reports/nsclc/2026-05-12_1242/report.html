Company releases

Four company releases documented NSCLC-relevant pipeline or regulatory facts1234

The source dates on this slide were visibly verified from open browser screenshots and ranged from May 5 through May 7, 2026.1234

Stage III NSCLC

BioNTech: ROSETTA Lung-201

BioNTech stated that ROSETTA Lung-201, a global Phase 3 trial, is evaluating pumitamig versus durvalumab after concurrent chemoradiation in patients with unresectable stage III NSCLC.1

MET-altered disease

Pathos AI: DO-2

Pathos AI reported that in a Phase 1 study of 28 patients, DO-2 demonstrated tumor shrinkage in all 10 evaluable MET exon 14 skipping NSCLC patients and a peripheral edema rate of 5%.2

EGFR-mutant NSCLC

Black Diamond: silevertinib

Black Diamond stated that an oral presentation of silevertinib Phase 2 data in frontline EGFRm NSCLC, including preliminary DOR and PFS data, would occur at the 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting.3

EGFR exon 20ins

Cullinan: zipalertinib

Cullinan stated that the zipalertinib NDA for relapsed EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC was accepted by FDA, with a PDUFA target action date of February 27, 2027.4
