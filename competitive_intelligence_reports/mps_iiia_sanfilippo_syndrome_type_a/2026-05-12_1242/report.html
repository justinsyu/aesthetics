01 / 06
UX111 regulatory status

Ultragenyx reported FDA acceptance of the UX111 resubmitted BLA and a September 2026 action date1

Ultragenyx described UX111 as rebisufligene etisparvovec, an AAV9 gene therapy for Sanfilippo syndrome type A, and stated that FDA accepted the resubmitted BLA seeking accelerated approval in April 2026.1

BLA review

FDA accepted the resubmission

Ultragenyx stated that FDA accepted for review the resubmitted BLA seeking accelerated approval for UX111 as a treatment for MPS IIIA.1

PDUFA

September 19, 2026

Ultragenyx stated that FDA granted BLA Priority Review in February 2025 and assigned a PDUFA action date of September 19, 2026 in April 2026.1

Disease scope

No approved underlying therapy cited

Ultragenyx's Form 10-Q described MPS IIIA as a rare lysosomal storage disease with no approved treatment that primarily affects the central nervous system.2

Program profile recorded in Form 10-Q

  • Ultragenyx described UX111 as an AAV9 gene therapy product candidate administered by one-time IV infusion.2
  • Ultragenyx stated that the UX111 program had RMAT, Fast Track, Rare Pediatric Disease, and Orphan Drug designations in the United States, and PRIME and Orphan Medicinal Product designations in the European Union.2
02 / 06
UX111 evidence and operations

Ultragenyx linked the resubmission to longer-term data and disclosed launch-preparation manufacturing costs12

Ultragenyx stated that the resubmitted BLA included longer-term data presented at WORLD Symposium 2026 and included up to eight years of follow-up.1

Clinical evidence

Longer-term follow-up in the resubmission

Ultragenyx stated that the data showed further clinical improvement relative to natural-history decline, durable treatment effect across clinical evaluations and multiple biomarkers, and an acceptable safety profile.1

The current report does not add efficacy interpretation beyond the sponsor statement in the cited source.1

Operating disclosure

Manufacturing costs identified

Ultragenyx stated that the increase in gene therapy research and development expenses was primarily due to UX111 and DTX401 manufacturing costs in preparation for commercial launch.2

Ultragenyx reported cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities of $534 million as of March 31, 2026.1

Recorded facts

The same current source set ties UX111 to a regulatory-review event, a September 19, 2026 PDUFA action date, longer-term follow-up in the resubmission, and manufacturing costs described as commercial launch preparation.12

03 / 06
DNL126 clinical path

Denali described DNL126 as an IV ETV-enabled SGSH replacement therapy with Phase 3 start-up underway3

Denali stated that DNL126 is designed to deliver SGSH into the brain and body with the goal of addressing behavioral, cognitive, and physical manifestations of Sanfilippo syndrome type A.3

Modality

ETV-enabled SGSH

Denali described DNL126 as an investigational, intravenously administered Enzyme TransportVehicle-enabled SGSH replacement therapy.3

Current trial

Phase 1/2 ongoing

Denali stated that the Phase 1/2 trial of DNL126 is ongoing.3

Confirmatory study

Global Phase 3 start-up

Denali stated that start-up activities are underway for a global Phase 3 confirmatory study.3

2027 regulatory expectation

Denali stated that it expects a BLA submission and potential accelerated approval for DNL126 for Sanfilippo syndrome type A in 2027.3
