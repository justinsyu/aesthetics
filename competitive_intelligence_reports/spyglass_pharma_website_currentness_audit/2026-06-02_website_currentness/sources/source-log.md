## Reviewed But Not Used As Findings

- SpyGlass pipeline page statements that two Phase 3 clinical trials are underway were reviewed against the May 14, 2026 company update and retained as aligned context, not as a currentness observation.
- ClinicalTrials.gov records for SGP-005 (NCT07218783), SGP-006 (NCT07218796), and related SpyGlass studies were searched as scope context. The BCDVA time-frame divergence was retained as a potential registry-versus-website/source observation; no trial-status discrepancy was used because the pipeline wording did not conflict with current company update language.
- SpyGlass investor-relations webpages were opened as source candidates. Headless screenshot capture was blocked by the investor-relations host, so the same company releases filed with the SEC were used for rendered evidence screenshots.
