Executive summary

  • The Study Data page states that Phase 1/2 enrollment was completed in November 2024 and that 12-month interim data were expected in 2026; the March 9, 2026 company release filed with the SEC states that positive 12-month Phase 1/2 results had been announced.12
  • The BIM-IOL technology page's Clinical Results to Date module foregrounds Phase 1/2 data at 3 months and cites a November 2025 data source; later company primary sources identify 12-month Phase 1/2 results, including 98% drop-free status at the intended commercial dose in the May 14, 2026 update.34
  • The Study Data page lists the Phase 3 BCDVA co-primary endpoint at 12 months, while the current ClinicalTrials.gov records for SGP-005 and SGP-006 list the BCDVA primary outcome time frame as Month 6; this is framed as a registry-versus-website/source divergence.789
  • Context reviewed but not treated as a finding: the pipeline page states that two Phase 3 trials are underway, which aligns with the May 14, 2026 company update that enrollment remains on track with completion expected in 2027.56

Observation 3 | Phase 3 endpoint timing

Website Phase 3 BCDVA timing differs from current trial registries

The observation is framed as a registry-versus-website/source divergence. The Study Data page lists BCDVA of 20/40 or better at 12 months, while ClinicalTrials.gov lists the BCDVA primary outcome time frame as Month 6 for both SGP-005 and SGP-006.
