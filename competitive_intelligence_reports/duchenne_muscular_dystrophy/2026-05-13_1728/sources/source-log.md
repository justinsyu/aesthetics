### Reference 1 - Entrada Therapeutics

- Source URL: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/07/3289797/0/en/Entrada-Therapeutics-Announces-Positive-Topline-Results-from-Cohort-1-of-Participants-with-Duchenne-Muscular-Dystrophy-Treated-with-ENTR-601-44-in-Phase-1-2-ELEVATE-44-201-Study.html
- Visible publication date: May 07, 2026 07:00 ET
- Date screenshot: screenshots/date-verification/source-01-date.png
- Evidence screenshots:
  - screenshots/evidence/source-01-evidence-01.png
  - screenshots/evidence/source-01-evidence-02.png
- Facts extracted:
  - ELEVATE-44-201 is a clinical study of ENTR-601-44 in ambulatory participants ages four to 20 with a DMD mutation amenable to exon 44 skipping.
  - Entrada described the study as Phase 1/2 in the release title and body.
  - Cohort 1 demonstrated a 2.36% dystrophin increase over 4.00% baseline and a 2.31% exon-skipping increase over 2.66% baseline.
  - Entrada reported statistically significant Time to Rise velocity improvement in treated participants versus placebo.
  - Entrada reported no serious adverse events, no adverse events leading to discontinuation, and expected open-label Cohort 1 and Cohort 2 MAD results by year-end 2026, with Cohort 3 data to follow.
