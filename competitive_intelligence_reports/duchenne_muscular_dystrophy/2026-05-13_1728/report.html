Duchenne muscular dystrophy competitive intelligence update

This update uses five primary source pages from Entrada Therapeutics, Solid Biosciences, Capricor Therapeutics, Dyne Therapeutics, and Atossa Therapeutics published between May 7 and May 13, 2026.12345

Executive summary

  • Entrada reported ENTR-601-44 Cohort 1 biomarker increases and statistically significant Time to Rise velocity improvement versus placebo in ELEVATE-44-201.1
  • Solid reported first participant dosing in Phase 3 IMPACT DUCHENNE and 47 participants dosed in Phase 1/2 INSPIRE DUCHENNE as of May 11, 2026.2
  • Capricor reported deramiocel BLA review with an August 22, 2026 PDUFA target action date, and Dyne reported z-rostudirsen pre-BLA alignment with Q2 2026 BLA timing.34
  • Atossa reported FDA Orphan Drug and Rare Pediatric Disease designations for (Z)-endoxifen in DMD and preclinical activity in dystrophic mouse models.5

Study scope

Ambulatory exon 44-amenable participants

Entrada described ELEVATE-44-201 as a clinical study of ENTR-601-44 in ambulatory participants ages four to 20 with a DMD mutation amenable to exon 44 skipping.1

Phase 1/21Exon 441
Biomarker result 2.36%1

Dystrophin increase over 4.00% baseline

Entrada reported a 2.36% dystrophin increase over 4.00% baseline and a 2.31% exon-skipping increase over 2.66% baseline in treated participants.1

Function and safety

TTR velocity signal with no SAEs reported

Entrada reported statistically significant Time to Rise velocity improvement in treated participants versus placebo, no serious adverse events, and no adverse events leading to discontinuation.1

Next update Entrada reported expected open-label Cohort 1 and Cohort 2 multiple-ascending-dose results by year-end 2026, with Cohort 3 data to follow.1

IMPACT DUCHENNE

First Phase 3 participant dosed

Solid reported first participant dosing in the Phase 3 IMPACT DUCHENNE clinical trial of SGT-003.2

Trial design

Randomized, double-blind Phase 3 study

Solid described IMPACT DUCHENNE as a placebo-controlled, randomized, double-blind trial evaluating a single dose of SGT-003 in ambulatory participants with genetically confirmed Duchenne diagnosis.2

INSPIRE DUCHENNE 472

Participants dosed as of May 11

Solid reported SGT-003 continued to be generally well tolerated in 47 participants dosed in INSPIRE DUCHENNE as of May 11, 2026.2

European regulatory designations were also reported

Solid reported positive opinion on its Pediatric Investigation Plan from the European Medicines Agency's Paediatric Committee and European Commission orphan drug designation for SGT-003.2

Deramiocel
Capricor Therapeutics		 Capricor reported that the deramiocel BLA was under active FDA review with an August 22, 2026 PDUFA target action date and expected labeling discussions soon.3 Capricor reported that HOPE-3 met its primary PUL v2.0 endpoint and all Type I error-controlled secondary endpoints.3 Capricor reported that its GMP manufacturing facility was fully operational and that second-floor expansion was underway.3
z-rostudirsen
Dyne Therapeutics		 Dyne reported a positive pre-BLA meeting with FDA for z-rostudirsen in exon 51 DMD and stated that BLA submission remained on track for Q2 2026.4 Dyne reported average unadjusted dystrophin production of 9.48% of normal and muscle-content-adjusted dystrophin production of 18.33% of normal in four participants treated for at least 12 months.4 Dyne reported plans to initiate a global confirmatory Phase 3 z-rostudirsen trial in Q2 2026.4
