## Reference 1

- Source: FDA, "FDA approves zenocutuzumab-zbco for advanced, unresectable or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma"
- URL: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-zenocutuzumab-zbco-advanced-unresectable-or-metastatic-cholangiocarcinoma
- Visible publication date: On May 8, 2026
- Date screenshot: screenshots/date-verification/source-01-date.png
- Evidence screenshot: screenshots/evidence/source-01-evidence-01.png
- Facts extracted: FDA approved zenocutuzumab-zbco for adults with advanced, unresectable or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma harboring an NRG1 fusion after progression on or after prior systemic therapy; eNRGy enrolled 22 patients with NRG1 fusion-positive cholangiocarcinoma and 19 were evaluable for efficacy; ORR was 36.8% and DOR ranged from 2.8 to 12.9 months.
