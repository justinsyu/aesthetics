Regulatory Approval

FDA added an NRG1 fusion-positive cholangiocarcinoma indication

Source date verified from visible FDA page text: May 8, 2026.1
FDA approved zenocutuzumab-zbco for adults with advanced, unresectable or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma harboring an NRG1 gene fusion with disease progression on or after prior systemic therapy.1

Clinical Development

KRAS G12D and platform-derived programs moved through Phase 1 and Phase 2 planning

Verastem | May 7

VS-7375 registration-directed expansion

Verastem stated that it initiated three Phase 2 registration-directed, open-label trials for VS-7375: TARGET-D 201 in second-line metastatic pancreatic ductal carcinoma, TARGET-D 202 in second- or third-line advanced NSCLC, and TARGET-D 203 in metastatic colorectal cancer.3

Recursion | May 6

REC-1245 and REC-4539 clinical updates

Recursion reported that early REC-1245 clinical data in solid tumors showed a well-tolerated safety profile, predictable dose-dependent pharmacokinetics, and no DLTs observed to date.4

Recursion also reported first patient dosing in the ENLYGHT Phase 1 clinical study for solid tumors including small cell lung cancer for REC-4539.4

ALX2004

ALX stated that the Phase 1 dose-escalation trial of ALX2004, a novel ADC for EGFR-expressing solid tumors, continued enrolling at ascending dose levels and was on track to report safety data in 2H 2026.7

KRAS

RNK08954 and VS-7375
