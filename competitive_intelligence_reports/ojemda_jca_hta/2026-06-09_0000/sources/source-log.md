## Evidence Scope

The review focused on where HEOR-related outputs were used or discussed in the JCA report. The report did not present a cost-effectiveness model, budget-impact model, utility model, or payer-value model. HEOR relevance was identified in clinical evidence generation and appraisal infrastructure: disease burden and epidemiology, systematic information retrieval, literature review and SLR use, population-adjusted indirect comparison methods, external-control feasibility, HRQoL and patient relevance, safety/effectiveness comparability, and additional data-quality requests.

## Included Evidence Types

| Evidence type | How it appeared in the JCA | Key pages / refs |
|---|---|---|
| Epidemiology and burden | The report framed the condition using incidence/prevalence availability, estimated BRAF-altered pediatric LGG incidence, and clinical disease burden | Ref 2, page 15 |
| Systematic retrieval | The report described HTD retrieval, assessor review of search strategies, and completeness checks using study registries and PubMed | Refs 6, 19, 20, 21 |
| Literature review / SLR | The HTD used a literature review, SLR-identified studies, grey literature, and expert input to identify prognostic and effect-modifying variables for the ITC | Refs 11, 12 |
| Indirect treatment comparison | The core comparative evidence was an unanchored MAIC for PICO 5; STC was discussed but not carried out | Refs 4, 9, 10, 13, 15 |
| Real-world / external control evidence | The HTD attempted to construct external control arms from patient data outside clinical studies, but no fit-for-purpose comparator source was identified | Ref 5 |
| HRQoL / patient relevance | HRQoL was requested in scope; patient/carer feedback highlighted missing HRQoL and real-world comparator evidence | Refs 3, 17, 18 |
| Safety/effectiveness data checks | Additional requests focused on comparable ORR analyses, corrected safety analyses, and pooled FIREFLY-1 Arm 1+2 questions | Refs 22, 23, 24 |
