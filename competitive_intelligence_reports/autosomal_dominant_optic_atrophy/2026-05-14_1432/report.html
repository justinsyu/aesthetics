Executive summary

  • Stoke reported that STK-002, an antisense oligonucleotide for OPA1-associated ADOA, was in the Phase 1 OSPREY study; the May 7 release states that the first patient was dosed in February, two patients had been dosed to date, and recruitment was ongoing at seven sites across the UK, Germany, Denmark, and Austria.1
  • The OSPREY study is described as a dose-escalating open-label Phase 1 study in children and adults ages 6 to 55 with an established ADOA diagnosis and a confirmed disease-causing OPA1 variant.1
  • Stoke's 10-Q identifies PYC Therapeutics as a potential competitor that initiated a dose-escalation study for an RNA-based therapy in ADOA; the same 10-Q states that no ADOA treatments were approved at the time of filing.2
