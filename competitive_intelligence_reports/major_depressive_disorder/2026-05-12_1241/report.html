Readout timing

Neumora

Phase 3

Xenon

Xenon stated that azetukalner Phase 3 studies continue to enroll in epilepsy and depression indications and that X-NOVA2 MDD topline data are expected in H1 2027.3

FDA clearance

NRx

NRx stated that FDA cleared initiation of an NRX-101 versus placebo trial in patients with depression and suicidality treated with robotic-assisted or sham TMS.4

  • NRx identified the MIND1 study as a placebo-controlled Phase 2/3 trial in adults with treatment-resistant MDD.4
