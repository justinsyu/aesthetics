- Facts extracted:
  - The article title is Translating advances in primary central nervous system lymphoma: from prognostic stratification to treatment innovation.
  - The abstract describes PCNSL as a rare but aggressive diffuse large B-cell lymphoma confined to the CNS.
  - The abstract states that HD-MTX-based induction regimens including MTR, R-MPV, MATRix, and R-MBVP are the standard first-line approach for fit patients.
  - The abstract states that thiotepa-based high-dose chemotherapy and autologous stem-cell transplantation yields durable disease control in eligible patients.
  - The abstract states that, in relapsed disease, methotrexate rechallenge benefits prior responders, while BTK- or IMiD-based regimens, CAR-T therapy, and focal or whole-brain radiotherapy are under active investigation.
  - The abstract states that maintenance with HD-MTX or targeted agents shows promise but requires validation.
  - The abstract states that long-term survival for elderly and relapsed patients remains unsatisfactory.
