Treatment review

Frontiers in Oncology published a PCNSL treatment review dated May 6, 20263

The article is titled Translating advances in primary central nervous system lymphoma: from prognostic stratification to treatment innovation.3

Disease framing

Rare aggressive DLBCL

The abstract describes PCNSL as a rare but aggressive diffuse large B-cell lymphoma confined to the CNS.3

First line

HD-MTX backbone

The abstract states that HD-MTX-based induction regimens including MTR, R-MPV, MATRix, and R-MBVP are the standard first-line approach for fit patients.3

Consolidation

HDC-ASCT noted

The abstract states that thiotepa-based high-dose chemotherapy and autologous stem-cell transplantation yields durable disease control in eligible patients.3

Relapsed and unresolved areas in the abstract

  • The abstract states that, in relapsed disease, methotrexate rechallenge benefits prior responders, while BTK- or IMiD-based regimens, CAR-T therapy, and focal or whole-brain radiotherapy are under active investigation.3
  • The abstract states that maintenance with HD-MTX or targeted agents shows promise but requires validation.3
  • The abstract states that long-term survival for elderly and relapsed patients remains unsatisfactory.3

The article is titled Central nervous system Burkitt lymphoma: fornix-to-ventricle dissemination and is indexed on the page with keywords including Primary central nervous system lymphoma and Burkitt lymphoma.4

Case details stated in the abstract

  • The abstract states that central nervous system Burkitt lymphoma is exceptionally rare in adolescents without systemic disease.4
  • The abstract states that the case involved a 16-year-old female with progressive headaches and isolated fornix lesions on MRI.4
  • The abstract states that follow-up MRI 50 days later showed multifocal ventricular dissemination and obstructive hydrocephalus, with histopathology and FISH confirming Burkitt lymphoma diagnosis.4
