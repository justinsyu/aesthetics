## References

### Reference 1: Prurigo nodularis economic burden
- Short label: Kwatra et al.
- URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13555-025-01349-7
- Source status/sponsor: 2025 / Dermatol Ther / Sponsor: Regeneron and Sanofi
- Evidence used: US claims analysis reported higher annual health care costs for prurigo nodularis than for matched controls.

### Reference 2: EGPA HCRU and costs
- Short label: Dolin et al.
- URL: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.24.26351614v1
- Source status/sponsor: 2026 / medRxiv preprint / Sponsor: AstraZeneca
- Evidence used: EGPA was associated with markedly higher all-cause costs than matched severe uncontrolled asthma.

### Reference 3: PBC burden by line of therapy
- Short label: Kumar et al.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41335327/
- Source status/sponsor: 2025 / Adv Ther / Sponsor: Ipsen
- Evidence used: PBC treatment-line analysis reported high economic burden and inpatient-cost concentration among untreated or less effectively managed patients.

### Reference 4: PBC ALP elevations and costs
- Short label: Gish et al.
- URL: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/13696998.2025.2595885
- Source status/sponsor: 2025 / J Med Econ / Sponsor: Gilead
- Evidence used: Elevated and highly elevated alkaline phosphatase were associated with higher adjusted costs than normal alkaline phosphatase.

### Reference 5: oHCM longitudinal HCRU and costs
- Short label: Reza et al.
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12643539/
- Source status/sponsor: 2025 / J Med Econ / Sponsor: Cytokinetics
- Evidence used: Five-year oHCM costs were substantial; hospital admissions were a principal medical-cost driver.

### Reference 6: MASH Medicare burden
- Short label: Albarmawi et al.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41719199/
- Source status/sponsor: 2026 / J Med Econ / Sponsor: Novo Nordisk
- Evidence used: MASH was associated with incremental Medicare HCRU and costs compared with matched beneficiaries.

### Reference 7: MMN claims burden

### Reference 8: AATD Medicare Advantage outcomes
- Short label: Khandelwal et al.

### Reference 9: Pompe disease ERT economic burden
- Short label: Steiner et al.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40904046/
- Source status/sponsor: 2025 / J Med Econ / Sponsor: Astellas
- Evidence used: ERT-treated Pompe disease was associated with very high all-cause costs; outpatient and drug-related costs were major components.

### Reference 10: Maribavir pre/post HCRU

- URL: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13696998.2026.2640810
- Source status/sponsor: 2026 / J Med Econ / Sponsor: Takeda
- Evidence used: Medical costs declined after maribavir initiation in refractory or resistant post-transplant CMV.

### Reference 11: Early pimavanserin treatment

- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41589195/
- Source status/sponsor: 2026 / JHEOR / Sponsor: Acadia
