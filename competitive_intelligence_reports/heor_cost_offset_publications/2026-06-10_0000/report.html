Interpretive frame

Economic burden evidence provides context but does not establish treatment cost offset

Burden of illness

Describes cost consequences

Burden analyses quantify medical costs, admissions, emergency-department use, or outpatient utilization associated with a disease state or severity marker.12561213

Treatment association

Closer to offset evidence

Comparative or pre/post treatment analyses provide a more direct, although still usually observational, basis for evaluating whether treatment patterns are associated with lower HCRU or costs.101114151718

Manufacturer use

Supports value framing

These studies can inform payer-relevant hypotheses, endpoint selection, budget-impact assumptions, and health-care economic information when limitations and sponsorship are explicitly disclosed.

Scientific boundary

For treatment value context, claims should match the study design: disease burden, biomarker-linked cost, recurrence cost, or treatment-associated utilization. Causal cost-offset language generally requires stronger evidence than retrospective claims analyses provide.

Evidence map | Rare and underdiagnosed disease

Rare-disease studies emphasize persistent cost, delayed diagnosis, and high-acuity utilization

MMN

Diagnostic and mimic-condition context

A Takeda-sponsored claims analysis reported higher post-index costs for multifocal motor neuropathy than for matched mimic conditions.7 The finding can inform discussions of diagnostic delay, treatment access, and IVIG-related economic context.

AATD with COPD

Acute-care utilization

A Medicare Advantage study reported higher admissions, emergency-department use, and spending in alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency with COPD.8 This supports economic framing around case identification and disease complications.

Pompe disease

Persistent treated-patient cost

In ERT-treated Pompe disease, total all-cause costs were very high, with outpatient and drug-related costs as major components.9 Interpretation should distinguish disease burden from therapy acquisition costs.

NTDT and AGHD

Rare hematologic and endocrine burden

NTDT was associated with higher costs and acute-care use than controls, while confirmed adult growth hormone deficiency had higher costs when GH treatment costs were excluded.1213

Evidence map | Recurrence and progression costs

Recurrence, disease activity, and hospitalization are recurring economic endpoints

MIBC recurrence

Recurrence as economic event

A claims analysis reported that muscle-invasive bladder cancer recurrence was associated with approximately $7,191 higher adjusted medical costs per patient per month.16 The evidence can support recurrence-delay as an economic endpoint when linked to clinical evidence.

PBC ALP

Biomarker-linked burden

Elevated alkaline phosphatase in PBC was associated with higher adjusted costs than normal alkaline phosphatase.4 This supports a biomarker-control value hypothesis, not a direct claim about any one therapy.

oHCM admissions

Hospitalization cost driver

oHCM hospital admissions were identified as a major medical-cost component over five years.5 This provides a measurable economic endpoint for therapies intended to alter symptoms, progression, or decompensation.

Cross-study pattern

The most reusable value-evidence variables were clinically interpretable drivers: admission, emergency care, recurrence, biomarker activity, diagnostic delay, and treatment sequence.

Use in manufacturer treatment support

The publications can inform value-evidence strategy without requiring promotional cost-offset claims

Disease burden

Establish baseline
economic burden

Burden studies can document cost magnitude and resource-use patterns before a therapy-specific economic model is developed.1256781213

Endpoint selection

Identify measurable
cost drivers

Studies that isolate admissions, emergency care, recurrence, biomarker elevation, or skilled-nursing-facility use can guide HCRU endpoints in RWE studies and budget-impact models.451116

Economic modeling

Parameterize assumptions
cautiously

Reported per-patient costs can inform sensitivity analyses, provided the model separately states population, time horizon, payer perspective, and whether drug acquisition costs are included.91415

HCEI context

Support nonpromotional
evidence exchange

For health-care economic information, claims should remain within the study population, comparator, outcome, and design reported by the publication.

Competitive intelligence

Map sponsor evidence
priorities

Manufacturer involvement helps identify which disease pathways, cost drivers, comparators, and outcomes competitors are emphasizing in value-evidence generation.

Evidence gaps

Locate needed follow-up
evidence

Burden studies usually require therapy-specific clinical or real-world evidence before a cost-offset proposition can be stated with confidence.

07 / 10
Evidence strength

Direct treatment-associated analyses should be separated from burden-only publications

More direct

Treatment-associated cost or HCRU changes

Semaglutide, apalutamide, maribavir, pimavanserin, and cariprazine publications reported cost or utilization differences associated with treatment exposure or sequencing.101114151718

Intermediate

Biomarker or event-linked burden

PBC alkaline phosphatase, MIBC recurrence, and oHCM admissions link clinical state or events to cost, providing plausible value pathways when paired with treatment-effect evidence.4516

Contextual

Disease-burden
quantification

PN, EGPA, MASH, MMN, AATD, Pompe, NTDT, and AGHD analyses primarily quantify burden; they do not demonstrate that any specific drug offsets that burden.1267891213

Required caveat

Claims data are vulnerable to selection, coding, severity, channel, and treatment-cost effects. Funding and author affiliations should be retained when these findings are reused.
