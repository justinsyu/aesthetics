Executive summary

The U.S. public record shows outcomes-based or warranty-style access across gene therapies: bluebird bio's LYFGENIA and portfolio payer agreements, Pfizer's BEQVEZ warranty, Orchard's LENMELDY risk-sharing, Abeona's ZEVASKYN value-based agreements, and BioMarin's ROCTAVIAN payer OBAs.125678
Ex-U.S. disclosures are led by HEMGENIX, with England documenting an outcomes-based payment model and Denmark documenting a performance-based model in which regions incur costs only if therapy proves effective.1011
Managed access and reimbursed-access agreements for CASGEVY, Libmeldy, and HEMGENIX are retained as similar access mechanisms, but labeled separately when the public text does not state an outcome-contingent payment mechanism.121517

United States | outcomes-linked

U.S. product-linked agreements emphasize payer risk sharing for high-cost therapies

Rows below include only public source text that explicitly documents an outcomes-based, value-based, warranty, or risk-sharing mechanism.

bluebird | 2023-2024
LYFGENIA and portfolio payer OBAs

bluebird disclosed a December 2023 outcomes-based agreement with an organization representing approximately 100 million covered lives, then disclosed Michigan Medicaid plus four national commercial LYFGENIA OBAs in March 2024.12

Pfizer | 2024
BEQVEZ warranty program

Pfizer reported launch of a warranty program based on durability of patient response, with the stated goal of offering payer financial protection against efficacy failure.5

Orchard | 2024
LENMELDY outcomes/value agreements

Orchard reported work with commercial and government payers to offer outcomes- and value-based agreements intended to share risk and support timely access.6

Abeona | 2025
ZEVASKYN value-based agreements

Abeona reported executed value-based agreements with several commercial payer groups representing downstream plans and approximately 100 million commercially insured lives.7

BioMarin | 2024
ROCTAVIAN third-party payer OBAs

BioMarin reported entering, and planning additional, outcomes-based agreements for ROCTAVIAN with third-party payers to share the risk of one-time treatment.8

Evidence note
Public text often omits financial terms

Most U.S. sources disclose agreement existence and broad mechanism but not contract formulas, patient-level triggers, reconciliation periods, or rebate amounts.

Medicaid activity moved from state-by-state contracting toward a CMS-negotiated CGT model

Ex-U.S. | explicit value or performance linkage

HEMGENIX and antimicrobial access show the clearest ex-U.S. value-based mechanisms

England | HEMGENIX
Outcomes-based payment model

CSL reported HEMGENIX as the first ATMP in the UK to use an innovative outcomes-based payment model under managed access.10

Denmark | HEMGENIX
Performance-based cost exposure

CSL reported Denmark reimburses HEMGENIX under a model in which regions only incur costs if the therapy proves effective over an agreed long-term period.11

Austria | HEMGENIX
Commercial funding
contract

CSL reported a commercial contract to fund HEMGENIX in Austria, retained as a similar access agreement because the public text does not state outcome-contingent payment mechanics.14

NHS England | antimicrobials
Subscription fee delinked from volume

NHS England guidance describes fixed annual fees for antimicrobials based on value to the NHS rather than volumes used.9

Mechanism pattern

The ex-U.S. set shows two different routes: performance-linked contracts for high-cost gene therapy durability uncertainty, and value-scored fixed subscription contracts for antimicrobials where stewardship policy requires volume delinkage.
