# Value-Based Contracts Source Log

Run date: June 8, 2026

Scope: Publicly disclosed, product-linked healthcare access or payment agreements implemented, launched, signed, or active between June 8, 2023 and June 8, 2026. Included mechanisms are outcomes-based contracts, value-based agreements, warranties, performance-based payment, subscription or volume-delinked payment, managed access with commercial access, and reimbursement agreements that are similar enough to inform value-based access intelligence. Broad provider value-based care network contracts were excluded unless tied to a specific medicine, gene therapy, digital therapy, device, or product-access pathway.

Limitations: Many payer-manufacturer agreements are confidential; therefore this is a public-disclosure inventory, not a complete count of all privately executed contracts. Items with live-page evidence screenshots and bounded supporting text are included in the report. Candidate items whose pages blocked Chrome capture, lacked visible support text, were future-dated, or described generic pricing rather than value/outcome risk sharing are documented in `excluded-candidates.md`.
