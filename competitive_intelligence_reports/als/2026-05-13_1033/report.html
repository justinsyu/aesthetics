Registry and access signals

TRICALS ended after no survival benefit. Hoffman awards targeted ALS care access

ClinicalTrials.gov

TRICALS platform trial listed as terminated

ClinicalTrials.gov lists TRICALS platform trial NCT06008249, a Phase 3 ALS treatment platform trial, as terminated and states that the planned interim analysis showed no survival benefit compared with placebo.7

The Record History table lists version 3 submitted 2026-05-12 with Recruitment Status, Study Status, Oversight, Study Design, and Contacts/Locations changes.7

Care access

Hoffman awards targeted multidisciplinary ALS care capacity

The ALS Association announced the newest Hoffman ALS Clinic Development and Capacity Awards, supported by the historic $58 million Hoffman gift, to expand access to multidisciplinary ALS care.6

The same source states that only around half of registered people living with ALS receive multidisciplinary care, a team-based model it describes as extending survival and improving quality of life.6

Decision-relevant distinction

ClinicalTrials.gov documented termination of the Phase 3 ALS treatment platform trial after interim analysis found no survival benefit versus placebo; the ALS Association announcement documented Hoffman awards to expand multidisciplinary ALS care access, not a therapeutic program milestone.76
