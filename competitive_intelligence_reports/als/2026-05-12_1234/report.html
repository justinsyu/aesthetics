AMX0114

Amylyx

ATH-1105

LeonaBio

LeonaBio stated that ATH-1105 was on track to initiate a Phase 2 proof-of-concept study in ALS patients in the second half of 2026.2

INS1202

Insmed

Insmed stated that it continued to enroll patients in the Phase 1 ARMOR study of INS1202, an intrathecally delivered gene therapy for patients with ALS.3

PMN267

ProMIS

ProMIS identified PMN267 as a lead preclinical candidate antibody directed against toxic misfolded TDP-43 for ALS and other TDP-43 proteinopathies.4

  • LUMINA was described as evaluating safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and ALS biomarkers including NfL.1
  • The Insmed disclosure named INS1202 under "Neuro & Other Rare" and described the ARMOR study as Phase 1.3
  • The ProMIS disclosure listed PMN267 under an "Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis Disease Program" heading.4
