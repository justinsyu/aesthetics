Madrigal commercial and pipeline update

Madrigal reported first-quarter Rezdiffra sales and pipeline timing1

Madrigal's release displayed a May 6, 2026 publication date and reported first-quarter 2026 financial results and corporate updates.1

Commercial

Rezdiffra sales

Madrigal reported first-quarter 2026 Rezdiffra net sales of $311.3 million, representing year-over-year growth of 127%.1

Patient count

Patients on therapy

Madrigal stated that more than 42,250 patients were on Rezdiffra as of March 31, 2026, up 2.5x from the first quarter of 2025.1

Pipeline

Clinical-stage siRNA

Madrigal said it advanced its pipeline with a global licensing agreement for a clinical-stage siRNA asset targeting a PNPLA3 gene mutation described as a genetically validated driver of MASH.1

Additional recorded pipeline timing

  • Madrigal stated that the MGL-2086 oral GLP-1 Phase 1 trial was on track to initiate in the second quarter of 2026.1
  • Madrigal stated that an ervogastat/resmetirom drug-drug interaction study was on track to initiate in the fourth quarter of 2026.1
  • Madrigal reported cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and marketable securities of $817.9 million as of March 31, 2026.1
