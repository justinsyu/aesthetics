Company pipeline updates

SEC exhibits described PV-directed JAK2V617F and siRNA programs12

Both company sources were filed or dated inside the May 6-12, 2026 evidence window.12

Prelude

PRT12396 Phase 1 enrollment initiated

Prelude stated that it initiated enrollment of a Phase 1 study of PRT12396, a mutant-selective JAK2V617F inhibitor, in patients with polycythemia vera and myelofibrosis.1

The same release states that the JAK2V617F mutation impacts approximately 95% of PV patients and that Prelude's JAK2V617F inhibitor program is subject to an exclusive option agreement with Incyte.1

Silence

SANRECO divesiran topline guided for August 2026

Silence stated that its Phase 2 SANRECO trial of divesiran, described by the company as a first-in-class siRNA for PV, remained on track for topline results in August 2026.2

The release describes SANRECO as a double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2 study evaluating divesiran 6 mg Q6W and Q12W in 48 phlebotomy-dependent PV patients.2

Additional pipeline fact

Silence also stated that a Phase 1 SANRECO abstract was accepted as a poster presentation at the EHA 2026 Congress on June 12, 2026.2

Publications and real-world evidence

Recent literature records addressed ruxolitinib use and thrombosis outcomes in PV56

The publication records carried visible dates of May 12, 2026 and May 7, 2026.56

PaVe study

Ruxolitinib German real-world data

The PubMed record identifies the Annals of Hematology article as a prospective, non-interventional study of ruxolitinib therapy in patients with polycythaemia vera using German PaVe study data.5

The abstract reports 433 PV patients, with 62% JAK-inhibitor pretreated and 38% not JAK-inhibitor pretreated, and states that no new safety concerns were observed.5

Thrombosis outcomes

Blood Cancer Journal PV cohort

The PMC XML for the Blood Cancer Journal article gives an epub date of May 7, 2026 and describes an ET/PV thrombosis analysis; the PV cohort included 2,302 patients with median follow-up of 5.7 years.6

In the PV cohort, the article reports 318 thrombotic events in 252 of 2,302 patients, or 10.9%, and reports that arterial thrombosis was associated with increased risk of death, HR 1.47, 95% CI 1.12-1.92, p=0.005.6

Additional outcome facts

  • The Blood Cancer Journal article reports post-PV myelofibrosis in 147 patients, or 6.4%, and second cancers after PV in 206 patients, or 8.9%.6
  • The article states that thrombotic events were associated with increased probability of second cancer in patients with PV.6
