Publications

Recent literature addressed PV iron homeostasis, ruxolitinib real-world data, and thrombosis-associated outcomes789

Iron biology

Blood Cancer Journal review

PV is a myeloproliferative neoplasm caused by a JAK2 mutation, and iron deficiency is common in PV7

Hepcidin mimetics or ferroportin inhibitors aim to restore iron homeostasis and may reduce the need for cytoreductive therapy in low-risk PV patients7

Real-world data

German PaVe study

PaVe is a prospective, non-interventional German real-world study of ruxolitinib therapy in PV8

PaVe included 433 PV patients, with 62% pretreated with a JAK inhibitor and 38% not pretreated; no new safety concerns were observed8

Thrombosis outcomes

PV cohort survival analysis

A PV cohort of 2,302 patients had median follow-up of 5.7 years and 318 thrombotic events in 252 patients, or 10.9%9

Arterial thrombosis was associated with increased risk of death in PV, HR 1.47, 95% CI 1.12-1.92, p=0.0059
