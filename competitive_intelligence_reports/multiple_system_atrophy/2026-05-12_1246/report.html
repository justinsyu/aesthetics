03 / 06
Clinical literature

New articles reported MSA prognostic markers and a broader treatment-landscape review45

The Oxford Brain article displayed a publication date of May 7, 2026, and the Wiley systematic review displayed a first-published date of May 9, 2026.45

Brain | prognostic indicators

555-patient MSA cohort

The Brain article reported survival prognostic factors in a cohort of 555 MSA patients, including 254 post-mortem confirmed people and 318 clinically diagnosed PROSPECT-M-UK participants.4

  • The combined cohort had mean onset age of 58.7 +/- 9.0 years and median survival of 8.25 years.4
  • Median survival after indoor wheelchair use, gastrostomy insertion, or development of unintelligible speech was consistently less than 1.5 years.4
CPT | systematic review

Disease-modifying therapy landscape

The Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics review stated that parkinsonian disorders including MSA are progressive neurodegenerative conditions with no treatment options to slow disease progression.5

  • The review stated that most clinical development has focused on Parkinson's disease, with limited progress in other parkinsonian disorders.5
  • The review stated that no disease-modifying therapy had been approved for any parkinsonian disorder despite substantial research efforts.5

Recorded endpoint and stratification context

The Brain article said in-clinic rating scales and clinical milestone assessment can aid MSA prognostication, and the CPT review stated that biomarker-driven approaches and stratified trial designs are needed to demonstrate disease modification.45

Journal of Neural Transmission

Cognitive and behavioral features

The indexed abstract stated that cognitive and behavioral disturbances are increasingly recognized as core features of PSP and MSA.6

  • For MSA, the abstract stated that cognitive impairment is typically milder and more variable and most commonly involves executive dysfunction.6
  • The abstract stated that apathy and depression are frequent but underrecognized behavioral symptoms in MSA.6
