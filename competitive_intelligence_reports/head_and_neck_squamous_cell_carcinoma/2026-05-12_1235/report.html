Trial population and design

The Phase 2 HexAgon portion evaluated INBRX-106, a hexavalent OX40 agonist, with pembrolizumab versus pembrolizumab monotherapy in first-line treatment-naive, PD-L1 positive (CPS >=20) metastatic or unresectable recurrent HNSCC.1

The randomized Phase 2 portion enrolled 68 patients, including 33 assigned to the combination arm and 35 assigned to pembrolizumab alone.1

Disclosed next steps

Inhibrx stated that Phase 2 progression-free survival data are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and that the Phase 3 portion of HexAgon is planned to begin during the third quarter of 2026.1

The same release reported three complete radiographic responses in the combination arm and no complete responses with pembrolizumab alone.1
