How Evidence Keeps It Calm

Not tame. Managed.

Value

Clinical plus economic logic

Define who benefits, why the comparator is fair, which outcomes matter, and where uncertainty still needs an honest label.

Budget

Transparent and interrogable

Give payers inputs they can pressure-test: population, uptake, offsets, utilization, discontinuation, scenarios, and sensitivity ranges.

Reality

Post-launch evidence loop

Use real-world evidence to confirm utilization, outcomes, safety, adherence, and the assumptions that got coverage moving.
