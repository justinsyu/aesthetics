## Source Interpretation Notes

- FDA promotional rules and guidance are the highest-priority sources for U.S. prescription drug/biologic/device promotional materials.
- FTC substantiation and endorsement guidance is important for broader consumer-facing health claims and testimonial/influencer content, including content that is not strictly FDA-regulated promotion.
- Payer/HEOR documents require a separate audience gate. The FDA HCEI/payor guidance and AMCP Format 5.0 are the most directly relevant sources found for payer-facing evidence packages.
- Vendor sources are useful for workflow design, role definitions, and operational controls, but they should not override company SOPs or primary regulatory requirements.
