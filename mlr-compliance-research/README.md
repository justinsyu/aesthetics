# MLR Compliance Research Package

Access date: June 3, 2026

This folder collects public sources and working notes on how Medical, Legal, Regulatory (MLR) review is commonly used to evaluate stakeholder-facing pharmaceutical, medical-device, HEOR, payer, Medical Affairs, patient, HCP, and broader health-product communications.

## Contents

- `source-log.md` - curated URL/source log with relevance notes, audience implications, and local-download status.
- `mlr-review-checklist.md` - practical synthesis and checklist for evaluating whether a document is ready for MLR review and approval.
- `download-log.csv` - direct-document download inventory and failed URL-only attempts.
- `subagent_regulatory_sources.md` - raw regulatory-source notes created by the research subagent.
- `documents/` - downloaded public PDFs and vendor documents that were accessible without credentials.
- `sources/` - reserved for future extracted source text or screenshots.

## Use Notes

MLR compliance is context-specific. The checklist here is a research aid, not legal advice or a substitute for a company's SOPs, product labeling, review committee decisions, or counsel/regulatory sign-off.

For a real document review, first classify the audience, channel, product status, geography, and intended use of the document. Then evaluate every express and implied claim against approved labeling, substantiation, fair-balance/risk requirements, audience limitations, and required submission or archival controls.
