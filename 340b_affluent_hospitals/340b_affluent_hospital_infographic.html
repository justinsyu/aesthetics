Measurement limits

This is a location-screening infographic, not a 340B compliance or benefit-use analysis. ZCTAs are Census statistical approximations of ZIP delivery areas; hospital systems may serve patients from many ZIP codes, and 340B eligibility can depend on entity type, ownership or contract status, DSH percentage, and other program-specific requirements.13
