An evidence package is a structure: load-bearing, or it falls.

HEOR is a core ingredient in the cement. And the rebar.

The top floor is what should be available at launch: dossiers (GVD, AMCP), HTA submissions, FDA submissions, and field tools. Beneath it, four levels of HEOR work carry the load. The foundation is poured before the trial begins, in burden of illness, unmet need, and the target product profile. Each floor above locks in load-bearing decisions: payer landscape, endpoints, comparators, the cost-effectiveness model. Skip a level, and the load path breaks. The whole tower fails, not just the floor you skipped.
