treated with anti-VEGF therapy in the United States
--- Page 7 ---
Methods
National Registry (IRIS, AAO)
Patients diagnosed with Neovascular AMD between January 1, 2013 - 
December 31, 2015 and treated with anti-VEGF therapy from January 1, 2013 
to December 31, 2018.
Loss to Follow Up was deﬁned as visit greater than 12 months from last 
intravitreal injection 
Multivariable logistic regression analysis involving baseline demographic and 
clinical conditions were utilized to determine odds ratios (OR) and 95% 
conﬁdence intervals (CI).
