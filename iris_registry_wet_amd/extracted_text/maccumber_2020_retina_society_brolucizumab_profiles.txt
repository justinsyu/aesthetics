Background
4AMD, age-related macular degeneration; FDA, Food and Drug Administration; US, United States
Brolucizumab(BEOVU®) was approved by the US FDA in October 2019 for the treatment of wet AMD
Limited evidence exists on real-world patient characteristics, outcomes, and treatment patterns of patients with wet AMD who initiated brolucizumab
This study adds to the understanding of brolucizumab patients in clinical practice, with a focus on how they were treated prior to initiating brolucizumab
