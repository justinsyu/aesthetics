01·A · A BLUEPRINT FOR THE EVIDENCE PACKAGE
01·A/ Variant
HEOR · Variant A · The Blueprint

You don't pour the
foundation last.

An evidence package is a structure: load-bearing, or it falls.

The top floor is what the launch ships: dossiers (GVD, AMCP), HTA submissions, FDA submissions (PCOR), and field tools. Beneath it, four levels of HEOR work carry the load. The foundation is poured before the trial begins, in burden of illness, unmet need, and the target product profile. Each floor above locks in load-bearing decisions: payer landscape, endpoints, comparators, the cost-effectiveness model. Skip a level, and the load path breaks. The whole tower fails, not just the floor you skipped.
