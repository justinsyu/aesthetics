• Improvements in BVA were greater in eyes with worse vision compared with 
those with better vision; those with worse vision and long-term follow-up 
maintained visual improvement
• Improvements in BVA from baseline were greatest in eyes that received 
≥ 5 injections each year
• The adjusted model demonstrated Black and Hispanic patients, as well as 
patients with Medicaid insurance and diagnosis of glaucoma, were associated 
with BVA decline from baseline to year 1 of follow-up
• These results will better equip ophthalmologists in their clinical decision-making 
and management of patients with DME
