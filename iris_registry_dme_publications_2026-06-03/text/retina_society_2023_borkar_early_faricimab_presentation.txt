Several Real-World Studies of Faricimab in DME are Ongoing

Faricimab (Vabysmo™) was approved in the US in January 2022 and Great Britain in May 2022 for DME treatment1,2

Faricimab is the first bispecific antibody for intraocular use that independently binds and neutralizes both Ang-2 and 
VEGF-A with high specificity and potency3

Several ongoing studies are collecting evidence on real-world faricimab treatment patterns and outcomes4-7:
