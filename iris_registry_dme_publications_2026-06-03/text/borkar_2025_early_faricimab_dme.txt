FARETINA-DME was a retrospective study among patients with DME initiating faricimab from February 2022 to June 2023 identified from the United States IRIS &#xae; Registry. Four thousand five hundred fourteen (4,514) patients (6,204 eyes) previously treated with anti-vascular endothelial growth factor (anti-VEGF) therapy and 691 treatment-na&#xef;ve patients (851 eyes) were included. In previously treated eyes, mean &#xb1; SD visual acuity was 64.0 &#xb1; 18.3 letters at index and 65.3 &#xb1; 19.0 at faricimab injection 4. In treatment-na&#xef;ve eyes, visual acuity improved from 60.3 &#xb1; 19.8 to 63.9 &#xb1; 18.5 letters ( P < 0.01). Mean &#xb1; SD central subfield thickness improved from 364.3 &#xb1; 132.2 to 330.1 &#xb1; 121.2 &#x3bc;m in previously treated eyes and 359.6 &#xb1; 115.0 to 307.4 &#xb1; 114.6 &#x3bc;m in treatment-na&#xef;ve eyes (both P < 0.01). In patients with DME receiving faricimab, visual acuity was maintained in previously treated and improved in treatment-na&#xef;ve eyes. Both groups had anatomical improvement.
