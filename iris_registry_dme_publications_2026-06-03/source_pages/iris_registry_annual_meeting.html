  • AAO 2017 Events to Help You Succeed with the IRIS Registry

  • Current and potential IRIS® Registry users can learn more about the registry's benefits and powerful tools during several sessions at AAO 2017 in New Orleans, Nov. 11 to 14. For complete descriptions of these sessions, use the online program search. You can either search by keyword, course number or session category. Once in the online program, you can then add events to your calendar. 

    Featured Events

    Saturday, Nov. 11

    • The Value of the IRIS Registry: What Can We Learn From 100 Million Patient Records? | SYM03
      12:15-1:15 p.m., 255-257
      The IRIS Registry is enabling studies on practice patterns, factors affecting clinical outcomes, complications and possible risk factors for disease development. 
      Access: Free

    Sunday, Nov. 12

    • Treatment Patterns for Diabetic Macular Edema in the United States: Analysis of the IRIS Registry | PA017
      11:39-11:46 a.m., 255-257
      Purpose: To characterize treatment patterns surrounding incident diabetic macular edema in the United States.
      Access: Free
    • Real-World Vision in AMD Patients Treated with Bevacizumab, Ranibizumab, or Aflibercept Only Over 1 Year in the IRIS Registry  | PO189
      12:45-1:45 p.m., Hall C
      Purpose: To compare real-world vision in neovascular AMD patients treated with a single anti–vascular endothelial growth factor drug type for one year in the IRIS Registry cohort.
      Access: Free

    • How the IRIS Registry Helps You Participate in the Merit-Based Incentive Payment System | 260
      3:15-4:15 p.m., 290
      This course will help participants understand how to use the IRIS Registry to successfully participate in MIPS.
      Access: Included in course pass

    Monday, Nov. 13

    • The Medicare Access and CHIP Reauthorization Act: What the New Changes Mean to Your Patients and Your Practice | SYM30
      10:15-11:45 a.m., New Orleans Theater C
      Does MACRA represent an improvement over the flawed SGR formula, or more of the same, with even more complex hurdles?
      Access: Free
    • Research to Prevent Blindness – Academy IRIS Registry Grant Program | SPE25
      10:15-11:15 a.m., 338
      This provides the opportunity to leverage big data and to perform analyses on real-world evidence that is representative of current clinical practice in the United States. 
      Access: Free
    • IRIS® Registry Dashboard and Analytics Demonstration - how too track performance and evaluate patient outcomes for practice improvement (Part IV of ABO and MIPS) | TECH17
      11:30 a.m. to Noon, Booth 5347
      Learn how to compare your own performance to peers within your practice, compare yourself to national benchmarks and run analytic reports.
      Access: Free

    Tuesday, Nov. 14

    • Return to the OR after Macular Surgery: IRIS Registry Analysis | PA100
      11:15-11:22 a.m., 255-257
      Purpose: To determine the rate of secondary surgery within one year after macular surgery.
      Access: Free
