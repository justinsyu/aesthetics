The evidence never breaks character. A field-guide poster for teams trying to get from approval to actual access.

Operation: Value Story LZ Access
Hot Zone: Payer Review
LZ Clinical approval gets the squad on the map. HEOR marks the extraction route to coverage, uptake, and value defense.
Mission Brief
No
Improv
at Launch

US access teams need more than a dramatic pivotal readout. Payers,
P&T committees, IDNs, clinicians, and patients need a disciplined
evidence package that explains who benefits, what value looks like,
what budget impact looks like, and how the story holds up in
real-world care decisions.

The Operation

Four scenes before the access review
Scene 01

Write the Script Early

Define the value story before the cameras roll.

Build HEOR input into endpoint strategy, comparator choice, subgroup planning, PROs, and evidence gaps while the clinical program can still adapt.

Phase 2 / Phase 3 planning
Scene 02

Always Scout the Terrain

Know who is waiting in the jungle.

Map US payer archetypes, PBM dynamics, IDN evidence standards, Medicare exposure, expected restrictions, and the questions each group will press hardest.

T-minus 24 to 18 months
Scene 03

Load the Evidence Kit ASAP

Do not show up with one prop.

Prepare burden of illness, budget impact, comparative
evidence, AMCP dossier content, model assumptions,
and field-ready value tools before
launch pressure peaks.

T-minus 18 to 6 months
Scene 04

Extract Real-World Proof

Keep filming after the premiere.

Use claims, EHR, registry, adherence, safety,
and utilization data to test assumptions,
update models, support contracting, and
defend ongoing access.

Launch through lifecycle

Radio Check

Three signals that must come through clearly

Value

Differentiated and defensible

Evidence of incremental benefit, transparent tradeoffs, and value that survives standard-of-care comparison.

Budget

PMPM, offsets, uncertainty

A model payers can interrogate without losing trust: population size, uptake, displacement, offsets, scenarios, and sensitivity analyses.

Proof

Real-world follow-through

Post-launch evidence that verifies utilization, outcomes, safety, adherence, and economic assumptions after the first access decision.
