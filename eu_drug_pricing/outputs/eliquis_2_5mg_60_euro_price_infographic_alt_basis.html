What this version changes

The main section foregrounds the country field used rather than treating the series as a clean ranking. The prices remain visible, but basis differences drive interpretation.

Needed before like-for-like use

  • Confirm each national field definition, including VAT, fees, wholesale margins, and reimbursement-list rules
  • Convert every row to one basis, such as ex-factory, pharmacy retail, or reimbursed amount
  • Account for discounts, rebates, and net-price arrangements absent from public files
  • Document currency treatment before adding Romania or Sweden
Source and limitation

Input: eu_drug_pricing/analysis/eliquis_2_5mg_60_price_extract.csv. Included rows have included_in_chart = yes and currency = EUR for Eliquis (apixaban) 2.5 mg, 60 tablets. Prices are official source-listed pack fields, not patient out-of-pocket prices, confidential net prices, procurement prices, or harmonized cross-country reimbursement comparisons. Romania and Sweden were present in the extract but excluded here because the rows are RON/SEK and no currency conversion was applied.
