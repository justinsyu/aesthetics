What is needed before comparing levels

The published files answer “what does this source list?” They do not, by themselves, standardize the economics.

Normalize price basis Convert retail, public, reimbursement-list, and wholesale fields to a defined target basis.
Align tax and margins Remove or add VAT, pharmacy margins, wholesale margins, and dispensing fees consistently.
Validate pack equivalence Confirm strength, unit count, dose form, and parallel import status are treated consistently.
Separate list from net Published list prices may exclude confidential rebates, tender discounts, or payer-specific terms.
