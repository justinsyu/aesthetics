# Eliquis 2.5 mg x 60 EU price extract

Target product: Eliquis (apixaban) 2.5 mg, 60-tablet pack. The chart uses euro-denominated rows where the exact product/strength/pack appears in downloaded official datasets. Romania and Sweden are extracted but excluded from the chart because values are in RON/SEK and were not converted.

Important limitation: public sources use different price bases. The comparison is a source-listed pack-price comparison, not a harmonized net-price or purchasing-power comparison. Bases include maximum retail price with VAT, pharmacy/public price, final reimbursement-list price, and wholesale price.

Downloaded but not used in chart: interactive-only countries, files without the target record, non-pricing Malta file, and unresolved Poland/Belgium direct downloads.
