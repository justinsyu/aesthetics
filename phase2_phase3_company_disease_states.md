# Company Phase 2/3 Disease-State Review
Source date: May 11, 2026.

## Scope and Method
- Scope: the companies supplied by the user.
- Source rule: official company pipeline, product, clinical-trial, investor, or company pages. Non-official sources were used only, where needed, to locate official pages.
- Inclusion rule: company-labeled Phase 2, Phase 2b, Phase 2/3, Phase IIb/III, pivotal Phase 2/3, Phase 3, Phase 3b, or equivalent current late-stage program labels.
- Exclusion rule: approved-only products, diagnostics-only products, non-therapeutic devices, nutrition products, care-delivery services, historical completed programs, NDA/BLA submission-only assets, or programs with no disclosed Phase 2/3 or pivotal status were not counted unless the company page still presented them as Phase 2/3 or pivotal development programs.
- Caveat: some company pages use broad labels such as "early stage clinical development" or "late stage clinical development" rather than numeric phases. Those are noted in the table rather than over-normalized.
