33471912 2021 11 24 2021 11 24 2325-8179 52 1 2021 Jan 01 Ophthalmic surgery, lasers & imaging retina Ophthalmic Surg Lasers Imaging Retina Increasing Incidence and Prevalence of Common Retinal Diseases in Retina Practices Across the United States. 29 36 29-36 10.3928/23258160-20201223-06 To provide an updated estimate of incidence and prevalence of the foremost retinal diseases in the U.S. Retrospective study of the Vestrum Health Database evaluating eyes with diagnoses of wet or dry age-related macular degeneration (AMD), diabetic macular edema (DME), diabetic retinopathy (DR), branch or central retinal vein occlusion (BRVO; CRVO) from January 2014 to December 2019 across 58 retina practices. Of the 3,086,791 eyes examined, 490,881 (15.9%) had dry AMD, 294,041 (9.5%) wet AMD, 270,703 (8.8%) DME, 254,690 (8.3%) DR without DME, 73,617 (2.4%) BRVO, and 50,670 (1.6%) CRVO. Dry AMD had the highest incidence. These diseases comprised 61.0% of total prevalence and 54.3% of incidence among patients at the retina practices analyzed. Based on a diverse database, these diseases comprised the majority of U.S. retina practice cases, with increasing annual incidences. AMD is the most common diagnosis, then diabetic eye disease.
