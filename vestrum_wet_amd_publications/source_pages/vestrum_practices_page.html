Clinical Trial Recruitment Acceleration Program (RAP)

Clinical Trial Accelerated Patient Recruitment is a service for industry-sponsored trials available to practices in the Vestrum Health panel

  • Quantify patient recruitment estimates for contract negotiations

  • Accelerate the patient recruitment process

  • Vestrum’s list replaces the need for manual file searches improving efficiency

  • Hit trial completion deadlines with faster recruitment

  • Become a preferred practice-site for trial sponsors

  • Measure the efficiency of your clinical trial process

Identifying patients for clinical trials enables your practice to reduce recruitment times. Vestrum can analyze your data and identify patients who meet study criteria. Our HIPAA-compliant process allows you to re-identify these patients. Clinical trial staff can call identified patients in for further assessment and enrollment where candidates fit the trial criteria.

Reduce your costs, increase trial revenue and enhance trial capacity through faster and higher levels of patient enrollment.

As a Vestrum Health panel physician, this is a complimentary service.
