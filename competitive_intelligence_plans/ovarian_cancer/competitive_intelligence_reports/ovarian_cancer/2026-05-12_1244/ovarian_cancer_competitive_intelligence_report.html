Company-disclosed modality signals

Cell therapy and oncolytic-virus updates sit beside registry movement

The two company releases in the evidence window were not ClinicalTrials.gov records: Ernexa reported ERNA-101 preclinical ovarian cancer model findings on May 6, and Genelux provided an Olvi-Vec Phase 3 business update on May 7.12

Preclinical | ERNA-101

Ernexa reported ovarian cancer model activity with anti-PD-1 combination therapy

  • The release describes ERNA-101 as an allogeneic induced mesenchymal stem cell therapy engineered to home to tumors and secrete IL-7 and IL-15.1
  • The release says ERNA-101 plus anti-PD-1 resulted in complete tumor clearance and 100% survival through long-term follow-up in ovarian cancer models.1
Phase 3 | Olvi-Vec

Genelux reiterated second-half 2026 topline timing for OnPrime/GOG-3076

  • The release identifies OnPrime/GOG-3076 as a randomized Phase 3 ovarian cancer registrational trial of Olvi-Vec.2
  • The release states the trial remains on track for topline data in the second half of 2026.2

Evidence-status contrast

Ernexa’s release reports preclinical model results, while Genelux’s release reports timing for a randomized Phase 3 registrational trial.12

Why it matters for surveillance
