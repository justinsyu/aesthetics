# ASCO 2026 Congress Intelligence Data Dictionary

Generated by `scripts/build_asco_intelligence_data.py` from the immutable local ASCO corpus at `ASCO-2026-Abstracts`.

## Core Outputs

- `generated_data/normalized_abstracts.jsonl`: one normalized JSON record per ASCO abstract.
- `generated_data/abstract_facts.csv`: lightweight tabular index for spreadsheet review.
- `generated_data/priority_signals.json`: top 250 scored congress-intelligence signals.
- `generated_data/dashboard_data.json`: compact data bundle used by `index.html`.
- `generated_data/role_outputs/*.json` and `*.csv`: role-specific queues for Medical Affairs, HEOR, Market Access, Commercial, and Launch.
- `generated_data/source_log.json`: source URLs, local raw files, and checksums.
- `generated_data/qa_summary.json`: reconciliation and completeness counts.

## Normalized Abstract Fields

- `uid`, `contentId`, `presentationId`: ASCO/API identifiers.
- `abstractNumber`: ASCO abstract number, including `LBA`, `TPS`, numeric, and `e` formats.
- `title`, `primaryPerson`, `meetingName`, `meetingYear`, `url`: source-facing citation fields.
- `sessionType`, `track`: derived from ASCO `relatedMaterials`; records without related material are labeled `Publication Only`.
- `summary`: ASCO summary when present, otherwise a truncated background fallback for internal triage.
- `sections`: extracted `background`, `methods`, `results`, and `conclusions` text.
- `plainTextLength`, `tableCount`, `hasSummary`: extraction completeness fields.
- `hasPosterFlag`, `hasSlideFlag`, `hasVideoFlag`: ASCO media flags from the downloaded record.
- `priorityScore`: heuristic score for triage, not an evidence conclusion.
- `evidenceTags`: detected tags such as `Late breaking`, `Phase 3`, `Randomized`, `Patient-reported outcomes`, and `Real-world evidence`.
- `roleRelevance`: keyword-based score by role.
- `recommendedRoles`: role queues where the record should be reviewed.

## Review Standard

Priority scores and tags are triage aids. Any claim promoted into a brief, dashboard, or field-facing output requires human review against the ASCO source URL and any enrichment sources.
