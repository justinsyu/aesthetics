# Learnings

This file captures durable, disease-specific feedback and workflow lessons for future AI agents working in this folder.

- For Alzheimer's disease competitive intelligence slide revisions, review rendered slide images for excessive blank space or padding and tighten layouts where possible before delivery.
- Use objective, scientifically neutral wording suitable for a medical journal; avoid promotional or overstated language in Alzheimer’s disease slide copy.
- When space allows, prioritize larger main body text for readability, and do not use periods at the ends of slide titles.
- Treat key-takeaway subtext and pathway-card copy as high-priority readability targets; keep key-takeaway subtext concise enough to fit within two lines on early PDF pages, and enlarge these component fonts before delivery when layout space allows.
- For cohere-style-ci reruns in this folder, start a fresh evidence workflow with fresh source-review/audit subagents and enforce the skill's last-7-days window rather than carrying forward older report evidence.
