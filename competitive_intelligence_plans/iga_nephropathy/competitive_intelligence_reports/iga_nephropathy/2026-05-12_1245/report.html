Atacicept | Vera Therapeutics

Vera reported priority-review timing and mid-2026 launch preparation for atacicept in IgAN1

Vera's May 7, 2026 business update is the only company press-release source included in this report.1

FDA timing

PDUFA date

Vera stated that FDA granted priority review to the atacicept BLA for accelerated approval in adult patients with IgAN and assigned a PDUFA date of July 7, 2026.1

Launch timing

Mid-2026

Vera stated that it was on track for a U.S. commercial launch of atacicept in mid-2026, pending regulatory approval.1

Milestones

Q2 and Q1

Vera listed initial PIONEER results expected in Q2 2026 and pivotal two-year ORIGIN 3 eGFR data expected in Q1 2027.1

Additional source-stated context

  • Vera stated that FDA granted priority review to the atacicept BLA for treatment of IgAN in adults and assigned a PDUFA target action date of July 7, 2026.1
  • Vera stated that the planned U.S. commercial launch of atacicept was expected mid-2026, pending FDA approval.1
