# Learnings

This file captures durable, disease-specific feedback and workflow lessons for future AI agents working in this folder.

- For ClinicalTrials.gov updates, inspect Record History and include only substantive changes worth mentioning to a leadership audience; exclude records where recent activity is limited to administrative status, site, contact, or location maintenance.
- Viewer-facing report text should describe scientific or business facts directly. Avoid source-mechanics phrasing such as "The PubMed abstract...", "The PMC XML...", "The article reports...", or "The record lists..." unless the source type itself is the relevant fact.
