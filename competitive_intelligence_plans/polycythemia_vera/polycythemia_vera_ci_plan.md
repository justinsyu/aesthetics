# Polycythemia Vera Competitive Intelligence Website Search Plan

Prepared: 2026-05-11

Disease state: Polycythemia vera (PV)

Purpose: Provide a current, credible website search plan for PV competitive intelligence. This is a source and monitoring plan, not a CI findings report.

## CI objectives

- Track PV treatment-landscape changes across low-risk, high-risk, phlebotomy-dependent, hydroxyurea-resistant/intolerant, interferon-treated, ruxolitinib-treated, symptomatic, thrombosis-risk, post-PV myelofibrosis, and disease-progression settings.
- Detect competitor events early: new trial starts, protocol amendments, enrollment changes, primary-completion updates, topline results, conference abstracts, peer-reviewed publications, FDA/EMA/PMDA/Health Canada/MHRA actions, label changes, safety communications, guideline updates, and payer-policy changes.
- Monitor current and emerging mechanisms relevant to PV: JAK1/2 inhibition, interferon/ropeginterferon, hydroxyurea cytoreduction, hepcidin mimetics, selective JAK2V617F approaches, MDM2 inhibitors, LSD1/epigenetic approaches, BET inhibition, iron metabolism, and combinations with standard cytoreductive therapy.
- Track evidence generation by endpoints that matter in PV: hematocrit control, phlebotomy independence, complete hematologic response, symptom burden, thrombosis/bleeding, spleen volume, JAK2 allele burden, disease progression to myelofibrosis or AML, safety/tolerability, quality of life, and real-world persistence.
- Monitor access and commercialization signals, including NCCN/compendia placement, prior authorization criteria, step therapy, specialty pharmacy requirements, Part D formulary placement, HTA recommendations, patient assistance, copay programs, launch language, and field-facing education.
- Build a repeatable search process that prioritizes primary sources, separates factual updates from sponsor claims, and flags uncertainty when evidence is only available from secondary or promotional channels.

- Rusfertide/PTG-300: prioritize Takeda, Protagonist, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA, EMA, ASH, EHA, ASCO, SOHO, PubMed.
- Ropeginterferon alfa-2b/Besremi/P1101: prioritize PharmaEssentia, FDA, EMA, PMDA, NCCN, ASH, EHA, PubMed, HTA agencies, payer policies.
- Ruxolitinib/Jakafi/Jakavi/Jakafi XR: prioritize Incyte, Novartis, FDA, EMA, NCCN, NICE, payer policies, PubMed, ASH/EHA real-world studies.
- Peginterferon alfa-2a/Pegasys: prioritize EMA, Roche/Genentech, guidelines, payer policies, shortage/access notices, PubMed.
- Hydroxyurea/hydroxycarbamide: prioritize guidelines, payer policies, labels, real-world treatment-pattern studies, and comparative studies.
- Selective JAK2V617F, MDM2, LSD1, BET, and other disease-modifying approaches: prioritize ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, ASH/EHA/AACR, company pipelines, SEC filings, and reputable academic centers.

## Source-quality notes

- Highest-confidence sources are primary regulatory pages, official labels, trial registry records, guideline bodies, peer-reviewed publications, HTA agencies, and SEC/company filings.
- Company press releases are primary for sponsor intent and stated milestones, but promotional for efficacy/safety interpretation. Confirm pivotal claims against trial registries, labels, conference abstracts, or peer-reviewed papers.
- ClinicalTrials.gov and CTIS are sponsor-reported and may lag real-world enrollment or regulatory status. Treat record changes as signals that require confirmation.
- Conference abstracts are credible but preliminary; check whether data are encore, interim, subgroup, post hoc, or late-breaking and whether a peer-reviewed manuscript follows.
- Patient advocacy and foundation sites are valuable for patient burden, registry activity, education, access concerns, and expert events. Do not treat patient-facing summaries as substitutes for labels, guidelines, or peer-reviewed data.
- MPN Hub and similar educational sites can be useful for triage and conference coverage, but always review sponsorship disclosures and verify against primary abstracts, journals, or regulators.
- FAERS, EudraVigilance, and pharmacovigilance dashboards are signal-generation tools only. They cannot estimate incidence or causality without formal analysis.
- Payer policy documents vary by line of business and geography. Record plan name, plan year, effective date, and whether criteria apply to commercial, Medicare Advantage, Part D, Medicaid, or exchange products.
- Use both "polycythemia vera" and "polycythaemia vera." For German and other EU sources, also search product names and "Ruxolitinib," "Ropeginterferon alfa-2b," "Rusfertide," "Polyzythaemia vera," and "PV."
- Distinguish PV from secondary polycythemia, essential thrombocythemia, primary myelofibrosis, post-PV myelofibrosis, and broader myeloproliferative neoplasm results before logging competitive findings.
