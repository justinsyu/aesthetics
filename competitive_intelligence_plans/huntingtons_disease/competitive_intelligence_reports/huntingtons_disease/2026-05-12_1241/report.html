Votoplam / INVEST-HD

Votoplam has a trial-record update and a sponsor-readout disclosure in the same evidence window

770

Estimated Phase 3 enrollment

ClinicalTrials.gov lists INVEST-HD as a recruiting Phase 3 interventional study sponsored by Novartis Pharmaceuticals, with estimated enrollment of 770.1

Primary endpoint

The INVEST-HD primary outcome is change from baseline in cUHDRS score, measured from baseline to Month 36.1

Extension readout referenced by PTC

PTC reported a dose-dependent cUHDRS benefit in Stage 2 participants relative to a natural history cohort, including 52% slowing of disease progression at the 10 mg dose.3

Operational and financial marker

PTC said the findings support the Novartis-led global Phase 3 INVEST-HD study and that first patient dosing triggered a $50 million milestone payment from Novartis to PTC in Q2 2026.3

Roche / SPK-10001

Roche gene therapy record remains recruiting with safety and functional endpoints

Trial profile

Recruiting2 Phase 1/22 53 estimated enrollment2 Quadruple masked2

ClinicalTrials.gov lists SPK-10001 and placebo surgery control as study interventions in a randomized interventional design.2

Primary safety outcomes

The record lists treatment-emergent adverse event count and severity as primary outcomes followed from Day 1 through approximately five years.2

Primary functional outcome

The record also lists change from baseline in Unified Huntington's Disease Rating Scale Total Functional Capacity score at Month 24 as a primary outcome.2

Planning marker

The record shows study start on February 21, 2025, and estimated primary completion on January 12, 2035.2

uniQure / AMT-130

AMT-130 disclosure separates FDA design discussion from UK filing preparation

US path

uniQure said FDA cannot agree that Phase I/II data compared with an external control are sufficient as primary evidence of effectiveness for an AMT-130 marketing application.4

Type B meeting

uniQure said it was granted a Type B meeting with FDA and plans to discuss a potential new clinical trial design plus the statistical analysis plan for four-year data expected in Q3 2026.4

UK filing plan

After a constructive pre-submission meeting with MHRA, uniQure said it expects to submit a UK Marketing Authorization Application for AMT-130 based on three-year data in Q3 2026.4

Company framing

uniQure's exhibit framed AMT-130 as advancing globally while also preparing for continued FDA engagement and evaluating additional international opportunities.4
