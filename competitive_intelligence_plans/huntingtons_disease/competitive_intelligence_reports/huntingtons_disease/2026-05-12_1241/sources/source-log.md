- Extracted facts used:
  - PTC reported positive topline results in April 2026 from the 24-month interim analysis of the PIVOT-HD long-term extension study of votoplam.
  - PTC reported dose-dependent cUHDRS benefit in Stage 2 participants relative to a natural history cohort, including 52% slowing of disease progression at the 10 mg dose.
  - PTC reported continued favorable safety and tolerability with no treatment-related NfL spikes.
  - PTC said findings support the ongoing Novartis-led global Phase 3 INVEST-HD study.
  - PTC said first patient dosing in Phase 3 triggered a $50 million milestone payment from Novartis to PTC in Q2 2026.
