West Virginia Medicaid Beneficiary Advisory Council (BAC) 
The West Virginia Bureau for Medical Services has established the Beneficiary Advisory​ Council (BAC) to give members, their family members, caregivers, and guardians the opportunity to share input about West Virginia Medicaid and West Virginia Children's Health Insurance ​Program (WV​CHIP) services.
