​ ​​Welcome to the Maryland Medicaid Pharmacy Program.   We provide services for the following programs: 
Medicaid:   Members r eceive complete pharmacy services. 
​ HealthChoice:   For more information see  Mental Health Formulary  and  Substance Use Disorder Medication Clinical Criteria Final ​. All other drugs are provided by  HealthChoice​  Managed Care Organizations (MCOs). 
​Family Planning:   Members r eceive only contraceptives and sexually transmitted infections treatment. 
​Medicare Part D:    Fully dual eligible Medicare beneficiaries receive most drugs excluded from Medicare Coverage. All other drugs are provided by Medicare Prescription Drug Programs (PDPs​​).
