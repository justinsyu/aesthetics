Coverage for Over-The-Counter (OTC) 
Pharmacy Products 
Maryland Medicaid Pharmacy Program will cover the following federally rebatable OTC 
products when a participant presents a prescription written by an authorized prescriber: 
(All other OTCs will deny with NCPDP 70 – NDC not covered)
