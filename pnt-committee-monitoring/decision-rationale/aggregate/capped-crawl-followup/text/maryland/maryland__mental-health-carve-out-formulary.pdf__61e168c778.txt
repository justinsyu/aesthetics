Mental Health Formulary 
Updated October 2025 
Medications from the American Hospital Formulary Service (AHFS) therapeutic classes listed 
below are included in the Maryland Medicaid Mental Health Formulary. These mental health 
medications are carved out of the MCO pharmacy beneﬁt and are payable as Fee-for-Service 
(FFS) through Maryland Medical Assistance. Some of these medications are subject to the prior 
authorization requirements of the Preferred Drug List (PDL) and clinical criteria. 
Refer to following links for: 
Preferred Drug List (PDL): https://mmcp.health.maryland.gov/pap/pages/Preferred-Drug-
List.aspx 
Clinical Criteria: https://mmcp.health.maryland.gov/pap/Pages/Clinical-Criteria.aspx 
The following ﬁve medications, which may be used for some mental health indications, are not 
payable FFS, unless otherwise noted, and are the responsibility of the HealthChoice MCOs for 
their enrollees regardless of the prescriber. 
Clonidine* Guanfacine* Leuprolide acetate** Liothyronine Medroxyprogesterone** 
* Generic clonidine (Catapres), and the generic guanfacine (Tenex) remain medications for which 
coverage is the responsibility of the member’s Managed Care Organization. For recipients 6 – 17 
years old, the extended-release forms of guanfacine (Intuniv) and clonidine (Kapvay) are included 
on the mental health formulary and billed FFS. For individuals not in this age range, guanfacine ER 
and clonidine ER will continue to be part of the MCO pharmacy beneﬁt. 
** When leuprolide acetate or medroxyprogesterone is used for the treatment of adult males with 
certain diagnosed behavioral disorders, these two medications will be paid FFS, but will require 
preauthorization (PA) through the University of Maryland School of Pharmacy CAMP program at 
410-706- 3431. 
Please note: Brand medications which currently do not have a generic equivalent are listed by 
brand name in italics. Those medications currently available generically are listed by generic 
name.
