7. Adjournment / Old Business 
a. MCO Pharmacy Directors asked the committee to review November 2019 minute notes and consider 
requiring an ICD10 approved diagnosis for pharmacy claims adjudication under the Stimulants drug class. 
Currently, the MCO’s can offer and provide Prior Authorization requirements for patients to still obtain 
approval for off-label usage if clinically appropriate. Topic was deferred as a recommendation for the 
state’s Drug Utilization Review (DUR) board. 
b. Hepatitis C Data: Dianne Garside notified the committee and public attendees that Hepatitis C claims 
data (paid and rejected) were reviewed as requested from the prior May 2021 meeting. The P&T 
Committee had made a request for claims data to determine if Epclusa and its generic were being denied 
appropriately. In conclusion, the generic of Epclusa did move to Preferred after May 2021 P&T, and no 
concerns were found with the reviewed claims data.
