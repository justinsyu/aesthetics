4. Resume Open Session 
A motion was made to Resume Open Session and was unanimously approved by all in attendance. 
During the public open session, committee members vote publicly on decisions with regards to the Nebraska Preferred 
Drug List recommendations. Per the State of Nebraska P&T Committee By-Laws, the minutes reflect how each member 
voted or if the member was absent or not voting. The chairperson votes only in the event of a tie. The details of each vote 
and the associated PDL recommendations are presented in the following tables.
