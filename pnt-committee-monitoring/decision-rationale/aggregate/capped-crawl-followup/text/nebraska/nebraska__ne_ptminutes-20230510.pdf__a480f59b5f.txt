Department information: Dianne Garside notified the committee and public attendees of recent staffing 
changes within the state’s pharmacy department, including the resignation of Ken Saunders. There are 
vacant positions open within the P&T committee board, including physician roles for family or internal 
medicine, psychiatry or neurology, and a public member position. Any interested candidates may reach 
out to Dianne Garside at dhhs.medicaidpharmacyunit@nebraska.gov for more information. A Request for 
Proposal (RFP) round was completed for the Heritage Health program and its MCO vendors. The 
awarded MCOs are Molina Healthcare, Nebraska Total Care, and United Healthcare and will be effective 
1/1/2024. All plans will follow the State of Nebraska’s Preferred Drug List (PDL).
