b) Status Update by DHCFP 
Chief of Pharmacy and DME Antonio Brown provided an 
announcement. 
Good afternoon, everybody. For the record, my name is Antonio 
Brown. I am the Chief of Pharmacy Services and Durable Medical 
Equipment with DHCFP. Happy New Year to all of you. I am so 
excited that we could be here today and, first and foremost, I just 
want to thank the Board and its Members for all their commitment 
and active participation. Your dedication has been instrumental in 
many achievements over this past year, and it is vital to take a 
moment and celebrate all that we have accomplished here. 
Together, with your invaluable support, we have successfully held 
three DUR meetings last year in 2024. We were able to revise nearly 
50 criteria. These efforts really reflect the shared commitment to 
Medicaid's mission to provide quality health care services to low 
income and in the most efficient manner while providing equal 
access to care at an affordable cost to the taxpayers. 
To highlight a few of the incredible contributions of our two new 
long-standing members, Doctor England has been serving on our 
board since 1992, bringing decades of wisdom and dedication to the 
table. And similarly, our board chair, Doctor Wheeler, who has been 
in that role since 2019, has been the guiding force steering us with 
vision and purpose. And most importantly, to each of our board 
members who play a critical part in our success of this board. None 
of our progress would have been possible without your engagement 
and tireless participation. 
As we look ahead into the year, I am filled with optimism and 
excitement to make collaboration our cornerstone working together 
to build our success and find innovative ways to serve the people in 
Nevada. 
On behalf of Nevada Medicare and all of those that we serve, I 
extend my deepest thanks to each of you and here is another year of 
--- Page 4 ---
impactful work in medical success. 
Also, I am excited to announce that we have a new member joining 
our Medicaid pharmacy team, Donna Cabrera, who is our new 
Program Officer and will be taking over the Pharmacy Lock-In 
Program. 
To highlight a few changes that have taken place since our last 
meeting, the RSV coverage season did open up as of November 1, 
2024, and it goes through March 31, 2024. We began accepting prior 
authorization as of October 21, 2024, for a date of services 
beginning in November 1, 2024. Effective September 26, 2024, 
we made some changes to our Diabetic Supplies Preferred Drug List. 
Full details can be found at nv.primetherapeutics.com. 
Lastly, I wanted to share is that we are actively recruiting for both 
SSSB and DUR board members. If you wish to nominate or know 
someone who may be interested, please share their CV to 
Rxinfo@dhcfp.nv.gov. Thank you.
