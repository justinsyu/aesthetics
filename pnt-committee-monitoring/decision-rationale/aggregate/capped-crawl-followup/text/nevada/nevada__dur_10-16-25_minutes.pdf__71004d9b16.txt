b) Status Update by DHCFP 
Chief of Pharmacy Servies Antonio Brown provided an announcement: 
Good afternoon, everyone. For the record, my name is Antonio 
Brown, Chief of Pharmacy Services for Nevada Medicaid. Thank you 
all for being here today and for your continued commitment to the 
health of our communities. 
I want to take a brief moment to update on the Single Preferred 
Drug List initiative. I am happy to share that the project remains on 
track for implementation on January 1st, 2026. 
We continue to collaborate closely with our Managed Care 
Partners. A few milestones are coming up for our focus on member 
communications. Our goal is to ensure that all the individuals that 
are going to be affected by this transition are well-informed and 
supported throughout the process. The primary objective, as a 
reminder for this initiative, is to reduce the administrative burden 
for both providers and recipients, simplify the prior authorization 
process and enhancements to care, and ultimately improve health 
outcomes for all Nevada Medicaid recipients. 
And as I always do, calling on our practitioners that may be joining 
us today. We have several open opportunities to serve on our 
board. If you're passionate about making a difference, we 
encourage you to get involved and help shape the future care of 
Nevada families and individuals. 
That concludes my updates, and thank you all for your time. I'll turn 
it over to Dr. Wheeler.
