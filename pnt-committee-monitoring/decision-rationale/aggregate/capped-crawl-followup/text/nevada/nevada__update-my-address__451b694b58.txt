Nevada Medicaid has an easy way for members to update their address with people who determine your eligibility: the Department of Human Services, Division of Social Services. Simply complete the form below.  
Please do not complete the form if you know that your address is up to date. You can also create an account, go paperless, and update your address at AccessNevada.nv.gov
