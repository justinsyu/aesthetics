Main Content 
Informational bulletins consist of a compilation of information on a topic, usually without significant analysis or recommendations. 
To find a specific bulletin: 
By bulletin number, click the column header named “Number” to sort numerically in descending or ascending order. Click the header again to change order. 
By a specific title, click column header named “Title” to sort alphabetically in descending or ascending order. Click the header again to change order. 
To open the bulletin PDF, click the link in the "Download" column. You may print out any bulletin using this link.  
Informational bulletins that are out of print are available only in PDF by the "Download" link on this page.  
Informational bulletins that are in print are available in PDF by the "Download" link on this page or are available by contacting in writing: 
LRC Publication Office 
Capitol Room 083 
700 Capitol Ave. 
Frankfort, KY 40601-3415 
Via email
