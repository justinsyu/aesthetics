Medicaid 
Iowa Medicaid is a joint federal and state government program that pays for medical care for some low-income and medically needy individuals and families.

Ready to apply? 
Members can apply online through the HHS Benefits Portal or with a downloadable paper application form.  Contact your local Iowa HHS office for more information.

Medicaid Coverage Plans & Programs 
Explore all Medicaid plans 
Iowa Health Link
Most Medicaid members will work through Iowa Health Link, our managed care program. Explore what this means to you. 
CHIP & Hawki
Find care for kids and teens with the Children's Healthy Iowa Program (CHIP) and Hawki. 
Iowa Health & Wellness Plan
Low or no cost health care for Iowans between the ages of 19 and 64. Eligibility is based on household income. 
Dental Wellness Plan
Dental benefits for most Iowa Medicaid members are provided through our Dental Wellness Plans. 
Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS)
Medical, social and supportive services for Iowans with functional, cognitive and other physical or mental health needs. 
Fee-for-Service (FFS)
Some Medicaid members are served by a program where care providers are paid separately for each visit.
