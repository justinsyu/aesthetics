Behavioral Health Services 
Inpatient Psychiatric Hospital (IPP) 
If requesting prior authorization or retroactive authorization for Inpatient Psychiatric Hospital stay, use  Form 470-5473 (705.72 KB) .pdf . For best results, save this document to your device, fill it out, and email to  inptps@hhs.iowa.gov. 
NOTE: If this is a request for continued stay you will also submit any related court orders or additional documentation to support continued stay. 
Psychiatric Medical Institution for Children (PMIC) 
If you are requesting prior authorization or retroactive authorization for PMIC stay, submit the PMIC Admission to PMIC2@hhs.iowa.gov .
