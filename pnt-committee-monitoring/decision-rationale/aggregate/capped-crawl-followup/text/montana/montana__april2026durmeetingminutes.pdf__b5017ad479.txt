April 2026 PDL/DUR Board Meeting Minutes 
Date: April 22, 2026 
Members Present: Barnhill, Anglim, Blake, Brown, Caldwell, Forster, Harrison, Jost, Nauts, Oley, Rask 
Members Absent: McGrane 
Others Present: Shannon Sexauer, Dani Feist, Josh Surginer (DPHHS); Kathy Novak (Prime Therapeutics); Bahny, 
Blank (Mountain Pacific); and representatives from the pharmaceutical industry. 
Public Comment: Speaker information is as follows: 
• Jason Smith, Gilead - Epclusa® 
• Kevin Schreur, ARS Pharmaceuticals - Neffy® 
• Krystal Ngo, Arcutis Biotherapeutics, Inc. - Zoryve® Cream 
• Sanaz Ziad, AstraZeneca - Breztri® 
• Anna Allen, UCB - Bimzelx® 
• Nissa Shaffi, Allergy & Asthma Network – epinephrine 
• Mark Harmon, AbbVie - Mavyret® and Rinvoq® 
• Christophe Langouet-Astrie, Boehringer Ingelheim - Jascayd® 
In addition to the speakers, we had 10 written documents submitted to the Board for review. They were for the 
following: 
• In the RED Group 3 we had 6 submissions: Bimzelx®, Otezla® from the Cytokines/CAM Antagonists 
category; Epclusa® from Hepatitis C Agents, Tezspire® from Immunomodulators, Asthma, and 
Zoryve® and Nemluvio® from Immunomodulators, Atopic Dermatitis. 
• In the BLUE Group 2 we had 3 submissions addressing the following: The Cystic Fibrosis Foundation 
recommendation for the Antibiotics, Inhaled category, Breztri Aerosphere® for the COPD agents, Neffy® 
for Epinephrine, Self-Injected. 
We also had a letter from a provider requesting we return the Spravato® criteria for review of the counseling 
requirements. The Board voted to bring the Spravato® counseling requirements back to discuss at a future DUR 
Board meeting. 
Meeting Minute Review: The meeting minutes from the March 18, 2026, PDL meeting were approved as 
written. 
Department Update: No Department update.
