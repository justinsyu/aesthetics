Department Update: Shannon Sexauer alerted the Board that effective March 2027 all prescribers will 
need to be enrolled as a Montana Healthcare Programs provider to have their prescriptions paid for at the 
pharmacy. This is a CMS mandate that the State of Montana must become compliant with. If a provider is 
not registered as a Montana Healthcare Programs provider, then the member will end up needing to pay cash 
for their prescription. Shannon requested all members of the Board share this information with their peers to 
get this information out in multiple different platforms.
