Interactive Dashboards 
Data and Statistical Information 
The Department of Public Health and Human Services (DPHHS) provides a variety of Interactive Dashboards to offer the public and policymakers data-driven insights into key health and human services areas across the state. These dashboards are generally organized into categories focusing on public assistance, child and family services, Medicaid, and public health data. These tools offer transparent access to current and historical information, allowing users to explore trends and outcomes.
