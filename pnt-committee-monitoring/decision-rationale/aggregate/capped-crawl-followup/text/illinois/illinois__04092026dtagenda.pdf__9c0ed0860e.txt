To Provide Public Comments, You Must Register in Advance As follows: 
• 
To speak during the meeting, interested parties must email 
hfs.boards.and.commissions@illinois.gov 
And register by no later than 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday, April 7, 2026. 
• 
Please title your email correspondence with the following statement in the subject line: 
Request to Provide Drugs and Therapeutics Advisory Board Public Comment during D&T 
Meeting: Thursday, April 9, 2026. 
• 
All public speakers must provide their name, title, organization name, email address and contact 
number when signing up to speak. 
• 
Speaker Requirements During the Meeting: The Department will try to accommodate 
as many requests as possible, however there is limited availability for public comments. 
Comments must be short in length. At the Department’s discretion and based upon the public 
comment time allotted, each speaker will be given less than 3 minutes to provide comments 
during the meeting. 
• 
Speakers on the same subject are encouraged to collaborate and have a single spokesperson, if 
possible. 
• 
Once your request has been reviewed, you will receive a confirmation email from hfs.boards.and.
commissions@illinois.gov 
• 
Registrants will be provided with details of how to join the meeting to provide comments 
by Wednesday April 8, 2026 
Regarding the Submission of Written Comments: 
• 
Additionally, interested parties may also provide written comments to the Department in lieu 
of speaking during the meeting. 
• 
Comments will be read into to record by the Chair or administrative personnel. 
• 
Comment Requirements: Comments must be short in length and must not exceed 3 minutes 
when read during the meeting. 
• 
Please email request to provide written comments 
to hfs.boards.and.commissions@illinois.gov by Tuesday, April 7, 2026. 
• 
Please title your email correspondence with the following statement in the subject line: 
Request to Provide Drugs and Therapeutics Advisory Board Written Comment on Thursday, April 
9, 2026. 
Postponing the WebEx Meeting: 
• 
If in the event of any technical errors with the Virtual WebEx platform that could impede upon the 
virtual operations of the meeting experience, the meeting will be postponed until further notice. 
• 
Registered Attendees will receive an email notification informing them the meeting has been 
postponed until further notice. 
• 
Registered Attendees will receive an email notification regarding the new date of the rescheduled 
meeting.
