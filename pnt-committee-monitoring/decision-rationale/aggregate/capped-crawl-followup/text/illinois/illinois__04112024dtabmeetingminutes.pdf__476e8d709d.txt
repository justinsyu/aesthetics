A. Product: Vraylar 
Presented by: Neelesh Nadkarni, PharmD, MSPH, Medical Outcomes & Science Liaison 
Presented Material Highlights: 
x 
Once daily 
x 
Four indications 
x 
D3 preferring mechanism of action 
x 
Longest half-life of 1 week 
x 
Neutral metabolic profile 
x 
Minimal sedation 
Discussion Highlights: 
x 
Well tolerated with coverage of all 4 indications 
x 
We need to expand access to these medications to decrease hospitalizations. 
x 
By the time patients get to a psychiatrist they have likely already tried 1st line drugs 
x 
Commercial insurance companies do make patients fail generics first 
x 
Significant utilization 
x 
Metabolic neutrality plays a role in patients adherence 
x 
Side effect profile in general is a huge determinant in adherence 
x 
Prevention of long-term metabolic issues is important 
x 
Should have metabolic monitoring 
x 
Due to psychiatrist shortages, primary care doctors need a larger tool box 
x 
No studies show superiority to preferred agents 
Motion: Dr. Berkowitz moved to make Vraylar preferred. 
Second: Dr. Vergara-Rodriguez

VIII. 
New Drug Initial Reviews 
A. Product: Daybue 
x 
Presented by: Jen Phillips, PharmD 
Presented Material Highlights: 
x 
For Rett’s Syndrome 
x 
Severe and rare neurodevelopment syndrome 
x 
Loss of hand and fine motor symptoms 
x 
Approved for 2 years and above 
x 
Diarrhea in 80.6% was main side effect 
x 
Guidelines do not mention since this was recently approved 
x 
Only drug for core symptoms of Rett’s 
Discussion Highlights: 
x 
Very low evidence of efficacy with high incidence of side effects 
x 
60% in study had no response 
x 
Reviewing on a case by case basis 
x 
Not enough clinical evidence at this time to fully back this as preferred 
Motion: Dr. Patel moved to leave non-preferred 
Second: Dr. Vergara-Rodriguez 
Motion passed without dissent. 
B. Product: Skyclarys 
Presented by: Jen Phillips, PharmD/Daphne Ni, Medical Account Director 
Presented Material Highlights: 
x 
For Friedrich’s Ataxia 
x 
Symptoms range from mild to severe 
x 
Hallmark symptom is loss of motor function 
x 
Most require wheelchair 15 years after diagnosis 
x 
Oral 
x 
Some baseline labs should be run 
x 
2 point increase in MFARS score was seen 
x 
No published US guidelines 
--- Page 6 ---
Discussion Highlights: 
x 
Increase of 2 points out of 93 does not seem hugely significant 
x 
Only product for this disease 
x 
Decrease in falls was seen 
x 
Continue to watch long-term results 
x 
Substantial metabolic side effects 
Motion: Dr. Vergara-Rodriguez moved to leave non-preferred 
Second: Dr. Patel 
Motion passed with no dissent.
