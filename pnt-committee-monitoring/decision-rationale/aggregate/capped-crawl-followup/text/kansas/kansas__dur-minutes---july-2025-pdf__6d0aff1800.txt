3. Minimum Requirements 
Prior Authorization 
Background: 
This revision includes minor update to the indication of Amvuttra® and the 
addition of Journavx™ to the list of agents requiring prior authorization. 
Public Comment: 
Taha Khan (Vertex) spoke on behalf of Journavx™ and requested that the 
Board consider unrestricted access for Journavx™. 
Board Discussion: 
The State discussed an evaluation of Journavx™ claims. Currently, most 
requests are potentially for off-label use as patients have diagnosis codes 
related to chronic pain and/or have claims for other pain medications. The 
Board discussed that patients could potentially have more than one acute 
pain episode. The Board recommended that the criteria specify the 14 day 
supply limit is per incident or episode. 
Dr. Callahan moved to approve as 
amended. 
Dr. Snow seconded the motion. 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously.

D. New Prior Authorization 
(PA) Criteria 
1. Hypoparathyroidism 
Agents 
Background: 
Yorvipath® (palopegteriparatide) is indicated for the treatment of 
hypoparathyroidism. The prior authorization criteria are being proposed to 
ensure appropriate use based upon the FDA-approved labeling information. 
Public Comment: 
Paul Miner (Ascendis Pharma) yielded his time back to the Board with the 
offer to answer any questions. 
Board Discussion: 
None 
Dr. Snow moved to approve. 
Dr. Reynolds seconded the motion. 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously. 
2. High-Cost Dosage 
Forms 
Background: 
Different medication dosage forms may have significantly higher cost 
despite having comparable clinical efficacy. Examples include: Bucapsol™, 
Inzirqo™, Tezruly™, topiramate, and Vigafyde™. The prior authorization 
criteria are being proposed to ensure appropriate and cost-effective use 
based upon the FDA-approved labeling information. 
Public Comment: 
None 
Board Discussion: 
None 
Dr. Snow moved to approve. 
Dr. Reynolds seconded the motion. 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously.
