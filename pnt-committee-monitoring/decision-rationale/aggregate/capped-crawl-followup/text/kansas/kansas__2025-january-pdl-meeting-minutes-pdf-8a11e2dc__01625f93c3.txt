IV. New Business 
1. New PDL Classes 
i. 
Anti-Viral – Coronavirus: Paxlovid™ 
Background: 
The coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) global pandemic resulted in the introduction of new 
medication and vaccination approvals. Several therapies were granted FDA Emergency Use 
Authorization but in May 2023, Paxlovid™ gained full FDA approval for the treatment of mild to 
moderate COVID-19 in adults who are a high risk for progression to severe COVID-19, including 
hospitalization or death. High risk adults include those with comorbidities such as cancer, chronic liver 
disease, chronic kidney disease, and cystic fibrosis, among others. Clinical trial data demonstrated that 
compared to placebo, Paxlovid™ had an 86% relative risk reduction in COVID-19 related hospitalization 
or death through day 28 in this patient population. The “Anti-Viral – Coronavirus” is a new class 
presented for inclusion to the PDL today. A class comparison chart is included for the committee’s 
review.

ii. 
Cystic Fibrosis Transmembrane 
Conductance Regulator (CFTR) 
Modulators: Alyftrek™, Kalydeco®, 
Orkambi®, Symdeko®, Trikafta® 
Background: 
Cystic fibrosis (CF) is a progressive, genetic, multisystem disorder that can affect the lung and pancreas, 
among other organs. It is caused by pathogenic mutations in the CF transmembrane conductance 
regulator (CFTR) protein. This protein is important for regulating water and chloride in and out of cells 
and mutations in the CFTR protein result in the buildup of thick mucus, and thereby increasing the risk 
of infections and organ damage. CFTR modulators can improve CFTR protein production and/or 
function through potentiating, correcting, amplifying and/or stabilizing the target mutant CFTR protein 
but can only be used in patients with specific CF mutations. Alyftrek™, Kalydeco®, Orkambi®, 
Symdeko®, and Trikafta® are all FDA-approved CFTR modulators and have specific indications based 
on specific CFTR mutations. “Cystic Fibrosis Transmembrane Conductance Regulator (CFTR) 
Modulators” is a new class presented for inclusion to the PDL today. A class comparison chart is 
included for the committee’s review.

iii. 
Sympatholytic Agents: Aldomet®, 
Catapres®, Catapres-TTS®, 
Nexiclon™ XR, Tenex® 
Background: 
Hypertension is a major risk factor for heart disease and stroke. In the US, the estimated prevalence of 
adults with hypertension is approximately 47.7%. Sympatholytic agents such as clonidine, guanfacine, 
and methyldopa, are often reserved for last line or resistant hypertension due to significant adverse 
effects. “Sympatholytic Agents” is a new class presented for inclusion to the PDL today. A class 
comparison chart is included for the committee’s review.

2. New Drugs to Existing PDL Classes 
i. 
Atopic Dermatitis Agents – Topical: 
Zoryve® cream, Vtama® 
Background: 
This PDL class was last updated in January 2022 for the inclusion of Opzelura®. Both Zorvye® cream 
and Vtama® were originally approved in 2022 for the treatment of plaque psoriasis and both recently 
received additional FDA approval for atopic dermatitis in July and December 2024, respectively. While 
Zoryve® is another phosphodiesterase-4 (PDE4) inhibitor, Vtama® is an aryl hydrocarbon receptor 
agonist, which is a new mechanism of action for the topical treatment of atopic dermatitis. A class 
comparison chart is included for the committee’s review.

ii. 
Immunomodulation Agents – Atopic 
Dermatitis: Ebglyss™, Nemluvio® 
Background: 
This PDL class was last updated in January 2022 when it was initially approved with Dupixent® and 
Adbry®. Ebglyss™ is an Interleukin-13 receptor antagonist, similar to Adbry®, and received FDA 
approval in September 2024. Nemluvio® has a new mechanism of action for the treatment of atopic 
dermatitis and is an Interleukin-31 receptor alpha (IL-31RA) antagonist. It received FDA approval in 
December 2024. Both are administered subcutaneously. A class comparison chart is included for the 
committee’s review.

iii. 
Immunomodulation Agents – Axial 
Spondyloarthritis: Bimzelx® 
Background: 
This PDL class was last updated in July 2024 for inclusion of Simlandi®. Bimzelx® was originally 
approved for the treatment of moderate to severe plaque psoriasis and recently received additional FDA 
approval for axial spondyloarthritis in September 2024. It has a new mechanism of action for the 
treatment of axial spondyloarthritis in that it antagonizes both interleukin-17A and F compared to Taltz® 
and Cosentyx® that only antagonize interleukin-17A. A class comparison chart is included for the 
committee’s review. The unbranded Simlandi®, approved from the Consent Agenda, is also listed in the 
comparison chart.
