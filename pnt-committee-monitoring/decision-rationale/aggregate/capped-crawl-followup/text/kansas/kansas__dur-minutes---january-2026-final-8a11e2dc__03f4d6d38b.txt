Background: New criteria for Zurzuvae® 
Public Comment: 
Lynda Finch (Biogen) spoke on behalf of Zurzuvae® and requested that the 
Board consider Zurzuvae® regardless of severity. 
Board Discussion: 
The State discussed that criteria presented was discussed and approved by 
the MHMAC committee at the January 13, 2026 meeting. Severity and 
timeline of treatment recommendations are from the ACOG guidelines. 
Previous ACOG guidelines also mentioned that SSRIs and SNRIs are also 
options for treatment of PPD.

Trials for Zurzuvae® did not have similar adverse reactions in clinical trials. 
Dr. Callahan moved to approve. 
Dr. Burger seconded the motion. 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously.

C. Revised Prior 
Authorization (PA) 
Criteria 
1. Duchenne Muscular 
Dystrophy (DMD) Gene 
Therapy Agents 
Background: This revision includes updates to the approval criteria related 
to FDA updates. 
In November 2025, the FDA updated the approval for Elevidys® to remove 
the indication for non-ambulatory patients. Given the safety issues tied to 
this, an interim update to the PA criteria to remove reference to the non-
ambulatory indication was emailed to the DUR Board for pre-approval. The 
interim update was unanimously approved by the Board via email and 
discussed during this meeting for final approval. 
Public Comment: 
Stephanie Kennedy (Sarepta Therapeutics) spoke on behalf of Elevidys® 
and requested that the Board consider removal of the upper age limit in the 
PA criteria. 
Board Discussion: 
The State discussed recent data for Elevidys®, including the EMBARK 
trial. 
The Board requested the State continue to research and evaluate the criteria 
and bring back to a DUR Board meeting when new data is available. 
Dr. Burger moved to approve and 
requested that the State bring back to 
the Board when new data is available. 
Dr. Callahan seconded the motion. 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously.

Cathleen Beaver, MD requested the Board remove the specialist 
requirement to allow for additional provider types to prescribe, to allow for 
the inclusion of ELF blood-based testing for diagnosis and requiring only 
one cardiometabolic condition for approval. 
Board Discussion: 
The State discussed that currently, ELF blood-based testing is not covered 
under Kansas Medicaid and thus would not be an available option for 
diagnosis. Additionally, step therapy is proposed for Rezdiffra® as there is a 
significant cost difference between the two therapies. 
The Board discussed removing the specialist requirement to allow for 
additional prescriber types to prescribe medications for MASH as renewal 
requirements would allow appropriate monitoring. 
The Board recommended requiring only one metabolic risk factor for 
approval instead of three.
