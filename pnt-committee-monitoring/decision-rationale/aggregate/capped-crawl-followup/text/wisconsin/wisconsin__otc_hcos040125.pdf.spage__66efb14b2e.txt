Over-the-Counter Drugs Covered by HealthCheck 
“Other Services” 
HealthCheck “Other Services”, known as Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnostic, and Treatment (EPSDT), are limited to 
children 20 years of age and younger who are enrolled in Wisconsin Medicaid and BadgerCare Plus. Members must provide the 
pharmacy with their ForwardHealth card and a valid prescription for over-the-counter (OTC) drug coverage. 
The following OTC drugs are covered as HealthCheck “Other Services” and do not require prior authorization. All other OTC 
drugs require prior authorization. 
For specific NDC information for covered OTC drugs, please refer to the Drug Search Tool .
