May 27, 2026 
Preferred Drug List (PDL) Update 
On May 6, 2026, LDH convened the Pharmaceutical & Therapeutics (P&T) Committee meeting. The 
updated Preferred Drug List (PDL) will take effect July 1, 2026, and will be available at: 
https://ldh.la.gov/assets/HealthyLa/Pharmacy/PDL.pdf. 
One new therapeutic class was added to the PDL as part of the Spring 2026 P&T Committee review: 
 Penicillins 
Brand preferred over Generic List: PHARMACISTS – adjust your inventory accordingly 
Effective July 1, 2026, the Brand preferred over Generic program will be reinstated. 
In certain cases, brand-name products are preferred because the net cost to the state is lower after 
manufacturer rebates are applied. Providers and pharmacists should review the updated Brand 
preferred over Generic List and adjust pharmacy inventory as appropriate.
