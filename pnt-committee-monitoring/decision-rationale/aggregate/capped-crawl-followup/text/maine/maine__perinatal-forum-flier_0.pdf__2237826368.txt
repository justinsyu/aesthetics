Supported by 
the State of Maine. 
MaineCare has extended 
postpartum coverage to 
12 months after birth.
This coverage is automatically applied to current MaineCare members– you don’t 
need to do anything to receive this benefit. This ensures that no one loses access to 
care and improves the health and wellbeing of Mainers during the postpartum period.
What does this cover? 
• Recovery from childbirth
• Follow-up on pregnancy complications
• Management of chronic health 
conditions
• Access to family planning
• Help for mental health conditions
• And much more
Talk to your doctor 
about it today.
