Standing Orders

Non-Prescription Oral Contraceptive Products 

This Standing Order authorizes licensed pharmacists to create a prescription for and dispense medically appropriate oral contraceptive products to eligible MaineCare members.

Naloxone 

This Standing Order for prescribing naloxone nasal spray products authorizes licensed pharmacists to create a prescription for and dispense naloxone nasal spray products to eligible MaineCare members.

Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT) Products

This Standing Order for Dispensing Nicotine Replacement Therapy Products to MaineCare Beneficiaries (PDF) authorizes licensed pharmacists to dispense medically appropriate NRT products to eligible MaineCare members who require the products for help with smoking cessation or vaping cessation.
