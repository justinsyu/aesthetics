Medicaid PA Advisory Committee May 6, 2026 Meeting

** Medicaid PA Advisory Committee meeting will be a virtual meeting scheduled to occur May 6, 2026.

To participate in this meeting, including the open sessions from 9:00am – 11:00am and 1:45pm – 3:00pm (afternoon session time may vary depending on when morning session ends), please click the link below to join the webinar (you may need to paste the link in your browser):

 

Webinar link: https://primetherapeutics.zoom.us/j/87962115980

or Telephone: dial (312) 626-6799 Webinar ID: 879 6211 5980

Meeting Information- The next meeting for the Medicaid PA Advisory Committee is scheduled for Wednesday, May 6, 2026, from 9:00am-3:00pm.  Department staff and their contractors will not meet with drug manufacturers from March 30th through June 19th, 2026, in order to prepare for and complete work associated with the meeting. 

  • Open Meeting Notice
  • Meeting Information
    • Final May 2026 Drug Classes
    • Preliminary May 2026 Drug Classes

Speaking requests will be accepted beginning March 30, 2026 through April 24, 2026. Please do not submit speaking requests prior to March 30, 2026.

 

    • Prior to requesting a speaking time or submitting written testimony, please review the Public Participation and Public Testimony section of the Medicaid PA Advisory Committee Department Guidelines for more detailed information.
    • Only one speaking slot will be permitted per company or organization for a maximum of two individual drug classes. However, multiple speakers per company or organization will be permitted to share the same four minute time slot per drug class.
    • Speakers may only provide testimony regarding drugs included in the specific drug class assigned to each four minute time slot.
    • Speakers are required to submit a Prior Authorization Public Testimony Registration form to register for a speaking slot prior to the meeting. Registration forms must be submitted by e-mail to:DHSWIPDL@Wisconsin.gov
      • Prior Authorization Committee Public Testimony Registration form
