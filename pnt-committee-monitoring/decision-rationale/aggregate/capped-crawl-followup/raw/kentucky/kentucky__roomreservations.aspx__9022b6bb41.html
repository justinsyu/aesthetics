Annex Committee Room Reservations

Main Content

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​

The Legislative Research Commission allows members of the public to reserve meeting rooms on the first floor of the Capitol Annex for various purposes throughout the year. Anyone can reserve a room, providing they agree to the terms in our Meeting Room Agreement Form.

Those hosting a breakfast or luncheon must also complete the Catering Information Form.

Those reserving rooms should also review our Policy Guidelines for Meeting Rooms beforehand. 

To reserve a room, contact Facilities Administration at LRCRoomReservations@kylegislature.gov. For more information about the process, please review our FAQ page. 
​
