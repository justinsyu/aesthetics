# Group B Buried P&T/DUR/PDL Insights

Scope: Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, and Maryland materials under `pnt-committee-monitoring/decision-rationale/group-b`.

## Top Findings

1. **New-drug access can be blocked before clinical review.** Illinois requires prior approval for all new drugs until D&T review, even inside an existing PDL class, and does not review a product until it has been marketed for six months. Maryland similarly marks new-to-market PDL products as PA-required until reviewed.

2. **Cost and capitation mechanics are often visible only in process pages or minutes.** Hawaii is moving Zolgensma back into MCO capitation effective January 1, 2026. Indiana's rule identifies carve-out triggers such as supplemental rebates, orphan/orphan-like drugs, capitation waste, and MCO solvency. Illinois separates evidence-based D&T review from HFS net economic decisions.

3. **Manufacturer engagement windows are tight and highly structured.** Idaho gives manufacturers one Industry Day per year, first-come first-served, with agenda materials due two weeks ahead. Iowa limits manufacturer comment to five minutes and excludes data-on-file. Kentucky limits industry presentations to three minutes per drug per manufacturer and gives only seven calendar days for post-meeting written exceptions.

4. **Rare-disease and specialty-drug criteria are being refined in public DUR records.** Kansas approved PA criteria for Journavx, Kebilidi, and NPC agents while documenting manufacturer objections around unrestricted access, FDA-label restrictions, and step therapy. Kentucky's Brinsupri and Leqembi IQLIK criteria show concrete diagnosis, specialist, biomarker, renewal, and quantity thresholds.

5. **Hidden access barriers are embedded in footnotes and carve-out lists.** Maine can invalidate sample-based trials and require pill counts or urine drug tests for controlled-substance failures. Maryland's mental-health carve-out changes by drug, age, and indication, not simply by class. Louisiana's public-comment queue previews pending MCO drug policy decisions across CAR-T, oncology, ophthalmology, HAE, and obesity.
