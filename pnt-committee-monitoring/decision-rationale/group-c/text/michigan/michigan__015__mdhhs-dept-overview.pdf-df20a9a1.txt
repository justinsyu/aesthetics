The Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS) administers dozens of 
programs to improve the health, safety and prosperity of state residents. MDHHS works 
hand-in-hand with hundreds of partners at the local and regional levels to provide these 
needed services. Below are among the most common MDHHS services utilized.
How To Find Resources 
Need help making ends meet? 
MDHHS provides a variety of direct aid to Michigan families, including 
food assistance, Medicaid, Healthy Michigan Plan, emergency cash aid, 
disability assistance, childcare support for working families, and help for 
mothers and their young children. Apply at Michigan.gov/MIBridges.
Concerned a child is in danger? 
Children’s Protective Services works to prevent child abuse and 
neglect and strengthen families. If you know of a child who is 
neglected or abused, call 855-444-3911. 
Want to foster or adopt a child? 
There are thousands of Michigan children who need foster 
care or a “forever home.” Learn about becoming a foster parent 
(Michigan.gov/FosterCare) or contact the Michigan Adoption Resource 
Exchange (MARE.org) to begin the process to adopt a child.
Need a vaccine? 
Find information on all your vaccination needs at 
Michigan.gov/Vaccines, including ﬂu shots. You can make sure your kids 
are up to date on vaccinations by visiting the American Academy of 
Pediatrics (AAP.org). 
Need information about your child support case? 
You can access case information at MiChildSupport at 
MiCase.state.mi.us.

Are you the victim of a crime? 
The Division of Victim Services provides help including crime
victim compensation, a sexual assault hotline to oﬀer support
and access to updated information about an oﬀender’s status.
Learn more at Michigan.gov/CrimeVictims.
Are you an older person who needs help with meals
or other services? 
The Bureau of Aging, Community Living and Supports website, 
Michigan.gov/Aging, provides tools to help you ﬁnd the local services 
you need.
Need a birth, death or marriage record? 
Connect with the Division for Vital Records and Health Statistics at 
Michigan.gov/VitalRecords. 
Aware of Bridge Card fraud? 
The MDHHS Oﬃce of Inspector General investigates any misuse
of MDHHS beneﬁts or payments to providers. If you know of such 
activity, please report it at Michigan.gov/Fraud. 
Worried about environmental hazards in your home? 
Division of Environmental Health employees are on hand to answer 
questions. The MDHHS Drinking Water Hotline is 844-934-1315, 
the MI Toxic Hotline is 1-800-MI-TOXIC (1-800-648-6942), and the 
Lead Hotline is 866-691-5323.
Know of someone in the mental health system 
being mistreated? 
The MDHHS Oﬃce of Recipient Rights ensures that everyone who 
receives public mental health services is treated fairly. You can 
report any abuse or exploitation of a recipient at 1-800-854-9090.

Are you or someone you know in crisis?
The Michigan Crisis and Access Line (MiCAL) is the statewide crisis line. 
MiCAL is a 988 call center and can connect a person in crisis any time 
day or night to compassionate care and support for any mental health 
or substance use-related distress. You can call or text 988 at any time.
