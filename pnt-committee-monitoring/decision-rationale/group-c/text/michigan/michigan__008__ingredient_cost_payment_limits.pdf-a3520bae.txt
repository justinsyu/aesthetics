Effective 4/1/2017 
Michigan Department of Health and Human Services 
Pharmacy 
Ingredient Cost Payment Limits 
Ingredient cost reimbursement will be the lesser of the following: 
National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC) 
Wholesale Acquisition Cost (WAC) plus 0% 
Maximum Allowable Cost (MAC) 
Provider’s usual and customary charge to the general public minus any other 
insurance and/or other monies collected toward the claim
