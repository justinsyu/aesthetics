Michigan Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee

The Committee shall be advisory in nature and shall assist the Department with the following functions pursuant to the applicable state and federal law: A. The Committee shall advise the Department on issues affecting prescription drug coverage for its various health care programs. B. The Committee shall recommend to the Department guidelines for prescription drugs covered in its various health care programs.
