III. 
Housekeeping Items / Updates 
A. 
Approval of the January 22, 2025, Meeting Minutes 
The Committee moved to approve the January 22, 2025, Meeting Minutes, with the 
addition of the committee’s discussion surrounding Anti-Obesity Class coverage on the 
statewide Preferred Drug List (PDL).  All were in favor of revisions.   
B. 
PDL Compliance / Generic Percent Report Updates 
Joe Bergondo provided an explanation of the PDL Compliance and Generic Percent 
reports. 
 
• 
Joe Bergondo reviewed the Generic Percent Report; overall generic utilization for Q4 2023 
was 85.5%. 
 
• 
Joe Bergondo reviewed the PDL Compliance Report; overall compliance for Q4 2023 was 
92.8%.
