Scott Brown welcomed all attendees to the committee meeting. Committee members, Bureau for 
Medical Services staff, contract staff, and Change Healthcare staff introduced themselves.

The Committee moved to approve October 23, ,2024, meeting minutes, with the addition of

B. PDL Compliance / Generic Percent Report Updates:  
• 
Joe Bergondo provided the PDL Compliance and Generic Percent reports.  
• 
Joe Bergondo reviewed the Generic Percent Report; the most updated generic utilization 
data was unavailable and the data from the last available quarter was reported. Overall 
generic utilization for Q1 2024 was 85.5%. 
• 
Joe Bergondo reviewed the PDL Compliance Report; the most updated PDL Compliance 
report data was unavailable and the data from the last available quarter was reported. 
Overall compliance for Q1 2024 was 92.8%.
