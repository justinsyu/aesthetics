Who we are: The Children’s Health Insurance Program Advisory Committee (CHIPAC) was 
established in 2004 as a body mandated by the Code of Virginia to: 
• Assess the policies, operations, and outreach efforts for Virginia’s Medicaid and 
FAMIS programs;  
• Evaluate enrollment, utilization of services, and the health outcomes of children 
eligible for these programs;  
• Report on the current status of the programs and make recommendations to the 
Director of the Department of Medical Assistance Services (DMAS) and the 
Secretary of Health and Human Resources.

What we do: CHIPAC meets quarterly to assess timely topics in children’s health and 
health coverage, and regularly receives reports from DMAS, the Virginia Department of 
Social Services, other state agencies, and key state and national partners.  
 
CHIPAC’s past policy recommendations have included encouraging the reauthorization of 
the federal CHIP program (2017), preparing for potential changes in federal immigration 
policy (2018), and streamlining enrollment and data collection in preparation for 
resumption of annual eligibility redeterminations following the COVID-19 Public Health 
Emergency (2023).
