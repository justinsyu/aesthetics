4. Director's Report 
 
Director Roberts presented to the Board an overview of the THRIVE 2025 DMAS Initiatives and 
Accomplishments, DMAS’ current and 2026 Initiatives.  Director Roberts made formal introductions 
of Dr. Greg Barabell, DMAS’ new Chief Medical Officer and Cheryl Gallon, DMAS’ new Deputy of 
Programs and Operations.   
 
There was a review of the H.R.1 – Community Engagement and Work Requirements with Timeline 
for Medicaid Requirements and a review of the Rural Health Transformation Program.  
 
Director Roberts provided updates on the Behavioral Health Services Redesign Project, Pharmacy 
Benefit Manager (PBM) Study, Procurement Updates, Maternal Health Initiatives and Nursing Home 
Updates. 
 
Director Roberts also notified the Board of upcoming meetings at DMAS and encouraged all Board 
members to attend the meetings. 
 
 
5. Home and Community Based Settings Regulations

Community Based Settings Regulations.
