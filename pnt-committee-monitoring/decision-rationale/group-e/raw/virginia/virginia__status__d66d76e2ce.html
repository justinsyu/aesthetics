Regular Scheduled MES Maintenance

MES is composed of modules representing many areas of the MES ecosystem.  These modules are maintained by DMAS and several MES vendors who ensure that optimal performance, user features and security are always maintained to the highest levels.  In order to accomplish this, maintenance is performed on a regular basis, usually in the evening.  While DMAS takes every precaution to ensure MES operations are not affected by ongoing upgrades and important maintenance, some interruptions can occur.  DMAS will make every effort to provide advance notice of these types of events.
