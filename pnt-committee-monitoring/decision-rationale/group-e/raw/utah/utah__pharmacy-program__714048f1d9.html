Medicaid Pharmacy Program

Welcome to the Utah Medicaid Pharmacy Program!

Here you will find information about covered pharmacy products, prior authorization requirements, utilization review programs, and the materials from the Pharmacy & Therapeutics (P&T) Committee and Drug Utilization Review (DUR) Board.

Archived materials created prior to the merger of the Utah Department of Health (UDOH) and the Utah Department of Human Services (UDHS) are available upon request.
