V. 
Ohio Department of Medicaid Policy Update 
On January 1, 2026, the Ohio Department of Medicaid (ODM) implemented the Next Generation MyCare program to provide enhanced healthcare 
benefits to Ohioans who have both Medicaid and Medicare coverage. The program is now available in 39 counties. Later this year, it will be available 
in the rest of Ohio. More information is available at Next Generation MyCare Program (https://medicaid.ohio.gov/families-and-individuals/citizen-
programs-and-initiatives/mycareohio/mycare-ohio). 
 
In clinical topics, ODM has recently carried out several DUR interventions. One intervention is aimed at members with claims evidence of being non-
adherent to their antiretroviral (ART) drug therapy.  The goal is to encourage prescribers to address barriers to ART adherence.  Another intervention 
identifies members with a diagnosis of anxiety and claims for a benzodiazepine but without evidence of an antidepressant or buspirone use.  The 
\n\n--- Page 3 ---\n 
 
goal is to encourage prescribers to use SSRIs or SNRIs for maintenance therapy to avoid adverse outcomes of long-term benzodiazepine use.  The 
most recent intervention is aimed at members with a diagnosis of heart failure with reduced ejection fraction but without claims for an SGLT-2 
inhibitor.  The goal is to encourage prescribers to consider adding SGLT-2 inhibitor therapy to reduce the risk of heart failure-related complications.  
As a reminder, a summary of all DUR interventions is posted on the ODM website in the quarterly DUR Digest publication. 
 
The Ohio Department of Medicaid (ODM) DUR Board convened for its 2026 Quarter 1 meeting on February 10th. Minutes are available on the DUR 
tab of the Ohio Medicaid Pharmacy website (https://medicaid.ohio.gov/stakeholders-and-partners/phm/drug-util/drug-util). The DUR Board will 
meet next on May 12th. 
 
Lastly, the Ohio Department of Medicaid was recently honored by the National Association of Medicaid Directors (NAMD) with their 2025 Spotlight 
Award.  This award was presented to recognize ODM’s sweeping “Next Generation of Managed Care” transformation—an ambitious, multi-year 
overhaul that has redefined how Medicaid services are delivered and administered to roughly three million Ohioans. Ohio Medicaid’s Next 
Generation transformation includes: OhioRISE, Single Pharmacy Benefit Manager (SPBM), Outcomes Acceleration for Kids (OAK), Ohio Medicaid 
Enterprise System (OMES), and a widespread collaborative managed care model. More information is available at "Next Generation” Medicaid 
Overhaul Earns Ohio National Spotlight
