6. 
New Business 
Lisa presented new drugs to market.    William updated the Committee on PDL, 
effective August 1, 2020, where brands moved to generic.   He provided an 
overview of the process for Pharma meetings which will continue in 2021.   The 
SC DHHS website offers COVID19 information addressing the needs of providers 
and members.   In addition, William provided updates on items that were 
implemented in July 2020.   Effective July 1, 2020, SC DHHS (1) adopted CMS 
standard list of HCPCS codes for MAT, (2) implemented 12 month supply of 
systemic birth control if written by the prescriber and (3) HCV carved back in to 
the MCOs.    Effective July 15, 2020, payment algorithm updated to include NADAC 
+ $10.50 dispensing fee.    SC DHHS currently developing AAC pricing with Mercer 
with a proposed implementation date of November 2, 2020.   William did share 
that the agency has a new Deputy Director, Janelle Smith.   In closing, William 
advised he is reviewing P&T Committee by-laws and will forward them to the 
Committee for review/discussion during the next meeting on November 4, 2020.
