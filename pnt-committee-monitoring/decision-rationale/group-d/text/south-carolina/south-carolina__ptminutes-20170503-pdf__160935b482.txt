6. 
New Business 
Lisa presented new drugs to market.    Bryan advised the Committee of 
organizational changes.   Former Director, Christian Soura, resigned as of April 7, 
2017.    Deidre Singleton is the acting Director and Bryan will fill the acting Deputy 
Director position.      
 
Bryan also advised the Committee that effective July 1, 2017 all smoking cessation 
agents will be available with no prior authorization (PA) and no copay 
requirements.    The monthly script limitations will be eliminated effective July 1, 
2017 as well.     Treatment of opioid abuse (MAT) will be moving to a protected 
class as well.   P&T will develop a sub-committee to open discussions.
