6. 
New Business 
Lisa presented new drugs to market.    Bryan introduced William Wynn, PharmD, 
as SC’s new Pharmacy Director and welcomed the new P&T Committee members.   
Bryan provided an overview of current SC Medicaid statistics, SC initiatives to 
address opioid and substance abuse disorders, the expenditure trend of Hepatitis 
C treatment from Q1 2013 through the present and the “Community Engagement 
Waiver”(Sec 1115 Demo Waiver).   The waiver is open for public comment with 6 
public hearings planned.   Target date of July 2020 for Go Live.
