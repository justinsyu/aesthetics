6. 
New Business 
Bryan addressed P&T’s primary charge.     The committee members are charged 
with recommending drugs to be preferred, acting as a sounding board for agency 
policies related to medications, ensure SC DHHS’ drug coverage policies are 
consistent with best medical evidence and avoid cost discussion with a focus on 
relative effectiveness and safety.   Bryan also discussed moving the February P&T 
meeting to April or June going forward.   Lisa will survey the committee for their 
availability.    
 
Lisa presented new drugs to market and new Hepatitis C drugs in the pipeline.  
Bryan discussed the Governor’s task force on Opioids.   Effective April 1, 2016, 
Medicaid providers must access a patient’s controlled substance scripts via the 
SCRIPTS database before issuing a prescription.    Bryan also discussed how 
pharmacy expenditures have outpaced historical trends and budget projections.   
He will engage the P&T committee to evaluate opportunities.
