6. 
New Business 
Cheryl Anderson introduced Dr. Kevin Wessinger, new Chief Medical Officer.      
She also updated the committee on new policies coming in 2023.  CMS announced 
the expiration of the Public Health Emergency (PHE) effective May 11, 2023.   The 
agency will renew redetermination activities as of April 1, 2023.   Effective March 
1, 2023, management of hemophilia factor products will transition away from 
SCDHEC to “any willing provider” for Fee-for-Service (FFS) beneficiaries.  A SPA is 
being submitted for the Pharmacy Access Act which will allow pharmacists to be 
reimbursed at parity for birth control services via medical.   Kim Bristow did place 
the FFS Preferred Drug List (PDL) on the SC DHHS website.   Additionally, Lisa 
Correll provided an overview of the new drugs to market.
