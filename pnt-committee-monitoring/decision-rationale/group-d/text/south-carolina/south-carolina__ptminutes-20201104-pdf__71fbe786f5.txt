6. 
New Business 
Lisa presented new drugs to market.    William updated the Committee that 
effective July 15, 2020, payment algorithm updated to include NADAC + $10.50 
dispensing fee.    SC DHHS has developed AAC pricing with Mercer which will be 
implemented November 16, 2020.   Additionally, William discussed COVID vaccine 
coverage and shared the dedicated COVID19 website.    Current COVID covered 
items will remain in effect until January 20, 2021 unless extended.
