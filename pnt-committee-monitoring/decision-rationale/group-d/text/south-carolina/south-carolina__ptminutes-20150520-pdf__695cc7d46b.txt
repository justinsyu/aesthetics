6. 
New Business 
Bryan spoke to the relevance of the P&T Committee.   Currently, there are 
approximately 1 million SC Medicaid recipients.   700,000 are enrolled in MCOs 
and the remaining 300,000 are Fee-for-Service (FFS).    Of those 300,000 
recipients, approximately 100,000 are in limited benefit plans (e.g., Healthy 
Connections, HIV/AIDS, TB, etc.), 100,000 are Duals and the remaining 100,000 
are enrolled in the full service benefit (FFS).    
 
SC DHHS will implement the next phase of the VBID (Valued Based Design) plan 
on July 1, 2015.   This phase will remove the $3.40 copay from Behavioral Health 
(except controlled substances) and Diabetic medications.
