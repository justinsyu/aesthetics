IV. 
State Updates …………………………………….……… Kevin Wessinger, MD and Kim Bristow, RPh  
• Dr. Wessinger notified committee members that all providers that order, 
refer or prescribe services or items for SC Medicaid members must be 
enrolled in South Carolina’s Medicaid program even if they are not billing 
Medicaid directly.  Providers may enroll through the SCDHHS’ website. 
Providers should select the “Ordering/Referring” provider type when 
enrolling. Providers who are only enrolled as an Ordering/Referring provider 
only need to enroll with SCDHHS and not with the state’s managed care 
organizations. Physicians who are already enrolled in Healthy Connections 
Medicaid are not required to enroll separately as ORP-only providers. 
SCDHHS has published a list of frequently asked questions and enrollment 
guidance for Ordering/Referring providers on the new ORP section of the 
website.  
• Mrs. Bristow notified committee members of SCDHHS PBM reform was 
effective 7/1/2025.  This reform will prohibit spread pricing, create contract 
definitions, require annual reporting on any fees charged by PBMs to 
contract network pharmacies, and will prohibit MCOs from requiring use of 
mail order prescriptions.
