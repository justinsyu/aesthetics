6. 
New Business 
Kevin Bonds, FACHE, HOP Program Manager, presented an overview of the 
“Healthy Outcomes Plan” which is a demonstration project to treat uninsured, 
high utilizers.   This program began in Summer 2013 and there are currently 45 
HOPS and 58 participating hospitals statewide.   Enrollment began in January 
2014.   Approximately 12,000 recipients are enrolled.    
 
Bryan addressed P&T’s primary charge.     The committee members are charged 
with recommending drugs to be preferred, acting as a sounding board for agency 
policies related to medications, ensure SC DHHS’ drug coverage policies are 
consistent with best medical evidence and avoid cost discussion with a focus on 
relative effectiveness and safety.
