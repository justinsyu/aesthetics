South Carolina Medicaid 
Pharmacy & Therapeutics Committee 
Disclosure Declaration 
 
 
Complete, sign, and return this form to South Carolina Department of Health and Human Services 
(DHHS) staff prior to making a presentation to the S. C. Medicaid P&T Committee.  Full 
disclosure by speakers is requested and expected by the Committee.  Note that full disclosure is 
expected of speakers who are not specifically invited by the P&T Committee or DHHS staff to 
make a presentation to the Committee. 
 
The purpose of this form is disclosure declaration. Having an interest or affiliation with a 
corporate organization does not necessarily preclude a speaker from making a presentation, but 
the relationship must be made known to the audience.  Completion of this form shall not disqualify 
a speaker from making a presentation, however, failure to disclose or false disclosure may prompt 
speaker disqualification.
