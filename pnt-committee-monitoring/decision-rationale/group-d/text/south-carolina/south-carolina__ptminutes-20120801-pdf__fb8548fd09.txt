A meeting of the P&T Committee convened at 4:00 p.m. on Wednesday, August

2. 
Welcome 
Deborah Tapley, R.Ph., called the meeting to order and welcomed members, 
guests, and staff members.   
 
Ms. Tapley opened the meeting by stating that the P&T Committee meetings are 
held in compliance with the Freedom of Information Act’s (FOIA) mandate that 
the public is notified when the public’s business is being done, and that 
furthermore, the public has been notified that this facility is accessible to 
individuals with disabilities, and special accommodations could have been 
provided if requested in advance.
