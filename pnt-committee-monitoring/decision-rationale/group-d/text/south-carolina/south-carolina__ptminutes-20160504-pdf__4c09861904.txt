6. 
New Business 
Bryan addressed P&T’s primary and secondary charge.   The committee 
members are charged with recommending drugs to be preferred, acting as a 
sounding board for agency policies related to medications, ensure SC DHHS’ drug 
coverage policies are consistent with best medical evidence and avoid cost 
discussion with a focus on relative effectiveness and safety.   Additionally, Bryan 
advised the group that State law requires four meetings per year.   Going 
forward, there will be two meetings in May, one in August and one in November.      
 
Lisa presented new drugs to market.    Bryan also discussed the following policy 
changes:  SCRIPTS mandate (April 1, 2016), Behavioral Health Carve-In (July 1, 
2016), MCO contract extension (July 1, 2016), the Outpatient Drug Final Rule and 
Check-up moving back to Family Planning.
