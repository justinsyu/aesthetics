Public Comment On Drug Classes Opened for Review 
 
Presenters must pre-register with DHHS no less than seven days in 
advance of the meeting.  Testimony will be limited to three minutes and 
there will be only one speaker per pharmaceutical agent under 
consideration.  P&T Committee members may use an additional two 
minutes to ask the speaker questions if warranted.\n
