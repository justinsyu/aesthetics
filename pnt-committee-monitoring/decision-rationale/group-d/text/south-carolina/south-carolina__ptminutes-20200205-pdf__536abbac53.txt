New Business 
Lisa presented new drugs to market.    William presented the P&T Committee By-
Laws.   The Committee discussed virtual attendance by teleconference suggesting 
two (2) in person and two (2) via teleconference per year.   Voting by electronic 
means may be allowed due to extenuating circumstances.   William will review the 
Committee’s suggestions with SC DHHS Legal counsel and provide feedback at the 
next P&T meeting.    William advised that the Community Engagement Waiver has 
been approved and will go into effect July 2022.   SC DHHS is leading ongoing 
meetings with Optum for the replacement MMIS system.   The Governor’s Opioid 
Summit will be held March 2020.
