New Business 
 
The P&T Committee asked for savings information since the PDL was 
implemented in 2004.  The Committee also suggested that the PDLs for each 
MCO be posted in one location (e.g., SC DHHS website).  And, the Committee 
would like to know how may children, between the ages of 0 – 10 years of age, 
are getting Tetracyclines?
