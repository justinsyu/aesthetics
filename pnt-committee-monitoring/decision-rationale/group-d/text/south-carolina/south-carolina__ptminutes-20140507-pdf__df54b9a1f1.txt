6. 
New Business 
Bryan presented information on draft PA criteria for the treatment of Hepatitis 
C.   In addition, he advised the Committee of upcoming changes to the Lock-In 
program.  These changes will be adopted on July 1, 2014.     
 
Bryan advised the Committee that most payers have 2 preferred options for 
diabetic supplies and it is advantageous for FFS to implement that methodology.   
Special provisions will continue to be made for those whose clinical condition 
warrants special consideration.    Manufacturers will be invited to speak at the 
next P&T meeting.    
 
SC DHHS plans to implement Phase 2 of the VBID (Valued Based Design) plan 
soon.   This phase will introduce a two tier copay structure.    SC DHHS is 
currently working through the process of amending the SC Medicaid State Plan 
to allow for more flexibility in patient cost share.
