6. 
New Business 
Cheryl Anderson, SC Pharmacy Director, provided policy updates.   SC DHHS now 
provides pharmacy coverage of OTC diagnostic antigen tests for COVID-19.    
Coverage for tests will not require prior authorization or cost sharing.    Quantity 
limits will be 8 tests per 30 day period up to a maximum of 10 tests per 30 day 
period.  Senate Bill 628, Pharmacy Access Act, was introduced on March 2, 2021 
and is currently residing with the House.   The purpose of this bill is to improve 
and expand access to contraception and allow pharmacists who are appropriately 
credentialed to administer and dispense contraceptive therapies.
