6. 
New Business 
Bryan opened the meeting recognizing Thomas Phillips, a pharmacist and 
supervisor for CVS and a member of our P&T committee, as well as the entire 
CVS chain of pharmacies, for taking the stand to no longer sell tobacco products 
in their pharmacies.    
 
Bryan then presented information on the new format for future 2014 P&T 
meetings.   February meetings will be dedicated to SC Medicaid policy and 
population,  May and November meetings will include PDL review and August 
will be dedicated to clinical criteria review.   In addition, Bryan advised the 
committee that SC Medicaid is undergoing recruiting efforts for additional P&T 
members.     
 
Lisa presented new drugs which came to market between the months of October 
and December 2013.    She also provided information on program changes 
including upcoming DOB edits, which go live on April 1, 2014.    Arixtra, Caduet 
and Lotrel will be removed from the SC “Brand over Generic” list effective, April 
1.   Lisa also discussed changes in the FFS population, as well as, the top 15 drugs 
broken out by ages 0 – 18 and 19 and older.
