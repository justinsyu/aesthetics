6. 
New Business 
Dr. Wessinger, Chief Medical Officer, shared the agency’s plan to add the 
OmniPod® 5 insulin pump to the Diabetic Supply (DSP) list effective October 1, 
2023 to better serve the needs of pediatric patients.    The P&T Committee voted 
to add this insulin pump to the DSP list.     
 
Lisa Correll presented new drugs to market and shared the cadence of PDL 
updates which occur every January and July.     Pharma meetings are offered for 1 
entity per year and remain virtual.
