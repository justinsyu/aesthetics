6. 
New Business 
Lisa presented new drugs which came to market in the 2nd quarter of 2014 and 
discussed the top PA (prior authorization) categories for the FFS population.    
 
Bryan provided an update from the Governor’s Prescription Drug Abuse 
Prevention Council.   Senate Bill 840 was passed requiring pharmacies to report 
to the Scripts program every 24 hours.   Additional legislation is forthcoming to 
mandate use of the PDMP where prescribers will review a patient’s drug history 
and document prescriptions for controlled substances and requiring prescribers 
to complete two CE hours dedicated to prescribing opioids.    A clinical sub-group 
will develop statewide guidelines for chronic use of opioids in non-cancer 
patients.    
 
Bryan presented changes to be made to the Smoking Cessation benefit.   This 
class of drugs will be added to the VBID classes which will exempt them from 
copay and monthly script limits.   Annual limits will be removed and concurrent 
therapy will be allowed.   These changes will be implemented later in 2014.   
Bryan presented information on the “Healthy Connections Checkup” program 
which is an expansion of the family planning benefit allowing an annual physical, 
mammography and colonoscopy services.    Bryan also presented information on 
the “Healthy Connections Prime” program, SC’s duals demonstration project 
which will move approximately 35,000 of these recipients into a managed care 
model.   Potential enrollment date is set for January 1, 2015.
