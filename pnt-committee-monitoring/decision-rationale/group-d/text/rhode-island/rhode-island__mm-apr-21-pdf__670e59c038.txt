The meeting was called to order by the Chairperson once a quorum was in attendance - 8:07 am. Chairman Tracey Taviera called the 
meeting to order and asked for introductions the committee and those supporting the meeting. The December 15th, 2020 meeting 
minutes were reviewed and by vote were accepted as presented. The following topics were discussed: election of Todd Brothers as 
committee co-chair, by-laws review including expansion of committee and length of public testimony.  By-laws review will be 
scheduled for the June meeting.  
 
Public testimony included the following speakers and topics.
