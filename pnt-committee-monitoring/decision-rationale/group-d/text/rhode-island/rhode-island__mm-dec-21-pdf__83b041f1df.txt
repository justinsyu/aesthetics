minutes were reviewed and by vote were accepted as presented.

Magellan Medicaid Administration (MMA) presented the following categories for therapeutic class reviews with discussion from the 
pharmacy and therapeutics committee.   MMA reminded the audience that Medicaid covers all medications as long as the 
manufacturer has signed a contract with CMS even if it is not on the PDL.  Also, informed the audience that this meeting and the 
Preferred Drug List is strictly for FFS RI Medicaid, not any of the Managed Care Organizations.
