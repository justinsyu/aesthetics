The meeting was called to order by the Chairperson once a quorum was in attendance - 8:05 am. Chairman Dave Feeney 
announces two resignations; his own, and that of Scott Campbell. The chair will transition in 2021 to Tracey Taveira. Co-chair will 
be voted upon at the April 2021 meeting.  Words of thanks were extended to both Dave and Scott for their service and 
commitment to the RI Medicaid FFS P & T advisory committee. 
 
The September 15th, 2020 meeting minutes were reviewed and by vote were accepted as presented.
