Electronic Prior Authorization Requests 
Electronic prior authorization requests are now accepted by NYRx, the Medicaid Pharmacy Program, 
via CoverMyMeds®. Consider this alternative option as an effective and efficient way to request a prior 
authorization other than by phone or by fax. For more information, contact NYRx Education & Outreach 
at NYRxEO@primetherapeutics.com. 
What Prescribers Need to Know 
Prescribers should review the NYRx Preferred Drug List (PDL) and prescribe preferred products. The 
drugs listed are preferred and generally do not require prior authorization when prescribed according 
to FDA labeling, unless otherwise indicated. Agents in the following PDL classes are preferred and do 
not have any additional clinical criteria: Antipsychotics – Injectable (except for Erzofri®, Rykindo® and 
risperidone injection [generic Risperdal Consta®BLTG], which are non-preferred), Hemophilia Agents – 
Factor VIII, Hemophilia Agents – Factor IX, and Hemophilia Agents – Other (except for Alhemo® and 
Hympavzi™, and Qfitlia®). For a copy of the full preferred drug list, click here.
