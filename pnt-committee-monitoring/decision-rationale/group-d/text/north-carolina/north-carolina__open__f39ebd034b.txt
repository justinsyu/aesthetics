NC Medicaid is beginning a new PDL Panel meeting policy in 2022. We will be moving to meeting 
two times per year (January and July), in addition to reviewing every PDL category at least once per 
calendar year. Public comments during the 45-day public comment period will still only be allowed for 
categories the State designates on the publicly posted document. Speakers at the PDL Panel meeting are 
only allowed for categories listed in the agenda for that meeting. The categories that are highlighted 
below are categories that have no State recommendations but are open for discussion. The following 
are considered the open categories for the 1/13/2022 meeting:
