As a reminder, the PDL Panel meets two times per year (January and July), in addition to reviewing 
every PDL category at least once per calendar year. Public comments during the 45-day public 
comment period will still only be allowed for categories the State designates on the publicly posted 
document. Speakers at the PDL Panel meeting are only allowed for categories listed in the agenda for 
that meeting. The categories below contain recommendations that will require a vote by the PDL 
Panel. Those categories that are highlighted have no State recommendations but are open for 
discussion:
