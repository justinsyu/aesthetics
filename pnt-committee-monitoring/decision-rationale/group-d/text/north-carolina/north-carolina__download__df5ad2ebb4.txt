NC Medicaid began a new PDL Panel meeting policy in 2022. We moved to meeting two times per 
year (January and July), in addition to reviewing every PDL category at least once per calendar year. 
Public comments during the 45-day public comment period will still only be allowed for categories the 
State designates on the publicly posted document. Speakers at the PDL Panel meeting are only allowed 
for categories listed in the agenda for that meeting. The categories that are highlighted below are 
categories that have no State recommendations but are open for discussion. The following are 
considered the open categories for the 7/14/2022 meeting:
