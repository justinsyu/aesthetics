Oregon State Drug Review


A monthly newsletter published by the College of Pharmacy on behalf of the OHA. Provides drug utilization reviews, drug and therapeutic guideline reviews, and cost-effective prescribing recommendations to Medicaid providers and pharmacies throughout Oregon. Each newsletter is offered in PDF format for viewing and printing.

You may elect to receive the newsletter monthly via email subscription through the OSUPHARMDI Listserv.
