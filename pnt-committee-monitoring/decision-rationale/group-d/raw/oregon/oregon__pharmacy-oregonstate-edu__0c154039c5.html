OSU College of Pharmacy Shifts Primary Campus to Portland

Oregon State University has approved the recommendation to move the primary campus of the Oregon State University College of Pharmacy to Portland. This exciting shift will strengthen our impact, expand opportunities for collaboration, and ensure our students are prepared to lead in the dynamic field of pharmacy. The transition will begin in Fall 2026.

If you are an incoming or prospective student and want to know more about the change, please contact us at [email protected].

Residency & Placement

Oregon State University Pharm.D. students exceed the national average in registering for the residency match process, actively listing in the process, and matching with a program.

Recent residency outcomes for applicants include:

  • 93% match rate among the 2026 graduates who had an active listing in the residency matching process
  • 87% match rate among the 2026 graduates who registered for the residency matching process
  • 57% match rate among the entire graduating class of 2026

Over seven years, College of Pharmacy graduates completed residency programs at over 110 unique residency placement sites across the country in 34 different states.

Join A Supportive Learning Community

You can find our home in the lush, tight-knit town of Corvallis, Oregon, where you’ll experience our personable culture and personalized education. You’ll spend the first two years of the Pharm.D. program here, before joining our partner campus at the Oregon Health and Science University (OHSU)—a renowned academic medical center in Portland— for your third year. And, though you’ll get hands-on learning experiences throughout your 4-year program, your fourth year will be entirely devoted to working directly with communities throughout the state and beyond.

A Community of Healers and Innovators

For over 100 years, The College of Pharmacy has been a cornerstone of Oregon State University’s research prowess and educational excellence. The College is fully accredited by the Accreditation Council for Pharmacy Education. Here, world-renowned faculty train the next generation of pharmacists while uncovering tools and treatments that give Oregonians the priceless gift of health.
