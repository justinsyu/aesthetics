P & T Committee Policies

Members of the pharmaceutical industry are requested to submit information regarding potential PDL drugs to SCSinglePDL@primetherapeutics.com and should not directly contact or send mailings to P&T Committee Members or their staff regarding the PDL status of drug products. Committee members must not discuss issues regarding PDL status of drugs with members of the pharmaceutical industry.
