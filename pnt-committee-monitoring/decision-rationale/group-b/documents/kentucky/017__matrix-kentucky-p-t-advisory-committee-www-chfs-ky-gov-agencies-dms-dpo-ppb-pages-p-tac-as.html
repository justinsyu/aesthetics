Pharmacy and Therapeutics Advisory Committee

Main Content

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​The Pharmacy and Therapeutics Advisory Committee (P&T) advises the governor, secretary of the Cabinet for Health and Family Services and the commissioner of the Department for Medicaid Services on the development and administration of an outpatient drug formulary.
