Kansas Drug Utilization Review 
Conflict of Interest Disclosure 
The Kansas Drug Utilization Review Board members and persons testifying or presenting to the DUR Board are asked to 
disclose any financial or other affiliation with organizations that may have a direct or indirect interest in the business in 
front of the Board.   
A financial interest may include, but is not limited to, being a shareholder in the organization (direct purchase); being on 
retainer with the organization; or having research or honoraria paid by the organization.  
An affiliation may include holding a position on an advisory committee or some other role or benefit to a supporting 
organization. 
The existence of such relationships does not necessarily constitute a conflict of interest and will not preclude an individual 
from participating on, or addressing the DUR Board.   This policy is intended to openly identify any potential conflicts so 
that DUR Board members and the public are able to form their own judgements. 
Please check the box of the statement that best applies. 
Non-Declaration:       
I do not have a current or recent (within the last 12 months) financial arrangement or affiliation with 
any organization that may have a direct interest in the business before the Kansas Medicaid DUR 
Board. 
Declaration:       
I have a financial interest, affiliation or am employed by an organization that may have a direct interest 
in the business before the Kansas DUR Board.  I am appearing at the request of a drug 
manufacturer.
