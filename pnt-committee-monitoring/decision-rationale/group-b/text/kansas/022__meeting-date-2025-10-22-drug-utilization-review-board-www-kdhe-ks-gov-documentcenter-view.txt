       E. New Prior Authorization 
 
(PA) Criteria 
1. Aromatic L-Amino Acid 
Decarboxylase (AADC) 
Deficiency Gene Therapy 
Agents 
 
 
 
Background: 
Kebilidi™ (eladocagene exuparvovec-tneq) is indicated for the treatment of 
Aromatic L-Amino Acid Decarboxylase (AADC) Deficiency. The prior 
authorization criteria are being proposed to ensure appropriate use based 
upon the FDA-approved labeling information. 
 
Public Comment:  
Lauren Warn (PTC Therapeutics) spoke on behalf of Kebilidi™ and 
requested that the Board consider PA criteria without step therapy and 
additional restrictions beyond FDA labeling.   
 
Board Discussion:  
The Board requested the criteria be reviewed for the October 2026 DUR 
Board meeting. 
 
Dr. Reisinger moved to approve.  
Dr. Snow seconded the motion. 
 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously.

2. Hidradenitis Suppurativa 
(HS) Agents 
 
 
Background: 
Adalimumab products (Humira®, Abrilada™, Amjevita®, Cyltezo®, 
Hadlima™, Hulio®, Hyrimoz®, Idacio®, Simlandi®, Yuflyma®, Yusimry®), 
Bimzelx® (bimekizumab-bkzx), and Cosentyx® (secukinumab) are 
indicated for the treatment of hidradenitis suppurativa. The prior 
authorization criteria are being proposed to ensure appropriate used based 
upon guidelines and FDA-approved labeling information.  
 
Public Comment:  
Shirley Quach (Novartis) spoke on behalf of Cosentyx® and requested that 
the Board consider removing the requirement of Hurley Staging for 
diagnosis.  
 
Board Discussion: 
None 
 
Dr. Burger moved to approve.  
Dr. Snow seconded the motion. 
 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously. 
3. Niemann-Pick Disease Type 
C (NPC) Agents 
 
Background: 
Aqneursa™ (levacetylleucine) and Miplyffa® (arimoclomol) are indicated 
for Niemann-Pick Disease Type C (NPC). The prior authorization criteria 
are being proposed to ensure appropriate use based upon the FDA-approved 
labeling information. 
 
Public Comment:  
Janelle Raymond (Intrabio) spoke on behalf of Aqneursa™ and requested 
that the Board consider the removal of step therapy with miglustat.  
 
Board Discussion: 
The Board requested the criteria be reviewed for the July 2026 DUR Board 
meeting. The State can defer discussion to a later meeting date if there is no 
utilization. 
 
Dr. Snow moved to approve.  
Dr. Reynolds seconded the motion. 
 
Motion to approve was carried 
unanimously.
