KanCare Eligibility Data Reports Drug Program Our Work Medicaid Home Programs & Services Medicaid / Health Care Finance Our Work Pharmacy Clinical Programs Preferred Drug List Program Preferred Drug List Advisory Committee Preferred Drug List Advisory Committee Meetings Meetings are held from 11:30 am-1:00 pm, bi-annually on the third Tuesday in February and August. The meeting is held via Zoom and/or at Gainwell Technologies (formerly DXC Technology), in the Capital Room located at 6511 SE Forbes Ave, Building 283J, Topeka, KS 66619. Conflict of Interest Members of the general public are required to complete a Conflict of Interest (COI) form (PDF) if they intend to provide public comments to the Committee. To assist the operator in coordinating public comment, completed forms are due one week prior to the meeting. Email the completed form to Anh Rongish .
