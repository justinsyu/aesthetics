Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee (P&T Committee) is comprised of Idaho health practitioners who are committed to providing the right care at the right price. This committee meets four times a year to review clinical evidence. The P&T Committee makes recommendations to the Department of Health and Welfare regarding drug safety and efficacy as well as prior authorization requirements. Meeting Dates Meeting Materials April May October November Drug class review schedule Idaho Medicaid Industry Days Idaho Medicaid Industry Days provide pharmaceutical manufacturers with a structured opportunity to engage directly with Idaho Medicaid staff. These sessions foster collaboration, enhance transparency, and support informed decision-making for the Idaho Medicaid Pharmacy Program. Industry Days will take place the 4th Wednesday of March, June, September, and December. Each company may sign up for one Industry Day per calendar year. Each meeting lasts up to 25 minutes including time for questions and answers. Additional meetings may occur if requested by Idaho Medicaid. How to Participate Registration: Companies must register in advance. Sessions are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis. Agenda submission: An agenda must be submitted at least two weeks prior to the scheduled meeting. The agenda should include discussion topics and objectives, supporting materials, and an explanation of how the information benefits Idaho Medicaid. Compliance All participants must adhere to Idaho Medicaid policies, confidentiality requirements, and professional conduct standards. Non-compliance may result in exclusion from future participation.
