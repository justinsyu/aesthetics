I. 
Board Denise Oshiro called the meeting to order at 12:10 pm. Dr. Curtis Toma 
introduced Denise Oshiro as the new pharmacy director. Denise Oshiro then 
proceeded with a roll call of the DUR Board members. Quorum was established 
with six members present. Introduction of the new DUR Board Member Kelsey 
Kong Trujillo. The board members participating were to declare if they were not 
alone. Each participating board members were able to declare they were alone 
except Denise Oshiro and Dr. Toma who were present in the meeting room. 
II. 
The August 14, 2024, meeting minutes were reviewed by the board. There was no 
public testimony. Without further discussion, it was moved by Dr. Curtis Toma to 
approve the minutes and seconded by Miki Morita. The motion passed 
unanimously, with no opposition and no abstentions. The May 14, 2025, minutes 
were reviewed by the board. There was no public testimony. Without further 
discussion, it was moved by Dr. Curtis Toma to approve the minutes and seconded 
by Miki Morita. The motion passed unanimously, with no opposition and no 
abstentions. 
III. 
New Business 
a. 
Former Board member Eddie Fox, a medical service representative has retired 
from the board. Med-QUEST (MQD) is currently in communication with a 
medical service representative who is interested in joining the board.  
b. 
In addition, there is an open Medicaid practicing board member position. 
Since the DUR Board focuses on FFS, and FFS covers members for dental 
services, a doctor of oral and maxillofacial surgery or the like is preferred, if 
available. MQD is requesting recommendations for this practicing physician 
board member position. No public testimony or board testimony was shared.  
c. 
Under Hawai’I Administrative Rules MQD Dur Board members are appointed 
for a three-year term. MQD seeks interests from those current Board 
Members who are interested in retention for an extended three-year term, or 
less if retirement is near. MQD will submit names in a letter for approval by 
DHS Director Ryan Yamane. No public testimony or board testimony was 
shared. 
d. 
Ultra high-cost drug Zolgensma for Spinal Muscular Atrophy will be carved 
back with the Managed Care Organizations (MCOs) effective January 1, 2026. 
This is because Zolgensma is among a dozen other high-cost medications now 
marketed, that are not carved out.  Zolgensma is not a unique situation any 
longer.  Also, MQD would like the MCOs to take back more ownership of drug 
therapy.  Therefore, the actuary has factored Zolgensma back into the

Election of chairperson and vice-chairperson 
a.
