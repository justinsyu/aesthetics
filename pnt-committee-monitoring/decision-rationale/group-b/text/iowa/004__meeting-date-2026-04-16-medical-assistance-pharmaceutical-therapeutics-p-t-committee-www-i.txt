In accordance with Chapter 249A.20A of the Code of Iowa (as amended by H.F. 619, 80th GA 2003) the P & T Committee was established within the Department of Health & Human Services (DHHS) for the purpose of developing, recommending and providing ongoing review of a Preferred Drug List (PDL). The committee consists of nine (9) voting members who are health care professionals and possess recognized knowledge and expertise in one or more of the following: The clinically appropriate prescribing of covered outpatient drugs. The clinically appropriate dispensing and monitoring of covered outpatient drugs. Drug use review, evaluation, and intervention. Medical quality assurance. The membership is comprised of at least one third, but not more than fifty-one percent licensed and actively practicing physicians and at least on third licensed and actively practicing will pharmacists. The committee is composed of 9 Members: 4 Physicians (including one Psychiatrist); 3 Pharmacists (including one research affiliated); 1 Physician Assistant and 1 Healthcare Professional (currently a RN). The committee members are appointed to terms of two years. Members may be appointed to more than one term. The Committee meets three times a year, the third Thursdays of April, August and November and provides recommendations on the PDL to the DHHS.
