## Pharma Implications From Filled Group B Evidence

- Treat public P&T monitoring as a three-layer workflow: meeting/agenda signal, committee recommendation or criteria artifact, then final PDL/PA implementation. Group B sources repeatedly show that these layers are not always published in the same location.
- Prioritize manufacturer-submission, public-comment, and testimony pages as much as agenda pages. They are the most actionable sources for shaping evidence before a committee action.
- Build class-level alerts for PDL/DUR pages even when product-level votes are absent. Review-class schedules, PA criteria updates, and implementation schedules can precede or substitute for formal rationale.
- Separate observable clinical rationale from non-public economics. Safety, efficacy, utilization, diagnosis criteria, step therapy, quantity limits, and preferred/non-preferred status are visible much more often than rebate or net-cost logic.
- Keep DUR boards, PDL advisory committees, Therapeutics Committees, and Medicaid pharmacy pages in scope. They often carry the actionable access evidence even when the body is not branded as a P&T committee.
