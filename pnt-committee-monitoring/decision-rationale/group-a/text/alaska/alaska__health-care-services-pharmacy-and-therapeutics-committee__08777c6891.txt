Services Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee Doctors reviewing drug classes for Medicaid Current Section Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee A team of Alaskan doctors and pharmacists appointed to a committee by the Commissioner of the Department of Health. These experts are reviewing drug classes for the Department's Medicaid program. They are identifying which drugs are safe and effective, and which drugs cost less than other drugs in the same class. Medicaid enrolled physicians are being asked to prescribe drugs that have been given “preferred status” by this committee. Members Robert H.
