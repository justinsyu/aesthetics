Departments Home Division of Health Care Services Alaska Medicaid Pharmacy and Therapeutics (P&T) Committee Alaska Medicaid Pharmacy and Therapeutics (P&T) Committee Experts evaluate and select preferred medications for Medicaid Current Section Who we are The Alaska Medicaid Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee is a team of Alaskan doctors and pharmacists appointed by the Commissioner of the Department of Health. What we do The committee reviews drug classes for the Department's Medicaid program. They identify which drugs are safe and effective, and which drugs cost less than other drugs in the same class. Medicaid-enrolled physicians are asked to prescribe drugs that have been given “preferred status” by this committee.
