Call to Order 
The Drug Utilization Review Board (DURB/DUR Board/Board) held its third meeting for the calendar 
year on July 16, 2025.  The Chair, Gurinder Doad, M.D., Ph.D., called the meeting to order at 10:07am. 
 
Minutes from the Previous Meeting 
Chair Doad asked for corrections or changes to the minutes from the April 16, 2025 meeting.  A motion 
was made and seconded and carried to approve the minutes as written. 
 
External Comments Session 
There were no external comments presented to the Board.  
 
New Drug Reviews 
Clinical information for the following new drugs, in the market six months or more, was presented for 
discussion and recommendations. The complete detailed drug summary is in the New Drugs for 
Review section of the DUR Board binder.

The Board discussed the drug information, provided comments, and raised questions on the 
following: 
• 
Attruby – If both manifestations of cardiomyopathy and polyneuropathy present, physicians 
may opt to use an agent that treats both; Win vs. Loss statistical analysis – takes the 
composite outcomes and gives a hierarchical range; with composite outcomes, results may 
be evenly weighted; there is a push to use hierarchical analysis. 
• 
Journavx – could be a niche for someone with Opioid Use Disorder who can’t take NSAIDs; 
concerned if patient is just discharged late evening and drug requires PA; do have the ability to 
do a silent authorization where system checks for diagnosis to allow approval; concerned 
about potential use for chronic pain; not sure if long-term tolerance would develop; clinical

trials-NSAIDs used as rescue therapy; no particular reason given why trials were with the 
selected surgeries (bunionectomy, abdominoplasty), may be level of pain; Initial/Loading dose 
would be given at retail setting; ongoing studies in diabetic neuropathy pain, so will have to 
wait and see what effect it has on nerve related pain; dosing-comparable to hydrocodone 5mg; 
Patient perception of whether placebo or active product received – didn’t see any data looking 
at this; slow uptake since adding to the PDL; concerns with patients still using additional pain 
therapies. 
• 
Alhemo/Hympavzi – 3ml SC (Alhemo) is a lot of fluid to inject, can use a higher concentration 
so may not be as much fluid, it is a daily injection; enzyme (ELISA) test (Alhemo)-not a lot of 
information about it, test has been approved but labeling hasn’t been updated yet; testing 
would be done 4 weeks after starting therapy, with any interruption in therapy, or possibly 
every 6-12 months; don’t have to stop for minor surgery but do have to stop for major surgery; 
no recommendations to check levels before surgery. 
• 
Tryvio – interesting that this class of meds is being used this way; class used in Pulmonary 
Hypertension (which is frequently seen in patients with severe obstructive sleep apnea ); there 
was a subset of sleep apnea patients in the study and it would be interesting to see whether 
there was any improvement in cardiovascular outcomes; CKD – may be beneficial if alternate 
therapies not tolerable; availability in Specialty Pharmacy may be more of a burden; anemia-
appears to be hemodilutional, seen in first 4 weeks, but then stabilize; if severe anemia, 
should avoid.

Georgia Department of Community Health (GDCH) 
Opportunities for Pharmaceutical Manufacturer Input on Clinical 
Recommendations and Clinical Management Strategies by the  
Drug Utilization Review Board 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Questions not addressed in this document may be sent to NorthStar 
HealthCare Consulting by e-mail:   GAMedicaid@nhc-llc.com 
Clinical Information and Clinical Management Strategies relevant to the GDCH Medicaid Fee-For-
Service program will be presented to the Drug Utilization Review Board (DURB) at each meeting 
through OptumRx by its vendor NorthStar HealthCare Consulting (NHC). Manufacturer input on new 
and existing drugs is welcomed and appreciated using these opportunities. Please note that new drug 
entities are generally not reviewed by the DURB until the drug has been on the market for at 
least 6 months. 
Presentation Opportunity: 
 
Manufacturers’ Forum: A forum prior to 
each relevant DURB meeting whereby 
manufacturers may present: 
 
1) Clinical information relevant to a new 
drug on the market under review by the 
DURB at the next meeting. 
 
2) Clinical information relevant to 
ongoing NHC/OptumRx clinical 
management strategies (e.g. review of 
drug benefit plan designs, new drugs 
coming to market, new indications, 
etc.) as deemed necessary by 
NHC/OptumRx. 
 
Please see the Manufacturers’ Forum 
Announcement at 
http://dch.georgia.gov/durb-meeting-
information. 
 
Upon review of information, and based on its 
expertise and discussions, the DURB makes 
recommendations to GDCH. 
Ongoing Opportunity: 
 
DUR Board Meeting Process: New drugs, 
therapeutic classes and/or supplemental rebate 
classes under review will be posted to the 
DCH website at http://dch.georgia.gov/durb-
meeting-information approximately 30 days 
prior to the Manufacturers’ Forum. Input 
specific to the drugs under review from 
manufacturers are made directly to NHC via 
GAMedicaid@nhc-llc.com and reported as 
appropriate by NHC at subsequent DURB 
meetings.  
 
Opportunity to Appeal to GDCH: 
 
GDCH Review Process: DURB recommendations are reviewed by GDCH for final decisions.  
Manufacturers may request an appeal meeting directly with GDCH after conclusion of each quarterly 
DURB meeting and this appeal meeting must be conducted within 10 business days following the 
DURB meeting.  Contact: Shirmary Hodges at (404) 656-4044 or shodges@dch.ga.gov
