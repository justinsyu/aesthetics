SUBJECT: 
Upcoming 340B Medical Billing Changes 
I. 
General Information 
As previously communicated, Arkansas Medicaid is working on updates to the MMIS core 
system to mandate the use of the 340B modifiers (JG or TB) for all 340B eligible non-
pharmacy claim lines. The official planned implementation start date is 4/1/2023. 
 
• 
There is no billing change for current outpatient global billing codes. 
• 
The procedure codes for outpatient surgical procedures that are global codes includes 
all related non-physician services. Separate charges should not be billed for drugs, 
injection, supplies, room charges, etc. See Section 272.160 in the hospital manual.  
• 
340B providers should supply the 340B modifiers only when current billing rules would 
mandate the appropriate J-Code.  
• 
FQHC and RHC 340B providers are not required to supply the 340B modifiers. 
However, per current Medicaid policy, all 340B Covered Entities (including FQHC and 
RHC 340B providers) must ensure that the appropriate billed charges are configured to 
actual invoice price, but not to exceed ceiling price.
