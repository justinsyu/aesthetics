SUBJECT: 
340B Medical Billing Changes 
I. 
General Information 
Arkansas Department of Human Services has made updates to the MMIS core system to 
allow the use of modifiers U7 and UA by 340B providers. 
 
• 
Effective 4/1/2023 340B providers should append modifiers U7 and/or UA to physician 
administered drugs (including Orphan Drugs) when they cannot be purchased at the 
discounted price for the DOS administered. 
o 
Modifier U7 should be appended for Orphan Drugs 
o 
Modifiers U7 and UA should be appended for all other drugs purchased outside of 
the 340B program 
• 
These new modifiers, bypassing 340B rules, will be subject to rebate.  Providers must 
keep all related drug purchasing records for any auditing. 
• 
New drugs to the market may not have price information built into the MMIS and may 
require a special handling process and verification of 340B purchase price. 
 
**340B modifiers JG or TB should not be used with these new modifiers for non-
discounted drugs.
