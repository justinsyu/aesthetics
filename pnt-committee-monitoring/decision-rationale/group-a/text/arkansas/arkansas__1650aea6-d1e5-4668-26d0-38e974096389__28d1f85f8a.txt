As alternatives to using a C-II stimulant: Atomoxetine, clonidine IR, and guanfacine IR do not require prior 
approval for treating adult ADD/ADHD. Qelbree® is non-preferred and requires documentation of medical 
necessity over atomoxetine and preferred C-II stimulants.
