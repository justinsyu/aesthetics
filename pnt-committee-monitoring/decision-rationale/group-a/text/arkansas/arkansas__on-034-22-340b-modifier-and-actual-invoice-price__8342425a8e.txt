SUBJECT: 
Upcoming Changes for 340B Medical Billing 
I. 
General Information 
Arkansas Medicaid is working on updates to the MMIS core system to mandate the use of 
340B Modifiers JG (340B Acquired Drug) or TB (Tracking 340B Acquired Drug) for all 340B 
eligible non-pharmacy claim lines, with implementation on or after 1/1/2023. 
• 
All 340B Covered Entities enrolled with Arkansas Medicaid (Medicaid Exclusion File) will 
need to ensure that billing systems will send the appropriate modifier for all Medicaid 
clients. 
• 
In addition, per current Medicaid policy, all 340B Covered Entities must ensure that the 
billed charges with the new mandated modifiers are configured to the actual invoice 
price. These billed charges must also be configured not to exceed ceiling price. 
 
Additional information including the date of the official implementation will be sent out later.
