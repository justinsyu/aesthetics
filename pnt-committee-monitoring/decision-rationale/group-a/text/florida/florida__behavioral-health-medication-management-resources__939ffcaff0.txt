The physician shall document the consent in the child’s medical record and provide the pharmacy with a signed attestation of this documentation with the prescription . The express and informed consent or court authorization for a prescription of psychotropic medication for a child in the custody of the Department of Children and Families shall be obtained pursuant to s. 39.407.
