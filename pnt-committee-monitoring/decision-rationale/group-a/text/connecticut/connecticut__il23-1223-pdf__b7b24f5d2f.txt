Purpose: 
The interleukin (IL)-23 inhibitors are monoclonal antibodies that bind 
specifically to the p19 subunit of IL-23 and inhibit the release of 
proinflammatory cytokines and chemokines. The IL-12/23 inhibitor, 
ustekinumab, is a monoclonal antibody that binds to the p40 subunit, used by 
both the IL-12 and IL-23 cytokines, and disrupts cytokine cascade thought to 
be involved in chronic inflammation. Icotrokinra is an interleukin-23 receptor 
antagonist. The medications covered in these criteria are indicated for the 
treatment of a variety of conditions, including plaque psoriasis, psoriatic

A letter of medical necessity must be reviewed for Icotyde being requested for use in 
patients with documented needle phobia who are unable to trial a preferred tumor 
necrosis factor inhibitor or preferred ustekinumab biosimilar. A trial and failure (minimum 
of 30 days) of a preferred Otezla formulation (IR) or a documented adverse 
events/adverse drug reaction or contraindication to Otezla is required. Submit request 
with all relevant information relating to medical necessity, via email, to rx.lmn@ct.gov

•

•

drug reaction to a preferred formulation 
o 
Exception: a trial of a preferred ustekinumab formulation is not required for pediatric
