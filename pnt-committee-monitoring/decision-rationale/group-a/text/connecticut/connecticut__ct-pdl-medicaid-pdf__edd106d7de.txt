CONNECTICUT MEDICAID
Preferred Drug List (PDL)
• The Connecticut Medicaid Preferred Drug List (PDL) is a 
listing of prescription products selected by the 
Pharmaceutical and Therapeutics Committee as efficacious, 
safe and cost effective choices when prescribing for HUSKY 
A, HUSKY C, HUSKY D, EMDS, Tuberculosis (TB) and 
Family Planning (FAMPL) clients. 
• Preferred  or Non-Preferred  status ONLY applies to those 
medications that fall within the drug classes listed on this 
PDL
• HIV medications are excluded from the PDL and do not 
require prior authorization

AUTHORIZATION BASED ON CLIENT CUMULATIVE MORPHINE 
MILLIGRAM EQUIVALENTS THRESHOLD***
