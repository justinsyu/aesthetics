Pharmacy claims after 4/1/17: Paid at NADAC x units plus dispensing fee.  
NDC’s without a NADAC rate are paid at the lesser of:  
WAC – Wholesale Acquisition Cost 
FMAC – (FUL) Federal Upper Limits  
 
Pharmacy claims prior to 4/1/17: Paid at EAC x units plus dispensing fee. Some 
NDC’s are paid at:  
SMAC – State Maximum Allowable Cost 
FMAC – (FUL) Federal Upper Limits  
 
Pharmacy claim Indicators BMN/DAW  
0 - No Product Selection Indicated  
1 - Substitution Not Allowed By Subscriber  
3 - Substitution Allowed - Pharmacist Selected Product Dispensed  
5 - Substitution Allowed - Brand Drug Dispensed as a Generic  
9 - Substitution Allowed By Prescriber but Plan Requests Brand - Patient's Plan 
Requested Brand Product to Be Dispensed  
 
PR - Professional claims – are priced using Max Fee rates, by specific Provider 
Procedure Customary Charge rates, or by the NDC rate when manual price is indicated. 
J-code pricing is typically reviewed on an annual basis with the HCPC rate based off a 
percentage of the Medicare allowed amount.  
 
PX - Professional Crossover claims – Pay the Medicare assigned coinsurance and/or 
deductible up to the Medicaid allowed amount. If no calculated Medicaid Allowed amount 
exists, the detail submitted charge becomes the Actual Medicaid Allowed amount for that detail.  
This has the effect of lowering the paid amount for claims by the state. However, the 
State is entitled to rebate on the entire claim. (DRA 2005)  
 
OP - Outpatient Claims – Outpatient Hospital claims are priced using the Provider RCC 
customary Charge table and are paid at a percentage of the billed amount or established 
rate.  
 
OX - Outpatient Crossovers Claims – Pay the lesser of Medicare assigned coinsurance 
and/or deductible up to the Medicaid allowed amount. If no calculated Medicaid Allowed 
amount exists, the detail submitted charge becomes the Actual Medicaid Allowed amount for that 
detail.  
The Medicaid Allowed is determined by Provider RCC customary Charge table 
(percentage of the billed amount or established rate). This has the effect of lowering the 
paid amount for claims by the state. However, the State is entitled to rebate on the 
entire claim. (DRA 2005)\n
