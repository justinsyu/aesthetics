Symdeko: 
Is the patient 6 years of age or older who are homozygous for the F508del mutation or who have at least 
one mutation in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) gene that is responsive 
to tezacaftor/ivacaftor based on in vitro data and/or clinical evidence or if the patient's genotype is 
unknown, an FDA-cleared CF mutation test should be used to detect the presence of a CFTR mutation
