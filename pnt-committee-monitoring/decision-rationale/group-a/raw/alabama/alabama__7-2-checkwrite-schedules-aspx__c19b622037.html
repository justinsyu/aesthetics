Checkwrite Schedules

The release of funds is normally the second Monday after the checkwrite (remittance advice) date. Please verify direct deposit status with your bank. As always, the release of direct deposit and checks depends on the availability of funds.  For checkwrite schedules prior to FY 2020, please contact the Agency's Fiscal Agent Liaison Office, (334) 242-5559.
