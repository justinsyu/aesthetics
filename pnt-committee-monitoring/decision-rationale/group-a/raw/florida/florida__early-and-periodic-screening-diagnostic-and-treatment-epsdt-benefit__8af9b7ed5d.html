Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnostic and Treatment (EPSDT) Benefit

As required by federal law, Florida Medicaid provides services to eligible recipients under the age of 21 years, if such services are medically necessary to correct or ameliorate a defect, a condition, or a physical or mental illness.
