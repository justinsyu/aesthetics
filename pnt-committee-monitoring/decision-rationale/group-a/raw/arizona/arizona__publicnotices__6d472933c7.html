Public Notices and Opportunities for Public Comment

This page lists public notices, announcements, and meetings related to the AHCCCS program. Topics seeking public input, including instructions for submitting public comments, are indicated under each area. AHCCCS retains written and public comments that may be redacted as needed due to privacy and confidentiality. Personally identifiable information that individuals do not want publicly disclosed should not be included in comments. Comments are public records and will be made available to the general public.

  • 2026 DRAFT PATH Full Application for Public Comment

AHCCCS/DBHH is requesting public comments regarding the 2026/2027 PATH Application. The goal of the grant is to reduce or eliminate homelessness for individuals with serious mental illness or co-occurring serious mental illness and substance use disorders who experience or are at imminent risk of becoming homeless. Please provide your comments, questions, or concerns to path@azahcccs.gov.

Policy Changes

ACOM and AMPM Policies and related materials that have been opened for review/revisions and will serve to provide Tribal Consultation Notification/Public Comment can be found at the below location. This location allows stakeholders to review and submit comments regarding proposed revisions. The Policies will be open for comment for not more than 45 days unless otherwise stipulated. Should an expedited time period be utilized, the expedited time period will not be less than two weeks. The comment deadline will be specified on each document.
