# State P&T Committee Monitoring Matrix

Research date: May 27, 2026

This file summarizes public information on state Medicaid pharmacy decision bodies. "P&T" includes explicit Pharmacy and Therapeutics committees and functional equivalents such as DUR boards, drug formulary committees, drug review committees, PDL advisory committees, and prior authorization advisory committees when those bodies appear to influence Medicaid preferred drug list, formulary, or prior authorization policy.

## Alabama

- Sources: [P&T Committee](https://www.medicaid.alabama.gov/content/4.0_Programs/4.3_Pharmacy-DME/4.3.6_PandT_Committee.aspx), [P&T meetings](https://medicaid.alabama.gov/content/4.0_Programs/4.3_Pharmacy-DME/4.3.6_PandT_Committee/4.3.6.1_PnT_Meetings.aspx), [PDL](https://medicaid.alabama.gov/content/4.0_programs/4.3_Pharmacy-DME/4.3.7_Preferred_Drug_List.aspx)
- Body: Alabama Medicaid Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee.
- Process: Reviews drug classes for mandatory Medicaid PDL recommendations; considers clinical efficacy, safety, and cost effectiveness.
- Timing: 2026 meetings posted for February 4, May 6, August 19, and November 4; PDL updated quarterly.
- Participants: Committee members are posted; public meetings include Medicaid staff, clinical reviewers, manufacturers, and observers.
- Public materials: Members, policies/procedures, agendas, timelines, clinical packets, approved drug lists, minutes, and final PDL.
- Gaps: Final PDL documents do not always provide product-by-product rationale beyond packets/minutes.

## Alaska

- Sources: [P&T Committee](https://health.alaska.gov/en/division-of-health-care-services/medicaid-pharmacy-and-therapeutics/), [PDL and meeting materials](https://health.alaska.gov/en/education/pdl-preferred-drug-list/), [SFY 2026 schedule PDF](https://health.alaska.gov/media/dohlrzsl/pntcommittee_schedule_sfy2026_meetingtopicschedules.pdf)
- Body: Alaska Medicaid Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee.
- Process: Alaska physicians and pharmacists appointed by the Health Commissioner review drug classes for safety, effectiveness, and lower-cost preferred status.
- Timing: SFY 2026 meetings listed for September 19, 2025; November 21, 2025; January 16, 2026; April 17, 2026. Agendas noticed 30 days before meetings.
- Participants: Current member names and manufacturer clinical-submission deadlines are posted.
- Public materials: PDL, drug-class review schedules, notices, agendas, some minutes, and archives.
- Gaps: Recent agendas/notices are stronger than recent minutes availability.

## Arizona

- Sources: [AHCCCS Pharmacy / P&T page](https://azahcccs.gov/Members/Pharmacy/)
- Body: AHCCCS Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee.
- Process: Advisory to AHCCCS Administration; evaluates safety, efficacy, effectiveness, and clinical appropriateness; recommends statewide drug list and PA criteria.
- Timing: 2026 meetings include January 13 and May 19; recent cadence appears several times yearly.
- Participants: Roster/open positions posted; provider, public/tribal/member advocate, and behavioral health representation sought; conflict-of-interest forms required.
- Public materials: Schedules, agendas, presentations, written testimony, recommendations, approved minutes, contractor notices, preferred drug changes, drug lists, and PA criteria.
- Gaps: AHCCCS Administration has final authority; rebate/cost deliberations are not fully public.

## Arkansas

- Body: Arkansas Medicaid DUR Board / Drug Review Committee framework; no separate classic P&T committee found.
- Process: Clinical Evidence Reports inform review; Drug Review Committee reviews safety/efficacy; Drug Cost Committee reviews rebate/net cost; recommendations go to Arkansas Medicaid.
- Timing: 2026 DUR calendar includes July 15 and October 21; January and April 2026 agendas/minutes also posted. DRC agendas are posted 30 days prior.
- Participants: DUR Board voting/non-voting members are posted; DRC described as physicians, pharmacists, and a chair appointed by the Medicaid Pharmacy Administrator.
- Public materials: PDL, PA criteria, DUR/DRC bylaws, conflict-of-interest disclosure, agendas, minutes, and provider notices.
- Gaps: Cost/rebate deliberations are closed because they use proprietary data.

## California

- Process: Provides written recommendations to DHCS Director on Contract Drugs List additions/deletions using safety, efficacy, essential need, misuse potential, and cost.

- Public materials: FAQ, disclosure policy, beneficiary application, drug review procedure, and Contract Drugs List.
- Gaps: No public current roster, public agendas/minutes, or public-comment process found.

## Colorado

- Sources: [P&T Committee](https://hcpf.colorado.gov/pharmacy-and-therapeutics-committee), [Pharmacy resources / PDL](https://hcpf.colorado.gov/pharmacy-resources)
- Body: Colorado Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee.
- Process: Advisory board performs clinical drug-class reviews and recommends PDL management; considers safety, efficacy, and public testimony.
- Timing: Quarterly in January, April, July, and October; virtual meetings are recorded.
- Participants: Four physicians, four pharmacists, and one client representative appointed by HCPF Executive Director.
- Public materials: Meeting schedules, agendas/minutes, class-review announcements, PDL preferred-product announcements, policy/procedure manual, and PDL.
- Gaps: Rebate/cost detail is not fully public.

## Connecticut

- Sources: [CT Medical Assistance Program pharmacy page](https://www.ctdssmap.com/CTPortal/?tabname=Pharmacy+Information), [CT P&T bylaws PDF](https://www.ctdssmap.com/CTPortal/Portals/0/StaticContent/Publications/CT_PT_COMMITTEE_BYLAWS_v2.pdf), [CGS 17b-274d](https://www.cga.ct.gov/2021/pub/chap_319v.htm)
- Body: Connecticut Pharmaceutical and Therapeutics Committee within DSS.
- Process: DSS may adopt/expand the Medicaid PDL in consultation with the committee; committee reviews criteria, standards, PA recommendations, stakeholder evidence, and public concerns.
- Timing: Statute/bylaws require at least quarterly meetings; current 2026 schedule not clearly located.
- Participants: Sixteen Governor-appointed members including physicians, pharmacists, visiting nurses, DMHAS clinician, manufacturer representative, and consumer representative.
- Public materials: PDL, agendas, minutes, recommendations, bylaws, and statute.
- Gaps: Current roster and schedule were not easy to locate.

- Gaps: Detailed deliberation/rationale is limited.

## Kansas

- Gaps: Full cost/rebate analysis is not public.
