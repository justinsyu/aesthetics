Access decision chain

The vote is often not the access event

Buried governance language shows a recurring chain: advisory review, agency or executive approval, notice/comment, then PDL or PA implementation.

1 review

Committee advice

Clinical review or public panel action creates a recommendation, not always final policy.

2 authority

Agency approval

Mississippi sends recommendations to the Medicaid Executive Director for final approval 1; North Carolina routes Review Panel recommendations to the DHHS Secretary 23; Wisconsin sends PAC recommendations to the DHS Secretary 24.

3 notice

Comment and go-live

Mississippi ties PDL changes to a 30-day public notice before go-live 1; North Carolina records a 30-day comment cycle 23.

4 operation

Claims and PA artifacts

Provider notices, PDL files, PA forms, and criteria pages are the artifacts where access impact becomes confirmable.

Monitoring implication: Tag final-approval owner, comment deadline, effective date, and downstream claims artifact separately from the meeting date.

Manufacturer action windows

Influence windows close before the public meeting starts

The buried rules are tactical: who can speak, what can be submitted, how long testimony lasts, and whether materials must arrive before the agenda window.

14 days before

Alaska Manufacturer clinical submissions for the Jan. 16, 2026 P&T meeting were due Jan. 2, while general public materials were requested Jan. 14 14.

1 week before

Washington Written testimony is limited to two pages at least one week before the meeting; stakeholders receive three minutes 10.

Friday before

Ohio Presentation requests are due by 5:00 pm the Friday before; presentations are three minutes and clinical-only, with written materials two business days before 15.

24 hours before

Oklahoma Speaker forms must arrive after agenda posting and no later than 24 hours before; one speaker per manufacturer and no Zoom comment 5.

Monitoring implication: Capture submission windows as structured fields; missing one can matter more than missing the meeting date.

Visible net-cost logic is uneven but observable

Several states document how economics enter access decisions; others only show the resulting steps or criteria, which should be tracked as an evidence gap.

Ajovy/Emgality preferred status was tied to supplemental rebates; losing participation reverts criteria from two to three prior migraine therapy failures 4.

Metabolic access is criteria dense and renewal sensitive

Buried examples show budget triggers, product sequencing, fibrosis documentation, duplication blocks, and early loss thresholds.

Budget pressure sets gates

Increased utilization raised concern about budget restrictions; criteria include lifestyle program participation, no obesity-drug combination, Saxenda preferred-agent failures, and 3%/5% renewal loss thresholds 3.

NV p. 9

Rezdiffra step includes specialist exception

Nevada approved Rezdiffra criteria with a specialist-justification option when Wegovy is expected ineffective for the recipient's treatment 6.

Wyoming requires recent biopsy or noninvasive testing, F2/F3 fibrosis, diet/exercise attestation, no GLP-1 duplication, and renewal showing no F4 progression 8.

Adjacent signals: North Dakota sequences Rezdiffra through semaglutide, and adds pioglitazone for type 2 diabetes 19; Michigan embeds concurrency blocks and six-month renewal gates for anti-obesity GLP-1s 26.

Rare disease and specialty

Specialty launches require evidence maps beyond label scope

Rare and specialty findings repeatedly translate FDA-label access into chart-note, genetic, biopsy, lab, and legacy-step evidence requirements.

Genetic confirmation

Vykat XR: methylation-analysis records plus hyperphagia symptoms and renewal response 2.

Legacy step debate

Kansas approved Niemann-Pick C PA for Aqneursa/Miplyffa while keeping miglustat step therapy open for July 2026 review after manufacturer comment 12.

Biopsy and labs

North Dakota PBC criteria require liver-biopsy confirmation, ursodiol failure, alcohol testing when relevant, and ALP/bilirubin response for renewal 21.

CPAP failure required

Zepbound OSA coverage in North Dakota excludes diabetes and requires specialist involvement, PSG-defined disease, weight-management failure, and CPAP failure 20.

Monitoring implication: Pre-build the documentation checklist field teams will need to support PA submissions.
08 / 17
Immunology and biosimilar steering

Biosimilar economics shape immunology access

The documents show when preferred-agent ladders, off-label review, stability exceptions, and biosimilar economics alter access relative to product status.

West Virginia Cytokine/CAM

  • Non-preferred products require 90-day trials of all preferred agents indicated for the diagnosis unless an exception applies.
  • Stable patients may be grandfathered only for labeled use and only when a more cost-effective biosimilar is not available.
  • Off-label requests go to medical-director review 9.

Wyoming oral JAKs

  • Alopecia areata and vitiligo were treated as medical conditions.
  • Oral JAK approval requires high-potency topical steroid failure, SALT >50%, and dermatology consultation.
  • The class was referred for cost analysis and PDL placement 7.
The recurring pattern is not blanket denial. It is conditional access based on severity proof, preferred-product exhaustion, and lower-cost alternatives.

Operating fields

Convert buried-document monitoring into operating fields

Convert meeting, criteria, economics, and implementation details into defined fields for commercial, medical, policy, and account-team workflows.

01

Separate dates from decisions

Track meeting date, recommendation, final authority, comment cycle, notice, and effective date as distinct status fields.

02

Encode criteria as evidence asks

Convert each PA barrier into a field checklist: diagnosis proof, specialist, prior therapies, thresholds, renewal proof, and exclusions.

03

Capture economic exposure

Flag net-cost mentions, rebate pools, cost analyses, and step edits that change with rebate participation.

04

Calendar submission windows

Store manufacturer deadlines, presentation limits, page caps, comment rules, and contact restrictions next to each review.

05

Build TA watchlists

Use different schemas for GLP-1/MASH, immunology, rare disease, CNS/migraine/OUD, and specialty classes.

06

Record evidence gaps

If economics or implementation details are absent, log that absence instead of inferring hidden criteria.
